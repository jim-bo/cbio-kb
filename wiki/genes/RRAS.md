# RRAS

## Overview

RRAS is a RAS-family GTPase. In a pan-cancer hotspot analysis it is reported as an extremely rare but canonical driver in a specific tumor context.

## Alterations observed in the corpus

- Extremely rare (about 0.05% overall, together with CUL3 and ARAF) but canonical driver in LUAD (0.2%), with depletion of other drivers in that context. [PMID:41895280](../papers/41895280.md)

## Cancer types (linked)

- **[LUAD](../cancer_types/LUAD.md)** — canonical driver at 0.2% frequency, with depletion of other drivers in RRAS-mutant tumors. [PMID:41895280](../papers/41895280.md)

## Co-occurrence and mutual exclusivity

- Grouped with CUL3 (canonical in PRAD, 0.6%) and ARAF (canonical in SKCM 0.5% and IHCH 0.8%) as extremely rare but tissue-specific canonical drivers. [PMID:41895280](../papers/41895280.md)

## Therapeutic relevance

- No therapeutic data reported in the current corpus.

## Open questions

- The mechanistic basis for driver depletion specifically accompanying rare RRAS mutations in LUAD is unresolved. [PMID:41895280](../papers/41895280.md)
