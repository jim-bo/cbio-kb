# Arriba

## Overview

Arriba is a fast, sensitive gene-fusion caller for RNA-seq data built on STAR chimeric alignments. It detects fusion transcripts and reports supporting split-read/discordant-mate evidence, and is commonly used alongside DNA-based structural variant callers to confirm candidate fusions found in tumor whole-transcriptome sequencing.

## Used by

- Arriba called gene fusions from whole-transcriptome sequencing of 91 SDHB-related pheochromocytoma/paraganglioma tumors, complementing WGS-based structural variant calling with GRIDSS and LINX in this A5-consortium multi-omic cohort. [PMID:40097403](../papers/40097403.md)

## Notes

- Used as one component of a multi-omic (WGS + RNA-seq + methylation + small-RNA) pipeline for a hereditary PCPG cohort with germline SDHB variants.
