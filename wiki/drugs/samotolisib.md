# samotolisib

## Overview

Samotolisib (LY3023414) is an orally bioavailable dual PI3K/mTOR inhibitor investigated in PI3K/AKT/mTOR-pathway-driven solid tumors, including sarcomas.

## Evidence in the corpus

- Ex vivo dose-response screens of PDX-derived MYOD1L122R-mutant spindle cell/sclerosing rhabdomyosarcoma (SCSRMS) cells showed submicromolar activity for samotolisib (LY3023414), while cells were resistant to standard relapse chemotherapy (SN-38 IC50 > 29 uM; temozolomide IC50 > 100 uM). [PMID:41758938](../papers/41758938.md)
- Combination ex vivo screens pairing samotolisib with irinotecan and/or temozolomide showed additive/synergistic Bliss scores, most pronounced in the MSKRMS-93202.T3 PDX model. [PMID:41758938](../papers/41758938.md)
- In vivo PDX treatment (MSKRMS-93202.T3, n=8/arm) showed all arms significantly improved tumor volume control versus vehicle (P=0.0003); the triple combination of samotolisib plus irinotecan plus temozolomide produced objective tumor regression, outperforming both samotolisib monotherapy (P=0.0005) and chemotherapy alone (P=0.005), with a modest absolute median survival benefit over chemotherapy alone (50 vs 46 days). [PMID:41758938](../papers/41758938.md)
