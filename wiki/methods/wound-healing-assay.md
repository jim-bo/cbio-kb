# Wound-healing (scratch) assay

## Overview

The scratch wound-healing assay is an in vitro functional readout of two-dimensional cell migration: a confluent monolayer is mechanically wounded and closure of the gap is tracked over time.

## Used by

- Used as a migration readout in MCF7 breast cancer cells after CRISPR-Cas9 knockout or siRNA knockdown of AXIN2/CDH1, alongside qRT-PCR, low-attachment viability assays, E-cadherin immunofluorescence in 3D Matrigel culture, and flow cytometry [PMID:38347189](../papers/38347189.md)

## Notes

- One of several functional readouts (with qRT-PCR, 3D culture, flow cytometry) used to characterize AXIN2/E-cadherin-dependent phenotypes.
