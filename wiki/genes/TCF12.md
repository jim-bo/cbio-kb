# TCF12

## Overview

TCF12 encodes a basic helix-loop-helix transcription factor (HEB) that partners with E-box-binding proteins in development and has been implicated as a tumor suppressor in several cancers. In the cbio-kb corpus it appears as a low-frequency high-confidence significantly mutated gene (HC-SMG) in colorectal cancer.

## Alterations observed in the corpus

- TCF12 was identified as a high-confidence significantly mutated gene (HC-SMG) in approximately 2% of tumors in a colorectal cancer cohort [PMID:35487942](../papers/35487942.md).

## Cancer types (linked)

- [COAD](../cancer_types/COAD.md) — TCF12 mutated in ~2% of a 1,015-patient Chinese colorectal cancer exome cohort, among the lowest-frequency HC-SMGs reported [PMID:35487942](../papers/35487942.md).
