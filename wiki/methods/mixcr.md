# MiXCR

## Overview

MiXCR is a computational tool for extracting and assembling T-cell and B-cell receptor (TCR/BCR) repertoires from raw targeted or bulk RNA/DNA sequencing data, enabling quantification of clonality and repertoire diversity.

## Used by

- Used to profile T-cell receptor repertoires as part of immune-monitoring in a trial evaluating cyclophosphamide priming prior to pembrolizumab in metastatic triple-negative breast cancer [PMID:35121644](../papers/35121644.md).

## Notes

- Corpus-grown slug; not in the cBioPortal gene-panel or molecular-profile ontology.
- Used alongside dedicated TCR-seq/BCR-seq assays, mass cytometry, and multiparameter flow cytometry for a composite immune-profiling readout in this cohort.
