# WT1

## Overview

WT1 (Wilms tumor 1) encodes a transcription factor with both tumor-suppressive and oncogenic roles depending on context. Originally identified as a Wilms tumor suppressor gene, WT1 is recurrently mutated in acute myeloid leukemia ([AML](../cancer_types/AML.md)) where it functions to disrupt normal hematopoietic differentiation. It is one of 23 significantly mutated genes identified in the TCGA [AML](../cancer_types/AML.md) genome project.

## Alterations observed in the corpus

- Identified as an additional recurrent driver in AML (200 cases; TCGA whole-genome/exome sequencing); grouped with [PHF6](../genes/PHF6.md) as genes outside the major functional categories but with recurrent mutation and allelic expression bias observed for [PHF6](../genes/PHF6.md) [PMID:23634996](../papers/23634996.md).
- Mentioned in the context of AML genomic subgrouping in a cohort of 1,540 patients; contributes to the broader landscape of transcription-factor mutations alongside [RUNX1](../genes/RUNX1.md) and [CEBPA](../genes/CEBPA.md) [PMID:27276561](../papers/27276561.md)
- [EWSR1](../genes/EWSR1.md)–WT1 fusion detected by RNA-seq and used diagnostically for desmoplastic small round cell tumor ([DSRCT](../cancer_types/DSRCT.md)) in a pediatric patient [PMID:28007021](../papers/28007021.md)
- WT1 hemizygous deletions in 61% of Wilms tumor PDX models, frequently with 11p13 LOH, in the PPTC 261-model pediatric cancer genomic study [PMID:31693904](../papers/31693904.md).
- WT1 is listed among myeloid-malignancy driver mutations at transformation in pediatric neuroblastoma patients developing therapy-related myeloid neoplasms (t-MDS/AML); observed alongside [CUX1](../genes/CUX1.md) and [STAG2](../genes/STAG2.md) in a longitudinal BM surveillance cohort (n=17 transformation) [PMID:35078859](../papers/35078859.md)
- In myelodysplastic syndrome, WT1 mutations correlated strongly with marrow blast percentage, alongside STAG2 and [NRAS](../genes/NRAS.md) mutations [PMID:24030381](../papers/24030381.md).
- WT1 mutations occurred in 115 of 887 pediatric/AYA AML cases as a cooperating pathway mutation, co-occurring frequently with [NUP98](../genes/NUP98.md) rearrangements and FLT3-ITD [PMID:38212634](../papers/38212634.md).
- WT1 is the fusion partner in EWSR1::WT1 desmoplastic small round cell tumor (n=104); chromoplexy occurred in 19% of DSRCT cases and WT1 accounted for 24% of chromoplectic events, with closed-loop triangles linking EWSR1, WT1 and a third gene [PMID:38335254](../papers/38335254.md).
- In metastatic pheochromocytoma/paraganglioma, WT1 binding motifs were enriched in the open chromatin of ATRX- or TERT-altered tumor cells [PMID:40097403](../papers/40097403.md).

## Cancer types (linked)

- **AML:** Recurrent mutation identified in 200 clinically annotated adult de novo AML cases; one of 23 significantly mutated genes by TCGA analysis [PMID:23634996](../papers/23634996.md).

## Co-occurrence and mutual exclusivity

- No specific co-occurrence or mutual-exclusivity patterns reported for WT1 in this cohort; co-mutation structure was primarily analyzed for [NPM1](../genes/NPM1.md), [FLT3](../genes/FLT3.md), [DNMT3A](../genes/DNMT3A.md), and [TP53](../genes/TP53.md) [PMID:23634996](../papers/23634996.md).

## Therapeutic relevance

- WT1 is not directly targeted by approved agents; WT1 peptide vaccines have been explored in AML but are not addressed in this corpus [PMID:23634996](../papers/23634996.md).

## Open questions

- The precise mechanism by which WT1 mutations contribute to AML pathogenesis (vs. WT1 overexpression in other AML subtypes) requires further functional characterization [PMID:23634996](../papers/23634996.md).
