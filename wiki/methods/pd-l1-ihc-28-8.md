# PD-L1 IHC 28-8 pharmDx

## Overview

A tumor-cell PD-L1 companion-diagnostic [immunohistochemistry](../methods/immunohistochemistry.md) assay using the BMS/Dako automated platform and the 28-8 antibody clone, scored by percent tumor-membrane staining.

## Used by

- Scored tumor-cell PD-L1 (≥5% vs. <5% tumor-membrane staining, 56 evaluable baseline biopsies) as a biomarker in a [nivolumab](../drugs/nivolumab.md) metastatic renal cell carcinoma trial [PMID:27169994](../papers/27169994.md).

## Notes

- Not a cBioPortal gene panel or registered molecular-profile type; corpus-grown slug. Distinct from the 22C3 ([pembrolizumab](../drugs/pembrolizumab.md)) and SP142 ([atezolizumab](../drugs/atezolizumab.md)) PD-L1 companion-diagnostic assays.
