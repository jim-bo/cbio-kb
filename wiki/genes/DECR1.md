# DECR1

## Overview

DECR1 (2,4-dienoyl-CoA reductase 1) is a fatty-acid beta-oxidation enzyme gene located at 8q21.3. In the corpus it appears as part of an amplified locus whose transcript/protein levels track chemotherapy resistance in triple-negative breast cancer, rather than as a recurrently mutated driver gene.

## Alterations observed in the corpus

- DECR1 lies in the amplified 8q21.3 locus (with RMDN1, CPNE3, OTUD6B) showing higher mRNA and protein in non-pCR triple-negative breast tumors after neoadjuvant chemotherapy [PMID:36001024](../papers/36001024.md).

## Cancer types (linked)

- [BRCA](../cancer_types/BRCA.md) (triple-negative breast cancer): 8q21.3 amplicon associated with residual disease (non-pCR) after neoadjuvant chemotherapy [PMID:36001024](../papers/36001024.md).

## Co-occurrence and mutual exclusivity

- Co-amplified with RMDN1, CPNE3 and OTUD6B at 8q21.3 in non-pCR triple-negative breast tumors [PMID:36001024](../papers/36001024.md).
