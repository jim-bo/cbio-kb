# ETS1

## Overview

ETS1 is an ETS-family transcription factor gene occasionally found as a non-canonical fusion partner of EWSR1 in round cell sarcomas.

## Alterations observed in the corpus

- ETS1 is among the cancer-related genes identified as non-canonical EWSR1 fusion partners in chromoplectic structural variants in Ewing sarcoma/DSRCT, alongside ATR, EGFL7, EP300, EZR, FOSB, NAB2 and ZNF331; none of these rearrangements were expressed by RNA sequencing. [PMID:38335254](../papers/38335254.md)

## Cancer types (linked)

- [ES](../cancer_types/ES.md) / [DSRCT](../cancer_types/DSRCT.md): observed as a rare non-canonical EWSR1 partner in chromoplectic events in EWSR1-rearranged round cell sarcomas. [PMID:38335254](../papers/38335254.md)

## Co-occurrence and mutual exclusivity

- Not yet reported in the corpus.

## Therapeutic relevance

- Not yet reported in the corpus.

## Open questions

- Whether ETS1::EWSR1 rearrangements are drivers or passenger events is unresolved; none of the identified ETS1 rearrangements showed RNA expression in this cohort. [PMID:38335254](../papers/38335254.md)
