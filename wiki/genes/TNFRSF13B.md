# TNFRSF13B

## Overview

TNFRSF13B (TACI) encodes a TNF-receptor-superfamily member expressed on B cells that regulates B-cell survival and immunoglobulin class switching. In the cbio-kb corpus it appears as a differentially expressed gene distinguishing Burkitt lymphoma genetic subgroups.

## Alterations observed in the corpus

- TNFRSF13B was bimodally overexpressed in IC-BL versus DGG-BL Burkitt lymphoma subgroups, echoing an ABC cell-of-origin pattern [PMID:36201743](../papers/36201743.md).

## Cancer types (linked)

- [BL](../cancer_types/BL.md) — TNFRSF13B expression distinguishes the IC-BL (ID3/CCND3-mutant) and DGG-BL (DDX3X/GNA13/GNAI2-mutant) genetic subgroups of Burkitt lymphoma [PMID:36201743](../papers/36201743.md).

## Co-occurrence and mutual exclusivity

- Co-expressed with [IRF4](../genes/IRF4.md) in an ABC-like expression pattern that bimodally separates IC-BL from DGG-BL [PMID:36201743](../papers/36201743.md).

## Therapeutic relevance

- Not yet characterized in the corpus.

## Open questions

- Whether TNFRSF13B overexpression has functional consequences for IC-BL biology beyond its use as a subgroup marker is unresolved in the source text [PMID:36201743](../papers/36201743.md).
