# SIN3A

## Overview

SIN3A is a transcriptional corepressor scaffold protein. In a whole-genome-sequencing study of adult and pediatric Burkitt lymphoma ([BL](../cancer_types/BL.md)), SIN3A was identified as a significantly mutated gene (SMG) whose role in BL pathogenesis this larger cohort further supports.

## Alterations observed in the corpus

- SIN3A is one of several BL significantly mutated genes (SMGs) — alongside [USP7](../genes/USP7.md), [H1-4](../genes/H1-4.md) (HIST1H1E), [CHD8](../genes/CHD8.md), and [RFX7](../genes/RFX7.md) — whose role in Burkitt lymphoma pathobiology this study's larger cohort further supports [PMID:36201743](../papers/36201743.md).
