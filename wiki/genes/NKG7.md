# NKG7

## Overview

NKG7 encodes an NK/cytotoxic-lymphocyte granule membrane protein. In the cbio-kb corpus it appears not as a somatic cancer driver but as a pharmacodynamic transcript marking NK-cell and cytotoxic-lymphocyte activity in the tumor microenvironment during immune checkpoint blockade.

## Alterations observed in the corpus

- No somatic mutations or copy-number alterations reported in the corpus; observed only as an expression-level immune-effector transcript.

## Cancer types (linked)

- **[CCRCC](../cancer_types/CCRCC.md)**: in a nivolumab-treated metastatic clear cell renal cell carcinoma cohort, NKG7 transcript expression increased significantly in tumor tissue on treatment, among innate/NK-cell function genes (with KLRB1, KLRD1, KLRG1, CD69) [PMID:27169994](../papers/27169994.md).

## Co-occurrence and mutual exclusivity

- No co-occurrence or mutual exclusivity data reported in the corpus.

## Therapeutic relevance

- Rising NKG7 expression alongside other NK-cell markers under [nivolumab](../drugs/nivolumab.md) treatment led the authors to hypothesize engagement of NK cells and to suggest combination with NK-directed agents such as [lirilumab](../drugs/lirilumab.md) [PMID:27169994](../papers/27169994.md).

## Open questions

- Whether the on-treatment rise in NKG7 reflects a causal contribution of NK cells to anti-PD-1 response, or is a downstream correlate of broader lymphocyte infiltration, is not established in the corpus.
