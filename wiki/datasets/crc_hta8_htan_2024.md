# Colorectal Cancer (HTAN MSK, Nature 2025)

## Overview

A Human Tumor Atlas Network (HTAN) cohort of biospecimen trios (normal colon, primary tumor, metastasis) prospectively collected from 31 patients with microsatellite-stable, mismatch-repair-proficient colorectal cancer undergoing synchronous hemicolectomy and metastasectomy at MSK, used for single-cell characterization of primary-to-metastasis transcriptional programs. cBioPortal study record: 83 samples, hg19. [PMID:39478232](../papers/39478232.md)

## Composition

- 31 patients; metastatic sites predominantly liver (29), plus peritoneum, lung and chest wall; 9 treatment-naive, 22 received pre-operative 5-fluorouracil-based chemotherapy. [PMID:39478232](../papers/39478232.md)
- Cancer type: [COADREAD](../cancer_types/COADREAD.md). [PMID:39478232](../papers/39478232.md)
- Companion resources: patient-derived epithelial organoids (29 samples/15 patients) and multiplexed immunofluorescence (72 samples/21 patients). [PMID:39478232](../papers/39478232.md)

## Assays / panels (linked)

- [Single-cell RNA-seq](../methods/scrna-seq.md) — 83 samples from all 31 patients (10x Genomics 3' v3.1); 47,437 high-quality epithelial cell profiles. [PMID:39478232](../papers/39478232.md)
- Clinical [MSK-IMPACT](../methods/msk-impact-panel.md) targeted exon sequencing — tumor/normal tissue from 27/31 patients, OncoKB-annotated. [PMID:39478232](../papers/39478232.md)
- [Multiplexed immunofluorescence](../methods/multiplexed-immunofluorescence.md) (7-color Vectra, 12-color Lunaphore COMET). [PMID:39478232](../papers/39478232.md)
