# TAOK1

## Overview

TAOK1 (TAO Kinase 1) is a serine/threonine kinase in the STE20 family involved in microtubule dynamics and MAP kinase signaling. In the cbio-kb corpus it appears as the 5' partner of a clinically actionable kinase fusion identified in a pediatric solid-tumor patient-derived xenograft (PDX) cohort.

## Alterations observed in the corpus

- A TAOK1–[NTRK3](../genes/NTRK3.md) fusion was identified as a clinically actionable kinase fusion in a pediatric solid tumor PDX cohort [PMID:37990009](../papers/37990009.md).

## Cancer types (linked)

- The specific tumor type carrying the TAOK1–NTRK3 fusion was not stated in the source text; the cohort spanned multiple pediatric solid tumor types (Wilms tumor, hepatoblastoma, osteosarcoma, germ cell tumor, neuroblastoma, clear cell sarcoma, embryonal rhabdomyosarcoma, malignant fibrous histiocytoma, glioblastoma) [PMID:37990009](../papers/37990009.md).

## Co-occurrence and mutual exclusivity

- Not yet characterized in the corpus.

## Therapeutic relevance

- The TAOK1–NTRK3 fusion retains the NTRK3 kinase domain and was flagged as clinically actionable (consistent with NTRK-fusion-directed therapy), though no specific drug response was reported [PMID:37990009](../papers/37990009.md).

## Open questions

- Which specific pediatric tumor type(s) carried the TAOK1–NTRK3 fusion, and whether the fusion was functionally validated or clinically acted upon, is unresolved in the source text [PMID:37990009](../papers/37990009.md).
