# Metastatic Renal Clear Cell Carcinoma (Phase 1 Biomarker Trial, Clin Cancer Res. 2016) - iAtlas Harmonized

## Overview

A CRI iAtlas-harmonized whole-exome sequencing and neoantigen-landscape reprocessing (hg38) of 16 metastatic clear-cell renal cell carcinomas with matched normals, drawn from an open-label, parallel, four-group phase 1b dose/schedule trial of [nivolumab](../drugs/nivolumab.md) (NCT01358721). The trial paper itself reports only IHC, microarray gene-expression and serum-protein data on paired baseline/on-treatment biopsies; it does not describe exome sequencing, which is instead documented in the cBioPortal study metadata. [PMID:27169994](../papers/27169994.md)

## Composition

- Cancer type: [CCRCC](../cancer_types/CCRCC.md), metastatic. [PMID:27169994](../papers/27169994.md)
- Trial population: 91 treated patients (67 previously treated across 0.3/2/10 mg/kg arms, 24 treatment-naive at 10 mg/kg) enrolled Sept 2011–Sept 2012 at 14 international centers. [PMID:27169994](../papers/27169994.md)
- Paired baseline/C2D8 biopsies obtained from 60/91 patients. [PMID:27169994](../papers/27169994.md)

## Assays / panels (linked)

- [Whole-exome sequencing](../methods/whole-exome-seq.md) and neoantigen-landscape analysis — per cBioPortal study metadata; reprocessing coordinated by CRI iAtlas on hg38 (not described in the trial paper's own text). [PMID:27169994](../papers/27169994.md)
- In-paper biomarkers: multiplex [immunohistochemistry](../methods/immunohistochemistry.md) (CD3/CD8, CD3/CD4/FOXP3), tumor-cell PD-L1 by [pd-l1-ihc-28-8](../methods/pd-l1-ihc-28-8.md), and [microarray gene expression](../methods/microarray-gene-expression.md) (Affymetrix HG-U219). [PMID:27169994](../papers/27169994.md)
