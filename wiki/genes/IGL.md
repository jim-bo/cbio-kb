# IGL

## Overview

IGL (immunoglobulin lambda locus) is one of the immunoglobulin light-chain loci that can serve as a translocation partner for oncogenes such as MYC in B-cell lymphomas.

## Alterations observed in the corpus

- MYC translocation partner in 12% of MYC-translocated Burkitt lymphomas (vs. 79% for IGH and 7% for IGK). [PMID:36201743](../papers/36201743.md)
