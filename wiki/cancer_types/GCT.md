# Granular Cell Tumor (GCT)

> **Not germ cell tumor.** Papers, and some cBioPortal studies, use "GCT" for germ cell tumor. In OncoTree, `GCT` means Granular Cell Tumor. Germ cell tumor content lives on the [Germ Cell Tumor](germ-cell-tumor.md) corpus umbrella and the site-specific pages ([NSGCT](NSGCT.md), [SEM](SEM.md), [OGCT](OGCT.md)).

## Overview

OncoTree places Granular Cell Tumor (`GCT`) at level 3 under Sellar Tumor (`SELT`) in the CNS/Brain branch, i.e. the granular cell tumor of the sellar region. Granular cell tumors also arise outside the sella, most often in skin, subcutis, the tongue, and the digestive tract. OncoTree has no separate code for those sites, so cBioPortal studies file them under `GCT` too.

## Cohorts in the corpus

- [sarcoma_msk_2022](../datasets/sarcoma_msk_2022.md) — clinical genomic profiling of 7,494 soft tissue and bone sarcomas; the cBioPortal study codes 5 samples `GCT` (Granular Cell Tumor) [PMID:35705558](../papers/35705558.md).

## Recurrent alterations

- **[HRAS](../genes/HRAS.md)** — amplified in 20% of granular cell tumors in the sarcoma profiling cohort [PMID:35705558](../papers/35705558.md).

## Subtypes

- Not called out in the corpus.

## Therapeutic landscape

- No granular cell tumor–specific therapy is reported in the corpus.
