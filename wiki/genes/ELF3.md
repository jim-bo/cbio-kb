# ELF3

## Overview

ELF3 (E74-Like ETS Transcription Factor 3) is an ETS family transcription factor. ELF3 LOF mutations are recurrent in gallbladder carcinoma ([GBC](../cancer_types/GBC.md)) and represent one of the more frequent driver events in this disease.

## Alterations observed in the corpus

- ELF3 LOF mutations in 9% of [GBC](../cancer_types/GBC.md) [PMID:36228155](../papers/36228155.md)
- ELF3 identified as a novel significantly mutated gene (SMG) in 8% of muscle-invasive bladder cancers in TCGA urothelial carcinoma comprehensive genomic characterization [PMID:24476821](../papers/24476821.md)
- Mentioned in study [PMID:26804919](../papers/26804919.md)
- ELF3 is recurrently mutated in 37 [UTUC](../cancer_types/UTUC.md) cases at frequencies not significantly different from TCGA UCB [PMID:31278255](../papers/31278255.md)
- ELF3 is a high-confidence significantly mutated gene in colorectal cancer, mutated in 1% of the cohort [PMID:35487942](../papers/35487942.md).
