# DHITsig

## Overview

DHITsig (double-hit signature) is a gene-expression-based classifier that identifies diffuse large B-cell lymphomas (DLBCL) with molecular high-grade/double-hit-like biology, independent of MYC/BCL2 rearrangement status.

## Used by

- Used to score Burkitt lymphoma and DLBCL genetic subgroups from a whole-genome-sequenced cohort; 14 of the Burkitt-lymphoma-cluster cases were DHITsig-positive, and [IRF4](../genes/IRF4.md), [TNFRSF13B](../genes/TNFRSF13B.md) and [SERPINA9](../genes/SERPINA9.md) — components of the DHITsig and cell-of-origin classifiers — were the most differentially expressed genes between the IC-BL and DGG-BL subgroups [PMID:36201743](../papers/36201743.md)

## Notes

- Applied alongside the [LymphGen](../methods/lymphgen.md) genetic classifier and cell-of-origin (ABC/GCB) calls in the same cohort.

## Sources

- [PMID:36201743](../papers/36201743.md) — adult/pediatric Burkitt lymphoma WGS cohort; DHITsig scoring of genetic subgroups.
