# Olink Proximity Extension Assay Proteomics

## Overview

An antibody-based proximity extension assay (PEA) platform for multiplexed serum/plasma protein quantification. Paired oligonucleotide-labeled antibodies bind their target antigen in proximity, allowing a DNA-hybridization/qPCR (or NGS) readout that reports relative protein abundance across dozens to hundreds of analytes per panel.

## Used by

- Olink proximity-extension serum proteomics (172 analytes across two Target96 panels) profiled serial peripheral blood samples in a pancreatic cancer trial of chemotherapy plus/minus nivolumab and the CD40 agonist sotigalimab [PMID:35662283](../papers/35662283.md).

## Notes

- Multiplexes protein quantification via paired oligo-labeled antibody probes and a qPCR or NGS readout (Target96-style panels typically cover ~90-100 analytes each).
- Commonly deployed on serial serum/plasma samples to track treatment-associated changes in circulating protein biomarkers.
