**PMID:** [27939400](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27939400/) · **DOI:** 10.1016/S0140-6736(16)32455-2 · **Journal:** Lancet (2017)

## TL;DR

This paper reports cohort 1 of IMvigor210 (NCT02108652), a single-arm, multicentre phase 2 trial. 119 previously untreated, cisplatin-ineligible patients with locally advanced or metastatic urothelial carcinoma ([BLCA](../cancer_types/BLCA.md) and [UTUC](../cancer_types/UTUC.md)) received [atezolizumab](../drugs/atezolizumab.md), an anti-PD-L1 ([CD274](../genes/CD274.md)) antibody, at 1200 mg IV every 3 weeks. At a median follow-up of 17.2 months (data cutoff July 4, 2016), the independently reviewed objective response rate (RECIST v1.1) was 23% (95% CI 16–31) and the complete response rate was 9%. 19 of 27 responses were still ongoing and median response duration was not reached. Median progression-free survival was 2.7 months and median overall survival was 15.9 months. Responses occurred in every PD-L1 immune-cell (IC) subgroup, and response was not significantly enriched by PD-L1 expression. Higher tumour mutation load, measured by the [FoundationOne](../methods/foundationone.md) panel, was associated with response and with longer survival. Treatment-related adverse events occurred in 66% of patients (grade 3–4 in 16%), 8% discontinued for an adverse event, and there was one treatment-related death (sepsis). The paper is one of the two publications behind the cBioPortal study [blca_iatlas_imvigor210_2017](../datasets/blca_iatlas_imvigor210_2017.md) [PMID:27939400](../papers/27939400.md).

## Cohort & data

- **Trial design.** IMvigor210 was a multicentre, single-arm, 2-cohort phase 2 trial run at 47 academic medical centres and community oncology practices in 7 countries in North America and Europe. Cohort 1, reported here, enrolled patients who had not been treated for metastatic urothelial carcinoma (mUC). Cohort 2, published earlier, enrolled patients previously treated with platinum. Registration: NCT02108652 [PMID:27939400](../papers/27939400.md).
- **Eligibility.** Patients had inoperable locally advanced or metastatic urothelial carcinoma of the renal pelvis, ureters, bladder or urethra, measurable disease by RECIST v1.1, ECOG PS ≤2, and a tumour sample for PD-L1 testing. Prior (neo)adjuvant chemotherapy or radiation was allowed if more than 12 months had passed before recurrence. Patients had to meet at least one cisplatin-ineligibility criterion: GFR >30 and <60 mL/min (Cockcroft-Gault), grade ≥2 hearing loss or peripheral neuropathy, or ECOG PS 2 [PMID:27939400](../papers/27939400.md).
- **Treatment.** Patients received [atezolizumab](../drugs/atezolizumab.md) 1200 mg IV every 21 days until unacceptable toxicity or investigator-assessed radiographic progression. Dose interruptions were allowed but dose reductions were not [PMID:27939400](../papers/27939400.md).
- **Accrual.** Between June 9, 2014 and March 30, 2015, 167 patients were screened and 123 enrolled. Four of those did not meet eligibility and never received drug, leaving 119 patients who received ≥1 dose (the intention-to-treat, efficacy and safety population). Median treatment duration was 15 weeks (range 0–102), and 25 patients (21%) were treated for >52 weeks. Median follow-up was 17.2 months (range 0.2–23.5) [PMID:27939400](../papers/27939400.md).
- **Baseline (N=119).**
  - Demographics: median age 73 years (range 51–92); 25 (21%) were aged ≥80; 96 (81%) were male.
  - Primary site: bladder/urethra in 85 (71%) and renal pelvis/ureter (upper tract, [UTUC](../cancer_types/UTUC.md)) in 33 (28%).
  - Disease extent: 110 (92%) had metastatic disease. 31 (26%) had lymph-node-only disease, 78 (66%) had visceral metastases and 25 (21%) had liver metastases.
  - Cisplatin-ineligibility reason: renal impairment in 83 (70%), ECOG PS 2 in 24 (20%), hearing loss in 17 (14%), grade ≥2 peripheral neuropathy in 7 (6%), and both renal impairment and ECOG PS 2 in 8 (7%).
  - Risk profile: 56% had one Bajorin risk factor and 15% had both, and 87% had comorbidities.
  - Prior therapy: 22 (19%) had perioperative chemotherapy and 12 (10%) had radiotherapy.
  - [PMID:27939400](../papers/27939400.md)
- **PD-L1 assay.** PD-L1 on tumour-infiltrating immune cells was scored prospectively with the VENTANA SP142 [immunohistochemistry](../methods/immunohistochemistry.md) assay ([pd-l1-ihc-sp142](../methods/pd-l1-ihc-sp142.md)) at a central laboratory (HistoGeneX). Cut-offs were IC0 <1%, IC1 ≥1% and <5%, and IC2/3 ≥5% of immune cells. The distribution was IC2/3 32 (27%), IC1 48 (40%) and IC0 39 (33%). Patients, investigators and the sponsor were blinded to PD-L1 status [PMID:27939400](../papers/27939400.md).
- **Genomic and transcriptomic assays.** Somatic mutations and tumour mutation load came from the [FoundationOne](../methods/foundationone.md) DNA panel (Foundation Medicine), which also provided the NGS-based central confirmation of microsatellite status. Gene expression was quantified for a T-effector signature ([CD8A](../genes/CD8A.md), [GZMA](../genes/GZMA.md), [GZMB](../genes/GZMB.md), [PRF1](../genes/PRF1.md), [IFNG](../genes/IFNG.md), [TBX21](../genes/TBX21.md)) and for TCGA subtyping (luminal I papillary-like, luminal II, basal III squamous-like, basal IV). The paper's reference list includes the TCGA urothelial bladder carcinoma paper ([PMID:24476821](../papers/24476821.md)). The exploratory biomarker samples were 72 (61%) primary tumours and 47 (39%) metastases [PMID:27939400](../papers/27939400.md).
- **Efficacy assessment.** Tumours were assessed at baseline, every 9 weeks for 12 months and then every 12 weeks, with central independent review (BioClinica). Adverse events were graded with NCI CTCAE v4.0. Survival was estimated by the [Kaplan-Meier](../methods/kaplan-meier.md) method [PMID:27939400](../papers/27939400.md).
- **cBioPortal dataset.** [blca_iatlas_imvigor210_2017](../datasets/blca_iatlas_imvigor210_2017.md). According to the cBioPortal study metadata (not this paper), it is a CRI iAtlas-harmonized reprocessing of whole-exome sequencing and neoantigen data from 347 metastatic urothelial carcinomas with matched normals, drawn from both IMvigor210 cohorts (cisplatin-ineligible and platinum-pretreated), on reference genome hg38. The study is co-listed with PMID 39642637 (Rosenberg et al., ESMO Open 2024). This paper does not describe the WES data; its genomic readouts come from the FoundationOne panel.

## Key findings

- **Primary analysis missed its pre-specified hierarchical test.** At the primary analysis (cutoff September 14, 2015; median follow-up 8.5 months), the first step of hierarchical testing failed. The IC2/3 ORR was 22% (95% CI 9–40) against the historical control of 10%, so no further formal testing was done. The 10% control was a composite: 75% of patients assumed ineligible for any cytotoxic chemotherapy (expected ORR 0%) and 25% assumed eligible for carboplatin-based chemotherapy (expected ORR 36%) [PMID:27939400](../papers/27939400.md).
- **Updated ORR (17.2-month follow-up).** ORR was 23% (27/119; 95% CI 16–31), with 11 complete responses (9%) and 16 partial responses. The lower bound of the 95% CI now exceeded 10%. Investigator and independent-review responses agreed in more than 90% of cases [PMID:27939400](../papers/27939400.md).
- **ORR by PD-L1 IC status.**
  - IC2/3: 28% (9/32; 95% CI 14–47).
  - IC1/2/3: 24% (19/80; 95% CI 15–35).
  - IC1: 21% (10/48).
  - IC0: 21% (8/39).
  - Complete responses occurred in every IC subgroup: 4 in IC2/3, 4 in IC1 and 3 in IC0.
  - No statistically significant enrichment of response by PD-L1 expression was seen, which differs from earlier reports.
  - [PMID:27939400](../papers/27939400.md)
- **Durability.** Median time to first response was 2.1 months (range 1.8–10.5), and two patients responded after 6 months. Median duration of response was not reached overall (95% CI 14.1–NE; range 3.7–21.0+) or in any PD-L1 subgroup, and 19 of 27 responses (70%) were ongoing [PMID:27939400](../papers/27939400.md).
- **PFS.** Median PFS was 2.7 months (95% CI 2.1–4.2) overall, 4.1 months (2.3–11.8) in IC2/3, 2.1 months (2.1–5.4) in IC1 and 2.6 months (2.1–5.7) in IC0. The clinical benefit rate was 30% (95% CI 22–39) [PMID:27939400](../papers/27939400.md).
- **OS.** Median OS was 15.9 months (95% CI 10.4–NE) overall, 12.3 months (6.0–NE) in IC2/3 and 19.1 months (9.8–NE) in IC0/1. The 12-month OS rate was 57% (95% CI 48–66). There were 59 deaths (18 in IC2/3, 41 in IC0/1), and 52 of the 59 were due to progression [PMID:27939400](../papers/27939400.md).
- **Clinical subgroups.** Responses occurred in every subgroup evaluated.
  - Primary site: upper-tract (renal pelvis/ureter) ORR was 39% (13/33; 95% CI 23–58) and bladder/urethra ORR was 17% (14/85). Median OS was not reached in the upper-tract group.
  - Metastatic sites: lymph-node-only ORR was 32% (n=31), visceral 14% (n=78) and liver 8% (2/25). Patients with liver metastases had a median OS of 5.5 months.
  - Cisplatin-ineligibility reason: renal impairment ORR 25% (21/83; median OS 14.1 months) and ECOG PS 2 ORR 25% (6/24).
  - Age and prior therapy: age ≥80 ORR 28% (7/25; median OS 14.8 months) and prior perioperative chemotherapy ORR 36% (8/22).
  - [PMID:27939400](../papers/27939400.md)
- **Bajorin risk factors stayed prognostic.** With 0 risk factors (n=35), ORR was 34% and median OS was not reached. With 1 risk factor (n=66), ORR was 20% and median OS 13.4 months. With 2 risk factors (n=18), ORR was 11% and median OS 6.2 months [PMID:27939400](../papers/27939400.md).
- **Benefit without a RECIST response.** Patients whose best response was stable disease (n=29) had a median OS of 19.1 months [PMID:27939400](../papers/27939400.md).
- **Tumour mutation load (TML) predicts response and survival.** TML was significantly higher in responders than in non-responders (Wilcoxon rank sum p=0.0180), and this held across TCGA subtypes and PD-L1 IC subgroups. Patients in the highest TML quartile (Q4) had longer OS than those in Q1–3 (log-rank p=0.0041). The authors state these p values are descriptive only. Some patients with low TML also responded [PMID:27939400](../papers/27939400.md).
- **TCGA subtype.** Responses occurred in all four TCGA subtypes and were most frequent in luminal II, consistent with the platinum-treated IMvigor210 cohort. The sample size was too small for statistical significance [PMID:27939400](../papers/27939400.md).
- **The upper-tract advantage was not explained by the measured biomarkers.** Upper- and lower-tract patients did not differ significantly in metastatic sites, TML, T-effector gene expression, TCGA subtype or baseline tumour burden. Microsatellite instability was found in only 4 patients (2 upper-tract and 2 lower-tract), so the authors concluded it was not a primary determinant [PMID:27939400](../papers/27939400.md).
- **Safety.**
  - Overall: 114 patients (96%) had any adverse event and 79 (66%) had a treatment-related one. Grade 3–4 treatment-related events occurred in 19 (16%), most often fatigue (n=4), ALT increase (n=4) and AST increase (n=3).
  - Most common treatment-related events: fatigue 30%, diarrhoea 12% and pruritus 11%.
  - Deaths: one of four grade 5 adverse events, sepsis, was judged treatment-related.
  - Interruptions and discontinuations: 41 patients (35%) had an adverse event leading to dose interruption and 9 (8%) had one leading to withdrawal.
  - Immune-mediated events: these occurred in 14 patients (12%), grade 3–4 in 8 (7%). 36 patients received corticosteroids, and none needed non-corticosteroid immunomodulators.
  - Haematological and renal effects: no neutropenia was observed. Median GFR did not decline through ≥27 cycles (data not shown).
  - [PMID:27939400](../papers/27939400.md)
- **Post-protocol therapy.** 25 patients (9 IC0, 12 IC1, 4 IC2/3) received further therapy after progression. The most common was [gemcitabine](../drugs/gemcitabine.md)–[carboplatin](../drugs/carboplatin.md) (14/25) [PMID:27939400](../papers/27939400.md).

## Genes & alterations

- [CD274](../genes/CD274.md) (PD-L1) is the target of atezolizumab, which blocks PD-L1 binding to PD-1 and B7.1. PD-L1 protein was scored on tumour-infiltrating immune cells with the SP142 IHC assay. IC status did not significantly enrich response (IC2/3 28% vs IC0 21% ORR), and median OS was numerically shorter in IC2/3 (12.3 months) than in IC0/1 (19.1 months) [PMID:27939400](../papers/27939400.md).
- [PDCD1](../genes/PDCD1.md) (PD-1) and [CD80](../genes/CD80.md) (B7.1) are the PD-L1 receptors whose engagement atezolizumab blocks. The paper cites this as the drug's mechanism and did not measure either gene [PMID:27939400](../papers/27939400.md).
- [CD8A](../genes/CD8A.md), [GZMA](../genes/GZMA.md), [GZMB](../genes/GZMB.md), [PRF1](../genes/PRF1.md), [IFNG](../genes/IFNG.md) and [TBX21](../genes/TBX21.md) make up the T-effector gene signature. The manuscript prints IFNG as "INFG". The only result reported for the signature in the main text is that it did not differ significantly between upper- and lower-tract primaries (supplementary figure S3) [PMID:27939400](../papers/27939400.md).
- **Tumour mutation load** is a panel-level, not gene-level, measure (mutations per megabase, [FoundationOne](../methods/foundationone.md)). It was higher in responders (p=0.018) and Q4 was associated with longer OS (p=0.0041) [PMID:27939400](../papers/27939400.md).
- **Microsatellite instability** was found by NGS in only 4 of the patients analysed [PMID:27939400](../papers/27939400.md).
- The main text reports no findings for individual somatic driver mutations.

## Clinical implications

- **First-line option for cisplatin-ineligible mUC.** The authors present atezolizumab as "an attractive first-line option for cisplatin-ineligible mUC". They call it the first anti-PD-L1/PD-1 agent to show durable responses with a tolerable safety profile in this setting [PMID:27939400](../papers/27939400.md).
- **Comparison with chemotherapy (historical).** The median OS of 15.9 months is contrasted with 9.3 months for first-line [gemcitabine](../drugs/gemcitabine.md)–[carboplatin](../drugs/carboplatin.md) and 15.2–15.8 months for [cisplatin](../drugs/cisplatin.md)-based regimens in cisplatin-eligible patients. Gemcitabine–carboplatin is reported to have a 21% discontinuation rate and high haematological toxicity, compared with 8% adverse-event discontinuation and no neutropenia here. The authors stress that this is a single-arm comparison [PMID:27939400](../papers/27939400.md).
- **Suitable for renal impairment.** 70% of patients had baseline renal impairment and median GFR did not decline on treatment. The authors call this relevant for patients with reduced kidney function or a solitary kidney, which is common after upper-tract disease [PMID:27939400](../papers/27939400.md).
- **PD-L1 IC status alone is not a sufficient selector in this cohort.** Durable responses occurred in IC0 patients [PMID:27939400](../papers/27939400.md).
- **Candidate biomarkers.** TML, PD-L1 on immune cells and TCGA subtype are proposed as possibly independent predictors of response. The authors suggest multi-biomarker analyses in larger mUC studies could guide patient selection [PMID:27939400](../papers/27939400.md).
- **Endpoints for immunotherapy trials.** Responses deepened over time and patients with stable disease lived long (median OS 19.1 months). The authors argue that early response rates and PFS may not capture immunotherapy benefit, and that stopping treatment early, as is usual with platinum's 6–8 cycles, could compromise benefit [PMID:27939400](../papers/27939400.md).
- **Elderly patients.** Patients aged ≥80 (21% of the cohort) had outcomes similar to the intention-to-treat population and tolerated treatment well [PMID:27939400](../papers/27939400.md).
- **Confirmatory trials.** The authors defer to the phase 3 trials IMvigor130 (treatment-naive, NCT02807636) and IMvigor211 (platinum-treated, NCT02302807) [PMID:27939400](../papers/27939400.md).

## Limitations & open questions

- **Single arm, no concurrent control.** All comparisons with chemotherapy are historical and confounded by differences between patient populations. The single-arm design may also have attracted elderly patients who would avoid trials with a chemotherapy arm [PMID:27939400](../papers/27939400.md).
- **The primary endpoint was not formally met at the primary analysis.** The IC2/3 ORR of 22% did not reach significance against the 10% control. The later ORR, whose CI lower bound exceeds 10%, came from an updated cutoff without hypothesis testing [PMID:27939400](../papers/27939400.md).
- **Underpowered for PD-L1 subgroup differences.** The cohort was first planned as an exploratory group of 30 patients and later expanded to about 100, with a sample size based on an assumed 30% IC2/3 prevalence. The authors cite baseline differences (for example tumour burden, nodal vs visceral disease) and these statistical assumptions as possible reasons PD-L1 did not enrich response here, unlike in earlier reports [PMID:27939400](../papers/27939400.md).
- **Biomarker analyses were exploratory.** TML, T-effector signature and TCGA subtype analyses were not pre-specified and their p values are descriptive. The TCGA subtype comparisons lacked statistical power. MSI was too rare to study [PMID:27939400](../papers/27939400.md).
- **Why did upper-tract patients do so well?** Upper-tract patients had a 39% ORR and median OS not reached, yet showed no differences in TML, T-effector expression or TCGA subtype [PMID:27939400](../papers/27939400.md). This contrasts with [PMID:31278255](../papers/31278255.md), which describes sporadic [UTUC](../cancer_types/UTUC.md) as luminal-papillary, T-cell-depleted and low-TMB, and argues this profile should blunt response to checkpoint inhibitors. The two positions are unreconciled and the upper-tract subgroup here is small (n=33).
- **TML as a threshold.** The authors suggest a TML threshold may have to be exceeded to generate neoantigens that T cells can recognise. They also note that neoantigen generation relates to TML stochastically, so some low-TML patients respond. The reference list includes the NSCLC anti-PD-1 study ([PMID:25765070](../papers/25765070.md)) and the melanoma anti-CTLA-4 study ([PMID:26359337](../papers/26359337.md)) among the other-tumour-type immunotherapy studies it says it validates. In this paper TML comes from a targeted panel, whereas the linked cBioPortal study [blca_iatlas_imvigor210_2017](../datasets/blca_iatlas_imvigor210_2017.md) is described as iAtlas-reprocessed WES, so TMB values may not be directly comparable across these sources.
- **Small inconsistencies between text and table.** The 95% CIs for ORR differ slightly: IC1 is 10–35 in the text and 11–35 in Table 2, and IC0 is 9–36 in the text and 9–37 in Table 2 [PMID:27939400](../papers/27939400.md).
