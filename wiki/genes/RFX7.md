# RFX7

## Overview

RFX7 is a regulatory factor X family transcription factor. In the corpus it is nominated as a novel U-CLL driver with adverse prognostic impact.

## Alterations observed in the corpus

- Novel U-CLL driver associated with poor FFS/OS in the 1,148-patient CLL map [PMID:35927489](../papers/35927489.md).
- One of five significantly mutated genes (with SIN3A, USP7, H1-4/HIST1H1E, CHD8) in Burkitt lymphoma whose role in BL this study further supports. [PMID:36201743](../papers/36201743.md)

## Cancer types (linked)

- Chronic lymphocytic leukemia ([CLLSLL](../cancer_types/CLLSLL.md)) — novel U-CLL-enriched driver [PMID:35927489](../papers/35927489.md).

## Co-occurrence and mutual exclusivity

- Enriched in IGHV-unmutated (U-CLL) subtype [PMID:35927489](../papers/35927489.md).

## Therapeutic relevance

- No direct therapeutic link reported in the corpus.

## Open questions

- Functional validation of RFX7 as a CLL driver is deferred to future work [PMID:35927489](../papers/35927489.md).
