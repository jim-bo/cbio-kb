# C-circle Assay

## Overview

The C-circle assay detects extrachromosomal telomeric C-rich single-stranded DNA circles, a specific biomarker of the Alternative Lengthening of Telomeres (ALT) pathway, via phi29-polymerase rolling-circle amplification followed by dot-blot hybridization.

## Used by

- The C-circle assay was run on 89 of 94 SDHB-related pheochromocytoma/paraganglioma tumors as part of a telomere-maintenance assay panel, alongside WGS-derived telomere content and TERRA analysis (TelomereHunter). [PMID:40097403](../papers/40097403.md)

## Notes

- Complements TelomereHunter (WGS-based telomere content/TERRA quantification) as an orthogonal, protein-independent readout of ALT pathway activity.

## Sources

- [PMID:40097403](../papers/40097403.md) — Flynn et al. 2025, *Nat Commun*, multi-omic analysis of SDHB-deficient pheochromocytomas and paragangliomas
