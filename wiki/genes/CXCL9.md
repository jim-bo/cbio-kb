# CXCL9

## Overview

CXCL9 is an IFN-gamma-induced [CXCR3](../genes/CXCR3.md) ligand that directs CD8 T cell and NK cell recruitment into inflamed and tumor tissues.

## Alterations observed in the corpus

- Included in the 20-gene Immunologic Constant of Rejection (ICR) signature as a CXCR3/CCR5 chemokine ligand, alongside [CCL5](../genes/CCL5.md) and [CXCL10](../genes/CXCL10.md), applied to the 348-patient AC-ICAM colon cancer cohort [PMID:37202560](../papers/37202560.md).
- CXCL9 was identified as an [APC](../genes/APC.md) chemoattraction marker in spatial transcriptomic profiling (GeoMx WTA) of fallopian tube [HGSOC](../cancer_types/HGSOC.md) precursor lesions; CXCL9 expression used to define infiltrating immune-cell populations and their spatial relationship to [HLA-E](../genes/HLA-E.md)+ epithelium across the p53-signature → STIC.I → STIC.C → invasive cancer axis [PMID:39386723](../papers/39386723.md).
- CXCL9 is concordantly downregulated in the T-cell-depleted [UTUC](../cancer_types/UTUC.md) cluster, linking FGFR3-driven suppression of [IFNG](../genes/IFNG.md) response genes to immune exclusion in 37 upper-tract urothelial carcinoma cases [PMID:31278255](../papers/31278255.md)
- Higher CXCL9 expression in ipilimumab-treated melanoma responders to subsequent anti-PD1 ICB (MWW P=0.05); part of the CXCL9/CXCL10/CXCR3 chemokine axis elevated in responders in the ipilimumab-treated subgroup but not ipilimumab-naive tumors [PMID:31792460](../papers/31792460.md)
- CXCL9 increased 2.4-fold in tumor and a median +101% in serum on [nivolumab](../drugs/nivolumab.md) treatment, proposed to be induced by myeloid cells to recruit T cells. [PMID:27169994](../papers/27169994.md)

## Cancer types (linked)

- Colon adenocarcinoma — CXCL9 contributes to ICR-based prognostic clustering independent of CMS and MSI [PMID:37202560](../papers/37202560.md).
- [OV](../cancer_types/OV.md) ([HGSOC](../cancer_types/HGSOC.md)) / fallopian tube precursors — CXCL9 expression used as [APC](../genes/APC.md) chemoattraction marker to characterize immune compartments across [HGSOC](../cancer_types/HGSOC.md) progression stages [PMID:39386723](../papers/39386723.md).

## Co-occurrence and mutual exclusivity

- Co-expressed with the ICR Th1/chemokine/effector module in ICR-high colon tumors [PMID:37202560](../papers/37202560.md).

## Therapeutic relevance

- No direct CXCL9-targeted therapy is evaluated; it contributes to the prognostic ICR module [PMID:37202560](../papers/37202560.md).

## Open questions

- Independent contribution of CXCL9 vs the ICR module is not dissected [PMID:37202560](../papers/37202560.md).
