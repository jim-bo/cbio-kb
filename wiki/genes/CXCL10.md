# CXCL10

## Overview

CXCL10 is an IFN-gamma-inducible [CXCR3](../genes/CXCR3.md) ligand that recruits effector T and NK cells to tumor sites.

## Alterations observed in the corpus

- Included in the 20-gene Immunologic Constant of Rejection (ICR) signature as a CXCR3/CCR5 chemokine ligand, alongside [CCL5](../genes/CCL5.md) and [CXCL9](../genes/CXCL9.md), in the 348-patient AC-ICAM colon cancer cohort [PMID:37202560](../papers/37202560.md).
- IDH1-driven gliomas (330/351 [LGG](../cancer_types/LGG.md) samples) exhibit lower [STAT1](../genes/STAT1.md), decreasing CXCL10 expression and reducing immune infiltrate in the tumor microenvironment [PMID:29625049](../papers/29625049.md)
- CXCL10 is concordantly downregulated in the T-cell-depleted [UTUC](../cancer_types/UTUC.md) cluster, linking FGFR3-driven suppression of [IFNG](../genes/IFNG.md) response genes to immune exclusion in 37 upper-tract urothelial carcinoma cases [PMID:31278255](../papers/31278255.md)
- Higher CXCL10 expression in ipilimumab-treated melanoma responders to subsequent anti-PD1 ICB (MWW P=0.08); part of the CXCL9/CXCL10/CXCR3 immune infiltrate axis elevated in responders in the ipilimumab-treated subgroup but not in ipilimumab-naive tumors [PMID:31792460](../papers/31792460.md)
- CXCL10 increased 2-fold in tumor and a median +37% in serum on [nivolumab](../drugs/nivolumab.md) treatment; this interferon-gamma-regulated chemokine is proposed to recruit T cells via myeloid cells. [PMID:27169994](../papers/27169994.md)

## Cancer types (linked)

- Colon adenocarcinoma — CXCL10 contributes to ICR-based stratification that is prognostic for overall survival [PMID:37202560](../papers/37202560.md).

## Co-occurrence and mutual exclusivity

- Co-expressed with the other ICR Th1/chemokine and effector module genes in ICR-high colon tumors [PMID:37202560](../papers/37202560.md).

## Therapeutic relevance

- No direct CXCL10-targeting therapy is evaluated; it contributes to the prognostic ICR module [PMID:37202560](../papers/37202560.md).

## Open questions

- Independent contribution of CXCL10 vs the ICR module is not dissected [PMID:37202560](../papers/37202560.md).
