# Pediatric Acute Myeloid Leukemia (St. Jude, Nat Genet 2024)

## Overview

A discovery cohort of 887 unique pediatric acute myeloid leukemia ([AML](../cancer_types/AML.md)) cases assembled by St. Jude Children's Research Hospital from prior published cohorts, newly sequenced St. Jude cases, and TARGET-AML (n=187 of the 887). Study record per cBioPortal metadata: `aml_stjude_2024`, 887 samples, hg19. [PMID:38212634](../papers/38212634.md)

## Composition

- 887 unique cases, ages 0–23.5 years (median 9.3); 783 (88.3%) at diagnosis and 104 (11.7%) at relapse. [PMID:38212634](../papers/38212634.md)
- Cancer type: [AML](../cancer_types/AML.md) (pediatric acute myeloid leukemia). [PMID:38212634](../papers/38212634.md)
- Companion validation/outcome cohorts (not part of this cBioPortal study record): Children's Oncology Group AAML1031 (n=1,034) and St. Jude AML08 (n=221). [PMID:38212634](../papers/38212634.md)

## Assays / panels (linked)

- [RNA-seq](../methods/rna-seq.md) — all 887 cases; used for fusion, ITD/PTD, CNV, SNV and indel calling. [PMID:38212634](../papers/38212634.md)
- [Whole-genome sequencing](../methods/whole-genome-seq.md) and [whole-exome sequencing](../methods/whole-exome-seq.md) — orthogonal validation in 665 cases (74.9%; 59.2% WGS, 44.0% WES), validating 97.3% of RNA-seq-derived calls. [PMID:38212634](../papers/38212634.md)
