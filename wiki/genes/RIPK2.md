# RIPK2

## Overview

RIPK2 (receptor-interacting serine/threonine kinase 2) is located at 8q21.3, a region recurrently amplified in triple-negative breast cancer alongside the DNA-repair/replication genes LIG1, POLD1 and XRCC1.

## Alterations observed in the corpus

- 8q21.3 gene (co-amplified with RMDN1, CPNE3, DECR1, OTUD6B) with higher protein — but not mRNA — expression in non-pathologic-complete-response (non-pCR) triple-negative breast tumors after neoadjuvant chemotherapy. [PMID:36001024](../papers/36001024.md)

## Cancer types (linked)

- No cancer-type-specific prevalence data reported in the current corpus.

## Co-occurrence and mutual exclusivity

- Co-located in the amplified 8q21.3 region with RMDN1, CPNE3, DECR1 and OTUD6B. [PMID:36001024](../papers/36001024.md)

## Therapeutic relevance

- No therapeutic data reported in the current corpus.

## Open questions

- Whether RIPK2 protein overexpression (without matching mRNA elevation) at 8q21.3 contributes mechanistically to chemoresistance, versus reflecting a passenger effect of the LIG1/POLD1/XRCC1 codeletion described on the same chromosome arm, is unresolved. [PMID:36001024](../papers/36001024.md)
