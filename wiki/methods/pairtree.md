# Pairtree

## Overview

Pairtree is a computational method for reconstructing clone trees (tumor phylogenies) from multi-sample variant allele frequency data, used to infer clonal evolution and subclonal structure from bulk sequencing.

## Used by

- Used to infer clonal evolution in a multi-omic (WGS/RNA-seq/methylation) study of SDHB-deficient pheochromocytoma and paraganglioma [PMID:40097403](../papers/40097403.md).
