# Single-nuclear RNA sequencing (snRNA-seq)

## Overview

Single-nuclear RNA sequencing (snRNA-seq) is a single-cell transcriptomics method that works on nuclei isolated from frozen tissue, enabling cell type decomposition and transcriptional characterization in samples where intact cells cannot be recovered [PMID:34433969](../papers/34433969.md).

## Used by

- [PMID:34433969](../papers/34433969.md) — droplet-based snRNA-seq was performed on 8 meningioma tumors and 2 healthy meninges (54,393 nuclei total) from the University Health Network Brain Tumor BioBank; identified cell-type-specific transcriptional programs distinguishing meningioma molecular groups (MG1–MG4) and characterized the meningioma tumor microenvironment [PMID:34433969](../papers/34433969.md).
- [PMID:34493726](../papers/34493726.md) — Smart-Seq2-based snRNA-seq (nuc-Seq) on 11 neuroblastoma tumors (4,224 nuclei; 3,212 high-quality) and 3 postnatal human adrenal glands (1,536 nuclei; 1,322 high-quality) across INRG risk groups; identified undifferentiated nC3 cluster in high-risk disease resembling a novel postnatal human cholinergic progenitor [PMID:34493726](../papers/34493726.md).
- snRNA-seq on 10X Chromium performed on 18 of 99 [GBM](../cancer_types/GBM.md) tumors (7 im1, 5 im2, 1 im3, 5 im4) to validate xCell-derived immune subtypes; identified EMT-related genes in tumor cells of mesenchymal-like tumors and confirmed im4 as uniformly immunologically cold (1.3% T cells, 6% TAM) [PMID:33577785](../papers/33577785.md)
- Profiled 10 of 94 SDHB-mutant pheochromocytoma/paraganglioma tumors by 10x snRNA-seq alongside bulk WGS, RNA-seq and methylation [PMID:40097403](../papers/40097403.md).
- Profiled 177,051 malignant nuclei from 6 [MYOD1](../genes/MYOD1.md) L122R-mutant [SCSRMS](../cancer_types/SCSRMS.md) tumors (10x Genomics Chromium) plus PDX models to define tumor cell-state architecture [PMID:41758938](../papers/41758938.md).
