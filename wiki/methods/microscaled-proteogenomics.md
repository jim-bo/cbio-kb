# Microscaled Proteogenomics

## Overview

Microscaled proteogenomics refers to low-input proteomic (and phosphoproteomic) profiling workflows, typically TMT-based mass spectrometry, adapted to work from limited tissue amounts such as core-needle biopsies, and integrated with matched genomic and transcriptomic data from the same specimen.

## Used by

- Used to generate low-input proteomic and phosphoproteomic profiles integrated with exome and RNA sequencing to identify markers of chemotherapy resistance and response in triple-negative breast cancer [PMID:36001024](../papers/36001024.md).

## Notes

- Corpus-grown slug; not in the cBioPortal gene-panel or molecular-profile ontology.
- Enables proteogenomic characterization from small clinical biopsy specimens where standard bulk proteomic input requirements are not met.
