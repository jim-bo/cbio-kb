# ESM1

## Overview

ESM1 (endocan) is an endothelial secreted gene used as a component of angiogenesis-related gene expression signatures in renal cell carcinoma clinical trials.

## Alterations observed in the corpus

- Expression-only marker in the corpus so far (no somatic mutation/CNA/fusion reports); included here as a transcriptomic biomarker gene.

## Cancer types (linked)

- [CCRCC](../cancer_types/CCRCC.md) / [RCC](../cancer_types/RCC.md): ESM1 is one of six genes (with [VEGFA](../genes/VEGFA.md), [KDR](../genes/KDR.md), ESM1, [PECAM1](../genes/PECAM1.md), [ANGPTL4](../genes/ANGPTL4.md), [CD34](../genes/CD34.md)) comprising the "Angio signature" used in the IMmotion150 randomized phase 2 trial of [atezolizumab](../drugs/atezolizumab.md) +/- [bevacizumab](../drugs/bevacizumab.md) versus [sunitinib](../drugs/sunitinib.md). AngioHigh tumors predicted sunitinib benefit and tracked CD31 IHC vascular density (CD31 is encoded by PECAM1). [PMID:29867230](../papers/29867230.md)

## Co-occurrence and mutual exclusivity

- Not yet reported in the corpus.

## Therapeutic relevance

- Angio-signature status (including ESM1 expression) was used to stratify predicted benefit from the VEGF-pathway inhibitors sunitinib and bevacizumab in renal cell carcinoma. [PMID:29867230](../papers/29867230.md)
