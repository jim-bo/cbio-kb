# Pleomorphic Carcinoma of the Lung (LUPC)

## Overview

Pleomorphic carcinoma of the lung (LUPC) is a rare, aggressive non-small cell lung cancer ([NSCLC](../cancer_types/NSCLC.md)) histology composed of a mix of adenocarcinoma, squamous cell carcinoma or large cell carcinoma with spindle-cell and/or giant-cell (sarcomatoid) elements. It sits at OncoTree level 3 under [NSCLC](../cancer_types/NSCLC.md), alongside [LUAD](../cancer_types/LUAD.md), [LUSC](../cancer_types/LUSC.md) and [LUNE](../cancer_types/LUNE.md), and is often grouped with sarcomatoid carcinoma ([SARCL](../cancer_types/SARCL.md)) in genomic surveys.
