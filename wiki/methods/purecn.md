# PureCN

## Overview

PureCN is a copy-number and tumor-purity/ploidy estimation tool designed for targeted and whole-exome sequencing data.

## Used by

- Used alongside CNVkit, Sequenza and GISTIC2 for copy-number calling in a pediatric solid-tumor patient-derived xenograft genomic study [PMID:37990009](../papers/37990009.md).
