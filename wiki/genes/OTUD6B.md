# OTUD6B

## Overview

OTUD6B is a deubiquitinase-family gene located in the 8q21.3 amplicon region of
the genome, observed in the corpus as one of several co-amplified genes whose
expression tracks chemotherapy resistance in triple-negative breast cancer.

## Alterations observed in the corpus

- OTUD6B, located in the amplified 8q21.3 region (with RMDN1, CPNE3, DECR1), had
  higher mRNA and protein levels in TNBC tumors with non-pathologic complete
  response to neoadjuvant chemotherapy [PMID:36001024](../papers/36001024.md).

## Cancer types (linked)

- **BRCA** (TNBC): 8q21.3 co-amplification with RMDN1, CPNE3 and DECR1 is
  associated with non-pCR after neoadjuvant chemotherapy
  [PMID:36001024](../papers/36001024.md).

## Co-occurrence and mutual exclusivity

- Co-amplified with RMDN1, CPNE3 and DECR1 at 8q21.3 in TNBC tumors with
  non-pathologic complete response [PMID:36001024](../papers/36001024.md).
