# CDC7

## Overview

CDC7 encodes a serine/threonine kinase that, together with its regulatory subunit DBF4, controls origin firing during DNA replication initiation. It is studied as a druggable node in cell-cycle/proliferation kinase signaling and as a marker of replication activity in tumors.

## Alterations observed in the corpus

- CDC7 kinase target phosphosites were higher in pathologic complete response (pCR) triple-negative breast tumors in a neoadjuvant chemotherapy proteogenomics cohort, alongside CDK1, CDK2 and CDK7 target phosphosites [PMID:36001024](../papers/36001024.md).

## Cancer types (linked)

- [BRCA](../cancer_types/BRCA.md) (triple-negative breast cancer) — CDC7 substrate phosphorylation was elevated in tumors achieving pCR after neoadjuvant chemotherapy [PMID:36001024](../papers/36001024.md).

## Co-occurrence and mutual exclusivity

- CDC7 activity tracked with CDK1, CDK2 and CDK7 target phosphosites as part of a proliferation/cell-cycle kinase module associated with pCR in triple-negative breast cancer [PMID:36001024](../papers/36001024.md).

## Therapeutic relevance

- No CDC7-targeted therapy is directly evaluated in the corpus; elevated CDC7 kinase activity is reported as a correlate of proliferative, chemo-responsive triple-negative breast tumors [PMID:36001024](../papers/36001024.md).

## Open questions

- Whether elevated CDC7 activity is causally linked to pCR or is simply a marker of tumor proliferation is not established [PMID:36001024](../papers/36001024.md).
