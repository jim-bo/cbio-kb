# TPH1

## Overview

TPH1 (Tryptophan Hydroxylase 1) encodes the rate-limiting enzyme in peripheral serotonin synthesis. In the cbio-kb corpus it appears as a cell-of-origin marker gene distinguishing chromaffin and non-chromaffin states in pheochromocytoma/paraganglioma.

## Alterations observed in the corpus

- TPH1 is a cell-of-origin marker gene that is highly expressed in non-chromaffin pheochromocytoma/paraganglioma tumors [PMID:40097403](../papers/40097403.md).

## Cancer types (linked)

- [PHC](../cancer_types/PHC.md) / [PGNG](../cancer_types/PGNG.md) — TPH1 expression marks the non-chromaffin cell-of-origin state in a multi-omic SDHB-deficient [PCPG](../cancer_types/PCPG.md) cohort, in contrast to TH/DBH/SLC6A2-high chromaffin/dopaminergic tumors [PMID:40097403](../papers/40097403.md).

## Co-occurrence and mutual exclusivity

- Expression pattern is inverse to [CARTPT](../genes/CARTPT.md) and HOX/HOTAIR, which are low in non-chromaffin tumors where TPH1 is high [PMID:40097403](../papers/40097403.md).

## Therapeutic relevance

- Not yet characterized in the corpus.

## Open questions

- Whether TPH1-high, non-chromaffin PCPG carries distinct clinical behavior or treatment implications is unresolved in the source text [PMID:40097403](../papers/40097403.md).
