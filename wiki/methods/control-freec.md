# Control-FREEC

## Overview

Control-FREEC is a tool for detecting copy-number alterations and allelic imbalances (including loss of heterozygosity) from whole-genome or whole-exome sequencing data, normalizing tumor read depth against a matched control (normal) sample.

## Used by

- Used alongside Battenberg for copy-number calling in a whole-genome-sequenced Burkitt lymphoma / DLBCL genetic-subgroups cohort [PMID:36201743](../papers/36201743.md)

## Notes

- Commonly paired with subclonal copy-number callers such as [Battenberg](../methods/battenberg.md) and structural-variant callers such as Manta/GRIDSS in WGS pipelines.

## Sources

- [PMID:36201743](../papers/36201743.md) — adult/pediatric Burkitt lymphoma WGS cohort; Control-FREEC used with Battenberg for CNV calling.
