# LIG3

## Overview

LIG3 encodes a DNA ligase that, together with XRCC1, provides a backup
pathway for LIG1-mediated ligation during lagging-strand DNA synthesis.

## Alterations observed in the corpus

- Provides (with XRCC1) the backup ligation pathway for LIG1-mediated Okazaki-fragment sealing; discussed as part of a proposed hemizygous compound DNA-repair deficiency alongside LIG1/POLD1/XRCC1 codeletion at 19q13.31–33 in triple-negative breast cancer [PMID:36001024](../papers/36001024.md).

## Cancer types (linked)

- [BRCA](../cancer_types/BRCA.md) — backup ligation pathway gene discussed alongside the LIG1/POLD1/XRCC1 19q13.31–33 deletion associated with chemotherapy resistance in triple-negative breast cancer [PMID:36001024](../papers/36001024.md).

## Co-occurrence and mutual exclusivity

- None reported.

- None reported.

## Open questions

- Whether LIG3/XRCC1 backup-pathway activity modulates the severity of carboplatin resistance seen in LIG1-loss tumors was not directly tested [PMID:36001024](../papers/36001024.md).
