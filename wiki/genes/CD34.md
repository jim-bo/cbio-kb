# CD34

## Overview

CD34 is a transmembrane glycoprotein marker of hematopoietic progenitor and endothelial cells. In the corpus it appears as one of the genes composing an angiogenesis ("Angio") expression signature used to predict anti-angiogenic therapy benefit in renal cell carcinoma.

## Alterations observed in the corpus

- CD34 is one of six genes (with [VEGFA](../genes/VEGFA.md), [KDR](../genes/KDR.md), [ESM1](../genes/ESM1.md), [PECAM1](../genes/PECAM1.md), [ANGPTL4](../genes/ANGPTL4.md)) in the Angio expression signature; AngioHigh tumors predicted [sunitinib](../drugs/sunitinib.md) benefit and the signature tracked CD31 ([PECAM1](../genes/PECAM1.md)) IHC vascular density [PMID:29867230](../papers/29867230.md).

## Cancer types (linked)

- Renal cell carcinoma: CD34 is part of the Angio signature used to stratify sunitinib vs. [atezolizumab](../drugs/atezolizumab.md) + [bevacizumab](../drugs/bevacizumab.md) benefit [PMID:29867230](../papers/29867230.md).

## Co-occurrence and mutual exclusivity

- None reported yet.

## Therapeutic relevance

- AngioHigh signature (including CD34) predicted sunitinib benefit over checkpoint-inhibitor monotherapy in renal cell carcinoma [PMID:29867230](../papers/29867230.md).
