# Cancer Genome Interpreter

## Overview

Cancer Genome Interpreter (CGI) is a tool that annotates somatic mutations, copy-number alterations and fusions for oncogenicity and clinical actionability, drawing on curated driver-gene and biomarker knowledgebases.

## Used by

- Cancer Genome Interpreter was used alongside dNdScv and OncodriveFML to call driver genes in 94 SDHB-related pheochromocytoma/paraganglioma tumors. [PMID:40097403](../papers/40097403.md)

## Notes

- Used as one of three orthogonal driver-discovery tools (dNdScv, OncodriveFML, CGI); a gene was treated as a driver candidate when supported by this combination rather than by CGI alone.

## Sources

- [PMID:40097403](../papers/40097403.md) — Flynn et al. 2025, *Nat Commun*, multi-omic analysis of SDHB-deficient pheochromocytomas and paragangliomas
