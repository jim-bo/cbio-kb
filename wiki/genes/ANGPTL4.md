# ANGPTL4

## Overview

ANGPTL4 (angiopoietin-like 4) encodes a secreted glycoprotein that regulates lipid metabolism, vascular permeability and angiogenesis, and is transcriptionally induced by hypoxia and PPAR signaling. In cancer, ANGPTL4 is commonly used as a marker gene of tumor angiogenic activity and has been implicated in metastatic vascular remodeling and endothelial permeability in several solid tumors, including renal cell carcinoma.

## Alterations observed in the corpus

- Component of the 6-gene Angio expression signature (with [VEGFA](../genes/VEGFA.md), [KDR](../genes/KDR.md), [ESM1](../genes/ESM1.md), [PECAM1](../genes/PECAM1.md) and [CD34](../genes/CD34.md)); AngioHigh signature status predicted [sunitinib](../drugs/sunitinib.md) benefit in metastatic renal cell carcinoma treated on the IMmotion150 trial [PMID:29867230](../papers/29867230.md)

## Cancer types (linked)

- [RCC](../cancer_types/RCC.md) / [CCRCC](../cancer_types/CCRCC.md): part of the Angio gene-expression signature used to stratify PFS benefit from sunitinib vs. [atezolizumab](../drugs/atezolizumab.md) ± [bevacizumab](../drugs/bevacizumab.md) in the IMmotion150 trial [PMID:29867230](../papers/29867230.md).

## Co-occurrence and mutual exclusivity

- Co-expressed with [VEGFA](../genes/VEGFA.md), [KDR](../genes/KDR.md), [ESM1](../genes/ESM1.md), [PECAM1](../genes/PECAM1.md) and [CD34](../genes/CD34.md) as part of the composite Angio signature; AngioHigh tumors also tended to be [PBRM1](../genes/PBRM1.md)-mutant [PMID:29867230](../papers/29867230.md).

## Therapeutic relevance

- AngioHigh signature status (including ANGPTL4) predicted greater benefit from [sunitinib](../drugs/sunitinib.md) than from [atezolizumab](../drugs/atezolizumab.md) monotherapy or atezolizumab + [bevacizumab](../drugs/bevacizumab.md) in metastatic [RCC](../cancer_types/RCC.md) [PMID:29867230](../papers/29867230.md).

## Open questions

- Whether ANGPTL4 expression alone (rather than the composite Angio signature) carries independent predictive value for anti-angiogenic therapy response [PMID:29867230](../papers/29867230.md).
