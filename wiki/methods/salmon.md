# Salmon

## Overview

Salmon is a lightweight, alignment-free tool for transcript-level quantification of RNA-seq data. It uses quasi-mapping and an expectation-maximization procedure to estimate transcript abundances (TPM, estimated counts) directly from raw reads against a transcriptome index, without requiring a full read alignment step.

## Used by

- Used for RNA-seq expression quantification feeding DESeq2 differential expression and GSVA pathway analysis in a 281-tumor Burkitt lymphoma / DLBCL genomic study [PMID:36201743](../papers/36201743.md).
