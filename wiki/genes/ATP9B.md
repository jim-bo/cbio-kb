# ATP9B

## Overview

ATP9B encodes a P4-type ATPase (phospholipid flippase) with no well-established role in cancer biology. In the corpus it appears only as one of several low-frequency genes included in a clear cell renal cell carcinoma (ccRCC) prognostic mutation classifier because of preliminary associations with outcome or clinical features, though its individual significance was not robustly established.

## Alterations observed in the corpus

- Included among lower-frequency genes (with ATM, COL11A1, DMD, PTK7, TRRAP) added to a ccRCC prognostic mutation classifier because of preliminary links to outcome or clinical features in the C1/C2 subgroups; the authors state individual significance "could not be robustly established" even in this large cohort [PMID:36815791](../papers/36815791.md).
