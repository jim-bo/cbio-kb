# KIAA0586

## Overview

KIAA0586 encodes TALPID3, a centrosomal/ciliary protein. It was identified as a novel highly-confident significantly mutated gene (HC-SMG) in a large colorectal cancer whole-exome cohort, expanding the candidate driver list beyond canonical WNT/RAS/TGF-beta pathway genes.

## Alterations observed in the corpus

- KIAA0586 (encodes TALPID3) was a novel highly confident significantly mutated gene (HC-SMG) in colorectal cancer, mutated in 2% of the cohort [PMID:35487942](../papers/35487942.md).

## Cancer types (linked)

- Colorectal cancer ([COADREAD](../cancer_types/COADREAD.md)): one of eight novel HC-SMGs (with [LYST](../genes/LYST.md), [DAPK1](../genes/DAPK1.md), [CR2](../genes/CR2.md), [KIF16B](../genes/KIF16B.md), [NPIPB15](../genes/NPIPB15.md), [SYTL2](../genes/SYTL2.md), [ZNF91](../genes/ZNF91.md)) that together were mutated in 14.9% of the cohort and 19.55% of TCGA colorectal cases [PMID:35487942](../papers/35487942.md).
