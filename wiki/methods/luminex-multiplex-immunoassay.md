# Luminex Multiplex Immunoassay

## Overview

The Luminex multiplex immunoassay is a bead-based platform for simultaneous quantification of multiple soluble proteins (e.g., cytokines, chemokines) from a single small-volume sample, commonly used to profile circulating immune-response biomarkers in clinical trials.

## Used by

- Used to profile circulating cytokines as part of an immunomodulatory activity assessment of nivolumab in metastatic renal cell carcinoma [PMID:27169994](../papers/27169994.md).

## Notes

- Corpus-grown slug; not in the cBioPortal gene-panel or molecular-profile ontology.
- Used in combination with tumor microarray gene-expression profiling and IHC to assess treatment-associated immune changes.
