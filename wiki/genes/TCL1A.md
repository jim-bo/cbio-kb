# TCL1A

## Overview

TCL1A (T-Cell Leukemia/Lymphoma 1A) is a coactivator of AKT signaling and a known oncogene in mature B-cell and T-cell lymphomas. In the cbio-kb corpus it appears as a target of aberrant somatic hypermutation (aSHM) in Burkitt lymphoma.

## Alterations observed in the corpus

- TCL1A is an aberrant somatic hypermutation (aSHM) target gene mutated more often in Burkitt lymphoma than in diffuse large B-cell lymphoma [PMID:36201743](../papers/36201743.md).

## Cancer types (linked)

- [BL](../cancer_types/BL.md) — TCL1A aSHM burden is higher in Burkitt lymphoma than in DLBCL, consistent with its role as an aSHM target gene in a whole-genome-sequenced adult/pediatric [BL](../cancer_types/BL.md) cohort [PMID:36201743](../papers/36201743.md).
