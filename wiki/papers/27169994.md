**PMID:** 27169994 · **DOI:** 10.1158/1078-0432.CCR-15-2839 · **Journal:** Clinical Cancer Research (2016)

## TL;DR

Choueiri et al. report a hypothesis-generating, open-label, four-arm phase Ib pharmacodynamic trial (NCT01358721) of the anti-PD-1 antibody [nivolumab](../drugs/nivolumab.md) in 91 treated patients with metastatic [clear-cell renal cell carcinoma](../cancer_types/CCRCC.md). Previously treated patients were randomized to 0.3, 2 or 10 mg/kg every 3 weeks, and treatment-naive patients received 10 mg/kg. Protocol-mandated fresh core-needle biopsies were taken at baseline and on treatment (cycle 2 day 8, C2D8), with serial blood draws. On treatment, tumors showed more CD3+ and CD8+ T cells, and interferon-γ-driven transcripts went up, including [CXCL9](../genes/CXCL9.md), [CXCL10](../genes/CXCL10.md), [CD274](../genes/CD274.md) (PD-L1), [CTLA4](../genes/CTLA4.md), [ICOS](../genes/ICOS.md), granzymes and NK-cell genes. Serum [CXCL9](../genes/CXCL9.md) and [CXCL10](../genes/CXCL10.md) also rose. Tumor-cell PD-L1 staining did not change consistently. These pharmacodynamic effects were similar across doses and between previously treated and treatment-naive patients. The objective response rate (ORR) was 15%. Median overall survival (OS) was 16.4 and 25.2 months in the 0.3 and 10 mg/kg arms, and was not reached in the 2 mg/kg and treatment-naive arms. This paper backs the cBioPortal study [ccrcc_iatlas_choueiri_2016](../datasets/ccrcc_iatlas_choueiri_2016.md) [PMID:27169994](../papers/27169994.md).

## Cohort & data

- **Trial design:** Open-label, parallel, four-group phase Ib study of [nivolumab](../drugs/nivolumab.md) (NCT01358721). Previously treated patients were randomized 1:1:1 to 0.3, 2 or 10 mg/kg IV every 3 weeks. Treatment-naive patients received 10 mg/kg. Treatment beyond progression was allowed if the investigator saw clinical benefit. The data cutoff was January 12, 2015 [PMID:27169994](../papers/27169994.md).
- **Eligibility:** Histologically confirmed metastatic [RCC](../cancer_types/RCC.md) with a clear-cell component ([CCRCC](../cancer_types/CCRCC.md)), measurable disease by RECIST v1.1, Karnofsky performance score ≥70%, and soft-tissue lesions that could be biopsied at baseline and on treatment. The previously treated arms required 1–3 prior systemic therapies, with progression within 6 months of enrollment. Prior anti-CTLA-4, anti-PD-1, anti-PD-L1, anti-PD-L2, anti-CD137, anti-CD40 or anti-OX40 antibodies were exclusions [PMID:27169994](../papers/27169994.md).
- **Patients:** Enrolled September 2011 – September 2012 at 14 international centers. 92 were assigned and 91 treated: 67 previously treated (22 at 0.3 mg/kg, 22 at 2 mg/kg, 23 at 10 mg/kg) and 24 treatment-naive (10 mg/kg). Median age was 61 years, 67% were male, and 66% had prior therapy for metastatic disease [PMID:27169994](../papers/27169994.md).
- **Biopsies:** Image-guided core-needle biopsies were taken at screening and at C2D8. 60/91 patients had the same lesion re-biopsied, and 13/91 had a distinct or unknown second lesion. Sites were liver (16/73), lymph node (12/73), kidney (9/73), lung (6/73), adrenal (4/73), pancreas (2) and other soft tissue (24/73). Each biopsy was split, with half formalin-fixed paraffin-embedded (FFPE) for IHC and half stored in RNAlater for expression profiling [PMID:27169994](../papers/27169994.md).
- **Assays:**
  - Multiplex [immunohistochemistry](../methods/immunohistochemistry.md) (Mosaic Laboratories) with a CD3/CD8 dual stain and a CD3/CD4/[FOXP3](../genes/FOXP3.md) triple stain. Slides were imaged on a Nuance multispectral system and analyzed with inForm v1.2. Paired baseline and C2D8 values were available for n = 36 [PMID:27169994](../papers/27169994.md).
  - Tumor-cell PD-L1 by the BMS/Dako automated IHC assay with the 28-8 antibody ([pd-l1-ihc-28-8](../methods/pd-l1-ihc-28-8.md)), scored as ≥5% vs <5% tumor-membrane staining. 56 baseline biopsies were evaluable [PMID:27169994](../papers/27169994.md).
  - [Microarray gene expression](../methods/microarray-gene-expression.md) on the Affymetrix HG-U219 array plate run on the GeneTitan platform ([affymetrix-hg-u219](../methods/affymetrix-hg-u219.md)). RNA was labeled with WT-Pico Ovation and intensities were summarized by RMA over 18,562 loci (BrainArray v.10). Tumor samples were 59 at baseline and 55 at C2D8 (42 patients paired). Whole-blood samples were 82 at C1D1, 74 at C1D2 and 73 at C2D8. Data are deposited in ArrayExpress as E-MTAB-3218 and E-MTAB-3219 [PMID:27169994](../papers/27169994.md).
  - Serum chemokines and soluble factors measured on a [Luminex multiplex panel](../methods/luminex-multiplex-immunoassay.md) (Myriad Rules-Based Medicine) at C1D1 before and after dosing, C2D1, C2D8 and C4D1. n = 83 had paired baseline and C2D8 values [PMID:27169994](../papers/27169994.md).
  - Statistics: expression changes were modeled with an extended linear model fit by REML (nlme 3.1-109, R 3.0.1). The model used a spatial exponential within-patient correlation and a multi-contrast conditional F test for time on study. Thresholds were P < 0.01 with ≥1.3-fold change (tumor) or ≥1.2-fold change (blood). Immune-lineage enrichment was tested against 1,539 lineage genes with Bonferroni correction, and pathway impact with MetaCore. PFS and OS used [Kaplan–Meier](../methods/kaplan-meier.md) estimates with Greenwood CIs, and ORR CIs used the Clopper–Pearson method [PMID:27169994](../papers/27169994.md).
- **Cancer type:** [Renal Clear Cell Carcinoma (CCRCC)](../cancer_types/CCRCC.md).
- **Dataset:** [ccrcc_iatlas_choueiri_2016](../datasets/ccrcc_iatlas_choueiri_2016.md). cBioPortal's study metadata (not this paper) describes it as whole-exome sequencing and neoantigen analysis of 16 metastatic RCCs with matched normals from this phase 1b trial. The raw data were reprocessed by CRI iAtlas on hg38. The paper itself reports only IHC, microarray and serum-protein data, and does not describe exome sequencing.

## Key findings

- **Clinical activity (all 91 treated):** ORR was 15% (14/91; 95% CI 8.7–24.5). By arm, ORR was 9% at 0.3 mg/kg (2/22), 18% at 2 mg/kg (4/22), 22% at 10 mg/kg (5/23) and 13% in treatment-naive patients (3/24). There were 2 complete responses, both in the treatment-naive arm. Overall best responses were 12 PR, 42 stable disease (46%) and 27 progressive disease (30%) [PMID:27169994](../papers/27169994.md).
- **PFS and OS:** PFS rates were 43% (95% CI 32–53) at 24 weeks and 25% (16–35) at 48 weeks. OS rates were 75% (64–83) at 12 months and 58% (46–68) at 24 months. Median OS was 16.4 months (10.1–NR) at 0.3 mg/kg, not reached at 2 mg/kg, and 25.2 months (12.0–NR) at 10 mg/kg. It was not reached for treatment-naive patients, whose 24-month OS rate was 76%. The authors saw no obvious dose-response relationship [PMID:27169994](../papers/27169994.md).
- **Tumor-associated lymphocytes (IHC, n = 36 paired):** From baseline to C2D8, the median [absolute](../methods/absolute.md) increases in the proportion of CD3+, [CD4](../genes/CD4.md)+ and CD8+ cells were 9.83%, 0.44% and 2.64%. The median percent changes were +69%, +180% and +117%, with most patients increasing. Most patients had very low or undetectable CD4+ cells at baseline, and 14 had both baseline CD4+ levels and changes below 0.45%. Results did not vary by dose or prior-treatment status [PMID:27169994](../papers/27169994.md).
- **Matching transcript changes (n = 114 biopsies):** [CD3D](../genes/CD3D.md) rose 1.7-fold (P = 0.006) and [CD8A](../genes/CD8A.md) 1.7-fold (P = 0.002). [CD4](../genes/CD4.md) rose 1.2-fold, which was not significant (P = 0.175). None of these changes depended on dose or prior treatment [PMID:27169994](../papers/27169994.md).
- **PD-L1:** 18 of 56 evaluable baseline biopsies (32%) had ≥5% tumor-cell PD-L1. In matched biopsies, PD-L1 did not change consistently from baseline to on-treatment [PMID:27169994](../papers/27169994.md).
- **Tumor transcriptome on treatment:** 108 transcripts changed over time (≥1.3-fold, P < 0.01).
  - 71 had prior immune-lineage associations (enrichment P = 1.3×10⁻⁷⁶), and all 71 increased at C2D8.
  - 43 were lymphoid- or myeloid-lineage specific. Lymphoid examples include granzymes (reported as "GZMA/G/H") and [KLRB1](../genes/KLRB1.md), [KLRD1](../genes/KLRD1.md) and [KLRG1](../genes/KLRG1.md). Myeloid examples include [CXCL11](../genes/CXCL11.md), [CD274](../genes/CD274.md) and [IDO1](../genes/IDO1.md).
  - 10 transcripts were T-cell specific, e.g. [CTLA4](../genes/CTLA4.md), [CD8A](../genes/CD8A.md), [CD8B](../genes/CD8B.md), [CD3D](../genes/CD3D.md), [CD3E](../genes/CD3E.md), [CD3G](../genes/CD3G.md) and [ICOS](../genes/ICOS.md).
  - 16 were interferon-regulated, including [CXCL9](../genes/CXCL9.md) and [CD274](../genes/CD274.md). Interferon-γ ([IFNG](../genes/IFNG.md)) was the only interferon among the 108.
  - 47 overlapped with genes previously reported as differentially expressed in baseline biopsies of [ipilimumab](../drugs/ipilimumab.md) responders (P = 2.7×10⁻⁸⁶) [PMID:27169994](../papers/27169994.md).
- **Peripheral blood transcriptome:** 59 transcripts changed from C1D1 to C1D2, i.e. within 24 h (≥1.2-fold, P < 0.01). 30 of them were immune-lineage associated (P < 0.001). T-cell receptor α and β subunit transcripts and CD3γ decreased. All 29 interferon-regulated transcripts increased, but no interferon gene transcripts were regulated or detectable in blood [PMID:27169994](../papers/27169994.md).
- **Dose and prior-therapy independence:** Transcriptional effects were broadly similar across dose groups and between previously treated and treatment-naive patients (P > 0.01 for the time × group interaction). A small number of group-specific effects are listed in the supplementary tables: 37 tumor probesets and 24 blood probesets [PMID:27169994](../papers/27169994.md).
- **Serum chemokines (n = 83, baseline → C2D8):**
  - Median CXCL9 change was +1,861 pg/mL (range −2,000 to 22,890), or +101%. Median CXCL10 change was +157 pg/mL (range −398 to 3,930), or +37%.
  - Most patients rose in both, across all baseline values. The CXCL9 and CXCL10 changes were highly correlated, and CXCL9 changes tended to be larger [PMID:27169994](../papers/27169994.md).
- **Tumor chemokine transcripts:** CXCL9 rose 2.4-fold (P < 0.001) and CXCL10 2-fold (P = 0.011). At C2D8, serum levels correlated with transcript levels in the matching biopsy (N = 54): CXCL9 r = 0.37 (P = 0.006) and CXCL10 r = 0.30 (P = 0.029). Pre-treatment serum did not correlate with screening-biopsy transcripts (r < 0.21, P > 0.1). Neither change was associated with dose or prior treatment [PMID:27169994](../papers/27169994.md).
- **Safety:**
  - Treatment-related AEs of any grade occurred in all patients, most often fatigue and nausea, mainly grade 1–2. Grade 3–4 AEs occurred in 54% of previously treated and 50% of treatment-naive patients.
  - Pneumonitis (the only [select](../methods/select.md) pulmonary AE) occurred in 3 previously treated patients (1 at 0.3 mg/kg, 2 at 10 mg/kg) and 3 treatment-naive patients.
  - No new safety signals were identified [PMID:27169994](../papers/27169994.md).

## Genes & alterations

This study measured protein and expression changes under PD-1 blockade, not somatic alterations.

- [PDCD1](../genes/PDCD1.md) (PD-1): the target of [nivolumab](../drugs/nivolumab.md). All of the pharmacodynamic effects below are attributed to PD-1 inhibition [PMID:27169994](../papers/27169994.md).
- [CD274](../genes/CD274.md) (PD-L1): tumor-cell membrane PD-L1 by IHC was ≥5% in 32% of baseline biopsies (18/56), with no consistent on-treatment change. [CD274](../genes/CD274.md) transcripts nonetheless increased on treatment. The authors attribute this to myeloid infiltrates rather than tumor cells [PMID:27169994](../papers/27169994.md).
- [CXCL9](../genes/CXCL9.md), [CXCL10](../genes/CXCL10.md), [CXCL11](../genes/CXCL11.md): these interferon-γ-regulated chemokines rose in tumor (CXCL9 2.4-fold, CXCL10 2-fold) and in serum (median +101% and +37%). The authors propose that [nivolumab](../drugs/nivolumab.md) induces myeloid cells at tissue inflammatory sites to make CXCL9 and CXCL10, which recruits T cells. [CXCL11](../genes/CXCL11.md) was among the myeloid-specific transcripts that increased [PMID:27169994](../papers/27169994.md).
- [CD3D](../genes/CD3D.md), [CD3E](../genes/CD3E.md), [CD3G](../genes/CD3G.md), [CD8A](../genes/CD8A.md), [CD8B](../genes/CD8B.md): these T-cell transcripts increased in tumor ([CD3D](../genes/CD3D.md) and [CD8A](../genes/CD8A.md) 1.7-fold each), matching the IHC increase in CD3+ and CD8+ infiltrates. CD3γ transcripts fell transiently in whole blood, which the authors read as T cells leaving the periphery [PMID:27169994](../papers/27169994.md).
- [CD4](../genes/CD4.md), [FOXP3](../genes/FOXP3.md): CD4 transcripts did not change significantly (1.2-fold, P = 0.175), and most patients had very low or undetectable CD4+ infiltrates. [FOXP3](../genes/FOXP3.md) was part of the triple-stain IHC panel, but the paper reports no separate FOXP3 result [PMID:27169994](../papers/27169994.md).
- [CTLA4](../genes/CTLA4.md), [ICOS](../genes/ICOS.md): these T-cell-specific transcripts were upregulated on nivolumab. [CTLA4](../genes/CTLA4.md) upregulation is the authors' rationale for combining with anti-CTLA-4 therapy [PMID:27169994](../papers/27169994.md).
- [IFNG](../genes/IFNG.md), [GZMA](../genes/GZMA.md), [GZMH](../genes/GZMH.md), [PRF1](../genes/PRF1.md): Th1 and cytotoxic hallmark genes (interferon-γ, granzymes, perforin) were upregulated in tumor. [IFNG](../genes/IFNG.md) was the only interferon gene among the 108 changed transcripts, and was not detected in blood [PMID:27169994](../papers/27169994.md).
- [IDO1](../genes/IDO1.md): a myeloid-lineage transcript that increased in tumor on treatment [PMID:27169994](../papers/27169994.md).
- [KLRB1](../genes/KLRB1.md), [KLRD1](../genes/KLRD1.md), [KLRG1](../genes/KLRG1.md), [CD69](../genes/CD69.md), [NKG7](../genes/NKG7.md): these innate and NK-cell function genes increased significantly in tumor. The authors hypothesize that NK cells are engaged and suggest combining with NK-directed agents such as [lirilumab](../drugs/lirilumab.md) [PMID:27169994](../papers/27169994.md).

## Clinical implications

- The authors conclude that biomarker changes from baseline reflect nivolumab pharmacodynamics in the tumor microenvironment. These changes appear across all doses tested (0.3–10 mg/kg) and in most patients, and "may inform potential combinations" [PMID:27169994](../papers/27169994.md).
- **Anti-CTLA-4 combinations:** nivolumab raised CTLA4 transcripts and many genes linked to [ipilimumab](../drugs/ipilimumab.md) response (47 of the 108 transcripts). The authors present this as support for combining with anti-CTLA-4 therapy [PMID:27169994](../papers/27169994.md).
- **NK-directed combinations:** the rise in NK-function genes led the authors to suggest combining nivolumab with NK cell-directed therapies such as [lirilumab](../drugs/lirilumab.md) in metastatic RCC [PMID:27169994](../papers/27169994.md).
- **Clinical activity:** activity was seen in both treatment-naive and previously treated patients at every dose, with no clear dose-response. Median OS of 16.4–25.2 months in previously treated patients is described as similar to the earlier phase II dose-ranging study (18.2–25.5 months) and to the phase III trial of nivolumab versus [everolimus](../drugs/everolimus.md) (25.0 vs 19.6 months; HR = 0.73, P = 0.002) [PMID:27169994](../papers/27169994.md).
- **Chemokines as markers:** the authors cite prior work showing that high intratumoral CXCL9/CXCL10 expression is a favorable prognostic factor in RCC, and that pretreatment tumor CXCL9/CXCL10 transcripts are associated with [ipilimumab](../drugs/ipilimumab.md) response. Serum CXCL9/CXCL10 tracked on-treatment tumor transcript levels (r = 0.37 and 0.30), so blood could serve as a less invasive pharmacodynamic readout. That use is implied by the correlation, but the paper does not validate it [PMID:27169994](../papers/27169994.md).
- **PD-L1:** in this study, tumor-cell PD-L1 (28-8 assay, 5% cutoff) did not change consistently on treatment. The introduction notes that PD-L1 "does not appear to define the chance of response in a dichotomous way". The paper does not test baseline PD-L1 against outcome [PMID:27169994](../papers/27169994.md).

## Limitations & open questions

- **Exploratory design:** the study was explicitly hypothesis-generating and not designed to test a specific hypothesis. Sample size was not based on power calculations, and pharmacodynamic effects are reported mostly as summary statistics [PMID:27169994](../papers/27169994.md).
- **No link to outcome:** the paper does not relate biomarker changes to clinical response or survival. The authors say these analyses were still under way [PMID:27169994](../papers/27169994.md).
- **No untreated control:** every arm received nivolumab, so on-treatment changes are attributed to the drug without a concurrent control. Only one on-treatment timepoint (C2D8) was biopsied [PMID:27169994](../papers/27169994.md).
- **Small paired subsets:** paired IHC data covered only n = 36 of 91 treated patients, and PD-L1 was evaluable in 56 baseline biopsies. Tumor models had to adjust for process batch and for sex, because women were unevenly represented across arms [PMID:27169994](../papers/27169994.md).
- **Repeat biopsies of the same lesion:** about two-thirds of patients were re-biopsied at the same lesion. The authors do not think this affected their conclusions, but a biopsy-induced inflammatory effect is not formally excluded [PMID:27169994](../papers/27169994.md).
- **Internal inconsistencies in the source:**
  - The percent-change range reported for CXCL9 (45–1,730%) conflicts with a reported absolute-change range that includes decreases (−2,000 pg/mL).
  - Grade 3–4 treatment-related AE totals (36–68% per arm) sit uneasily with per-event grade 3–4 counts that are mostly 0–2 per arm, and with the Discussion's statement that "the frequency of severe AEs was low".
  - Treat these numbers with caution in cross-paper synthesis [PMID:27169994](../papers/27169994.md).
- **Mismatch with the cBioPortal study:** the linked study [ccrcc_iatlas_choueiri_2016](../datasets/ccrcc_iatlas_choueiri_2016.md) holds exome and neoantigen data for 16 tumors (per cBioPortal metadata). This paper does not describe those data, so the genomic content should not be attributed to its findings.
- **Sponsor involvement:** several authors were Bristol-Myers Squibb employees or shareholders at the time of the analysis [PMID:27169994](../papers/27169994.md).
- **Related corpus work:**
  - The on-treatment immune activation seen here parallels the paired pre/on-nivolumab biopsy study in melanoma, where checkpoint genes including [CTLA4](../genes/CTLA4.md) and [CD274](../genes/CD274.md) were also upregulated on therapy [PMID:29033130](../papers/29033130.md).
  - Genomic correlates of response to immune checkpoint therapy in clear-cell RCC are covered in [PMID:29301960](../papers/29301960.md).
