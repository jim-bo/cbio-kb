# KRT1

## Overview

KRT1 encodes keratin 1, a structural cytokeratin. In the corpus it appears as part of a pretreatment expression signature associated with non-response to pembrolizumab-based immunotherapy in triple-negative breast cancer.

## Alterations observed in the corpus

- KRT1 (with [NRAP](../genes/NRAP.md) and [KRT2](../genes/KRT2.md)) showed higher pretreatment expression in tumors of non-responders (SD+PD) to pembrolizumab-based therapy in triple-negative breast cancer [PMID:35121644](../papers/35121644.md).

## Cancer types (linked)

- Breast cancer ([BRCA](../cancer_types/BRCA.md)), specifically triple-negative breast cancer: elevated pretreatment KRT1 expression tracked with non-response in a phase 2 cyclophosphamide-priming plus [pembrolizumab](../drugs/pembrolizumab.md) trial [PMID:35121644](../papers/35121644.md).

## Co-occurrence and mutual exclusivity

- Elevated alongside NRAP and KRT2 in non-responder pretreatment tumors [PMID:35121644](../papers/35121644.md).

## Therapeutic relevance

- Proposed (not validated) as part of a predictive expression signature for immune-checkpoint-inhibitor non-response; KRT1 is not itself a drug target in this study [PMID:35121644](../papers/35121644.md).

## Open questions

- The mechanistic basis for the association between epithelial/keratin gene expression and checkpoint-inhibitor non-response is not established in the corpus [PMID:35121644](../papers/35121644.md).
