# NRAP

## Overview

NRAP (nebulin-related anchoring protein) is not a known somatic cancer driver. In the cbio-kb corpus it appears as a pretreatment tumor-expression biomarker candidate associated with non-response to pembrolizumab-based immunotherapy in metastatic triple-negative breast cancer.

## Alterations observed in the corpus

- No somatic mutations or copy-number alterations reported; observed only as a gene-expression finding.

## Cancer types (linked)

- **[BRCA](../cancer_types/BRCA.md)** (metastatic triple-negative breast cancer): higher pretreatment NRAP expression (with KRT1, KRT2) was seen in tumors of patients who did not respond (stable or progressive disease) to a cyclophosphamide-priming plus pembrolizumab regimen [PMID:35121644](../papers/35121644.md).

## Co-occurrence and mutual exclusivity

- Co-elevated with KRT1 and KRT2 as a non-responder pretreatment expression signature; contrasts with ANKRD30A, SYCE1, and TUBB2B, which were higher in responders [PMID:35121644](../papers/35121644.md).

## Therapeutic relevance

- No drug directly targets NRAP; it was studied as a candidate predictive biomarker for response to [pembrolizumab](../drugs/pembrolizumab.md) [PMID:35121644](../papers/35121644.md).

## Open questions

- The biological mechanism linking NRAP expression to immunotherapy non-response is not established in the corpus.
