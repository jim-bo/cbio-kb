# trastuzumab-deruxtecan

## Overview

Trastuzumab deruxtecan (T-DXd) is an anti-HER2 antibody-drug conjugate (ADC) composed of [trastuzumab](../drugs/trastuzumab.md) linked to a topoisomerase I inhibitor payload. It has FDA approvals in HER2-positive and HER2-low breast cancer, HER2-positive gastric/gastroesophageal junction adenocarcinoma, HER2-mutant [NSCLC](../cancer_types/NSCLC.md), and other HER2-expressing solid tumors.

## Evidence in the corpus

- In 177 cervical cancers profiled by MSK-IMPACT, [trastuzumab](../drugs/trastuzumab.md) deruxtecan is under investigation (NCT04482309) for HER2-mutant/amplified cervical cancer; [ERBB2](../genes/ERBB2.md) is altered in 28% of unusual endocervical adenocarcinoma (UEA) and 12% overall, establishing [ERBB2](../genes/ERBB2.md) as a likely important target [PMID:37643132](../papers/37643132.md).
- In endometrial carcinoma, [ERBB2](../genes/ERBB2.md) amplification enriched in Black patients (12% vs 3% in White patients) represents a therapeutic opportunity with [trastuzumab](../drugs/trastuzumab.md) deruxtecan; activity was noted even in low-HER2-expressing carcinosarcomas [PMID:37651310](../papers/37651310.md).
- In metastatic urothelial carcinoma (mUC), [ERBB2](../genes/ERBB2.md) alterations (detected in 14% of cfDNA samples from the CALGB 90601 cohort) were independently associated with shorter [OS](../cancer_types/OS.md) (multivariable HR 1.64, 95% CI 1.08–2.49; p=0.019). The authors highlight [trastuzumab](../drugs/trastuzumab.md) deruxtecan as a candidate HER2-directed antibody-drug conjugate for ERBB2-altered mUC, citing response rates of 33–83% in this setting. [PMID:40256659](../papers/40256659.md)
- HER2-positive CCA: basket phase II across 7 cohorts ORR 37.1% overall, 22.0% in 41 CCA patients; mPFS 4.6 mo, mOS 7.0 mo in CCA; risk of interstitial pneumonitis noted [PMID:41008893](../papers/41008893.md)
- In a HER2 S310F [UTUC](../cancer_types/UTUC.md) PDX model (UCC14), [trastuzumab](../drugs/trastuzumab.md) deruxtecan (DS-8201a) strongly inhibited tumor growth (p<0.0001) and significantly outperformed [neratinib](../drugs/neratinib.md) (p<0.0001); authors conclude that ADCs should be prioritized over HER kinase inhibitors in HER2-mutant urothelial cancer [PMID:32332851](../papers/32332851.md)
- Anti-HER2 ADC (T-DXd); [ERBB2](../genes/ERBB2.md) GoF mutations in 3% of [NSCLC](../cancer_types/NSCLC.md) in Chinese pan-cancer cohort (n=10,194) are actionable with [trastuzumab](../drugs/trastuzumab.md) deruxtecan per OncoKB [PMID:35871175](../papers/35871175.md)
- Trastuzumab deruxtecan (T-DXd) is proposed as promising for ERBB2-mutated endometrial cancer because it is active in HER2-low/negative tumors across extracellular- and kinase-domain mutations; a 55% objective response rate is cited for T-DXd in ERBB2-mutant [NSCLC](../cancer_types/NSCLC.md). [PMID:39031567](../papers/39031567.md)
- In BT-474 breast cancer cells, endogenous APOBEC3 activity conferred a selective growth advantage under trastuzumab deruxtecan exposure, similar to the advantage seen under [lapatinib](../drugs/lapatinib.md), [neratinib](../drugs/neratinib.md) and the AKT inhibitor MK2206. [PMID:40379787](../papers/40379787.md)
- In a metastatic gallbladder cancer case with retained [ERBB2](../genes/ERBB2.md) amplification (10-fold) and HER2 IHC 3+ after progression on [zanidatamab](../drugs/zanidatamab.md), trastuzumab deruxtecan gave sustained tumor shrinkage ongoing at 16+ months. [PMID:42360806](../papers/42360806.md)
