# GZMB

## Overview

GZMB encodes granzyme B, the principal pro-apoptotic serine protease of cytotoxic T cells and NK cells.

## Alterations observed in the corpus

- Effector cytotoxic component of the 20-gene ICR signature in colon cancer [PMID:37202560](../papers/37202560.md).
- Component of the T-effector gene expression signature (with CD8A, GZMA, PRF1, IFNG, TBX21) evaluated in atezolizumab-treated urothelial carcinoma; the signature did not differ significantly by tumor primary site [PMID:27939400](../papers/27939400.md).

## Cancer types (linked)

- [COAD](../cancer_types/COAD.md) — ICR component in the AC-ICAM cohort [PMID:37202560](../papers/37202560.md).

## Co-occurrence and mutual exclusivity

- Co-expressed with [GNLY](../genes/GNLY.md), [PRF1](../genes/PRF1.md), [GZMA](../genes/GZMA.md), [GZMH](../genes/GZMH.md) [PMID:37202560](../papers/37202560.md).

## Therapeutic relevance

- Component of the ICR prognostic immune module [PMID:37202560](../papers/37202560.md).
