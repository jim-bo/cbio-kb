# KLRG1

## Overview

KLRG1 (killer cell lectin-like receptor G1) encodes an inhibitory receptor expressed on NK cells and terminally differentiated T cells. In the corpus it appears as a pharmacodynamic marker of innate/NK-cell engagement in on-treatment tumor biopsies from an anti-PD-1 clinical trial.

## Alterations observed in the corpus

- KLRG1 transcript expression increased significantly in tumor on [nivolumab](../drugs/nivolumab.md) treatment, among innate/NK-cell function genes the authors propose reflect NK-cell engagement [PMID:27169994](../papers/27169994.md).

## Cancer types (linked)

- Clear cell renal cell carcinoma ([CCRCC](../cancer_types/CCRCC.md)): paired on-treatment tumor biopsies from a nivolumab phase 1b trial showed increased KLRG1 expression [PMID:27169994](../papers/27169994.md).

## Co-occurrence and mutual exclusivity

- Rose alongside [KLRB1](../genes/KLRB1.md), [KLRD1](../genes/KLRD1.md), [CD69](../genes/CD69.md) and [NKG7](../genes/NKG7.md) as a group of innate/NK-cell genes upregulated on nivolumab treatment [PMID:27169994](../papers/27169994.md).

## Therapeutic relevance

- The authors propose combining anti-PD-1 therapy with NK-cell-directed agents such as [lirilumab](../drugs/lirilumab.md), based on this NK-cell gene induction [PMID:27169994](../papers/27169994.md).

## Open questions

- Whether KLRG1 upregulation reflects NK-cell infiltration versus activation of pre-existing intratumoral NK cells is not resolved in the corpus [PMID:27169994](../papers/27169994.md).
