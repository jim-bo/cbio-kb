# BAX

## Overview

BAX (BCL2-Associated X protein) is a pro-apoptotic member of the BCL2 family. In the corpus it appears as a gene located within a recurrent GISTIC2 copy-number deletion peak, alongside its antagonist BCL2, in a pediatric solid tumor genomics cohort.

## Alterations observed in the corpus

- Located in a GISTIC2 copy-number deletion peak (with BCL2) among apoptosis regulators in a hepatoblastoma/pediatric solid tumor PDX genomics cohort [PMID:37990009](../papers/37990009.md).

## Cancer types (linked)

- [LIHB](../cancer_types/LIHB.md) — BAX deletion peak identified by GISTIC2 among hepatoblastoma and other pediatric solid tumor exomes in a PDX modeling cohort [PMID:37990009](../papers/37990009.md).

## Co-occurrence and mutual exclusivity

- Co-located with BCL2 in the same GISTIC2 deletion peak, consistent with combined loss of pro- and anti-apoptotic BCL2-family regulators in this cohort [PMID:37990009](../papers/37990009.md).

## Therapeutic relevance

- Not characterized in the corpus.

## Open questions

- Functional consequence of the shared BAX/BCL2 deletion peak on apoptotic signaling in these tumors is not established [PMID:37990009](../papers/37990009.md).
