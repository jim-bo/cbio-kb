# TUBB2B

## Overview

TUBB2B encodes a beta-tubulin isotype involved in microtubule structure and is best known for its role in cortical dysplasia; its expression has also been studied as a candidate biomarker in cancer. In the cbio-kb corpus it appears as a gene whose pretreatment tumor expression differed by immunotherapy response in triple-negative breast cancer.

## Alterations observed in the corpus

- TUBB2B expression was higher in pretreatment tumors of responders (CR+PR) in a triple-negative breast cancer pembrolizumab trial [PMID:35121644](../papers/35121644.md).

## Cancer types (linked)

- [BRCA](../cancer_types/BRCA.md) — In a phase 2 metastatic triple-negative breast cancer trial of cyclophosphamide priming plus pembrolizumab, higher pretreatment TUBB2B (with ANKRD30A, SYCE1) expression distinguished responders from non-responders [PMID:35121644](../papers/35121644.md).

## Co-occurrence and mutual exclusivity

- Grouped with [ANKRD30A](../genes/ANKRD30A.md) and [SYCE1](../genes/SYCE1.md) as genes with higher pretreatment expression in responders, contrasted with [KRT1](../genes/KRT1.md), [NRAP](../genes/NRAP.md) and [KRT2](../genes/KRT2.md), which were higher in non-responders [PMID:35121644](../papers/35121644.md).

## Therapeutic relevance

- Proposed as part of an exploratory pretreatment expression signature associated with response to pembrolizumab in metastatic TNBC; not independently validated [PMID:35121644](../papers/35121644.md).

## Open questions

- Whether TUBB2B expression is mechanistically linked to immunotherapy response or is a correlative biomarker in a small trial cohort is unresolved in the source text [PMID:35121644](../papers/35121644.md).
