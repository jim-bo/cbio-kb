# DAPK1

## Overview

DAPK1 (death-associated protein kinase 1) is a calcium/calmodulin-regulated serine/threonine kinase implicated in apoptosis. It has been reported as a novel significantly mutated gene outside its previously known cancer association.

## Alterations observed in the corpus

- DAPK1 is a novel high-confidence significantly mutated gene in colorectal cancer (~5%); its only prior cancer association was with pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma. [PMID:35487942](../papers/35487942.md)
