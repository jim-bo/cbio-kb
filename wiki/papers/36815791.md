**PMID:** 36815791 · **DOI:** 10.1158/1078-0432.CCR-22-1936 · **Journal:** Clinical Cancer Research (2023)

## TL;DR

Vasudev, Scelo, Glennon and colleagues looked at somatic mutation status in 12 genes relevant to clear-cell renal cell carcinoma ([CCRCC](../cancer_types/CCRCC.md)) in tumor–normal pairs from 943 nephrectomy patients. The patients came from a multinational cohort (17 centers in the UK, Czech Republic, Romania, Russia and Serbia; the CAGEKID/ICGC effort) and were split into a Discovery cohort (n=469) and a Validation cohort (n=474) ([ccrcc_sjuh_2023](../datasets/ccrcc_sjuh_2023.md)). No single gene was reproducibly prognostic after multiple-testing correction. A simple count-based classifier did work. Among [VHL](../genes/VHL.md)-mutant tumors, it compares [VHL](../genes/VHL.md) alone (VHL+0) with VHL plus 1, 2 or ≥3 more mutated genes, and it separated patients into groups with very different disease-free survival (DFS). In Discovery, 5-year DFS was 90.8%, 80.1%, 68.2% and 50.7% for VHL+0, +1, +2 and +≥3. Validation showed the same order (90.4%, 84.7%, 73.7%, 61.5%). The effect held after accounting for stage, age and tumor mutational burden (TMB). Among patients clinically eligible for adjuvant therapy, the 29% with VHL+0 tumors had 5-year DFS of 79.3% and might be spared adjuvant immune checkpoint inhibitor treatment such as [pembrolizumab](../drugs/pembrolizumab.md). The 32% with VHL+2 or VHL+≥3 tumors had 5-year DFS of only 45.6% and 35.3%, and the authors say they should be prioritized for adjuvant treatment [PMID:36815791](../papers/36815791.md).

## Cohort & data

- **Patients:** 943 [CCRCC](../cancer_types/CCRCC.md) patients who had nephrectomy for suspected renal cancer between March 1998 and February 2014 at 17 centers in the UK, Czech Republic, Romania, Russia and Serbia. All samples had panel pathology review [PMID:36815791](../papers/36815791.md).
- **Dataset:** [ccrcc_sjuh_2023](../datasets/ccrcc_sjuh_2023.md), the cBioPortal study backed by this paper (943 samples; reads aligned to GRCh37). Clinical data and somatic mutation lists for the 12 genes are in Supplementary Tables S1–S2. Raw data are in the European Genome-phenome Archive (EGAS00001000083 for C1/C2; EGAS00001007004 for C3) [PMID:36815791](../papers/36815791.md).
- **Three sequencing cohorts** [PMID:36815791](../papers/36815791.md):
  - **C1 (n=93):** tumor–germline pairs profiled by [whole-genome sequencing](../methods/whole-genome-seq.md), previously reported in the CAGEKID descriptive study.
  - **C2 (n=376):** 24 profiled by [whole-exome sequencing](../methods/whole-exome-seq.md) and 352 by a [targeted](../methods/targeted-dna-seq.md) 42-gene panel (Illumina Nextera Rapid Capture; HiSeq 2500, 2×100; mean coverage 296X).
  - **C3 (n=474):** targeted 12-gene RCC-focused panel (Lucigen AmpFree library prep with IDT xGen dual-index UMI adapters; NovaSeq 6000, 2×150; mean coverage 1475X).
  - **Discovery = C1 + C2 (n=469). Validation = C3 (n=474).** The two cohorts had no significant differences in per-gene mutation rates, stage, grade or age.
- **The 12 analyzed genes:** [ATM](../genes/ATM.md), [ATP9B](../genes/ATP9B.md), [BAP1](../genes/BAP1.md), [COL11A1](../genes/COL11A1.md), [DMD](../genes/DMD.md), [KDM5C](../genes/KDM5C.md), [PBRM1](../genes/PBRM1.md), [PTK7](../genes/PTK7.md), [SETD2](../genes/SETD2.md), [TP53](../genes/TP53.md), [TRRAP](../genes/TRRAP.md) and [VHL](../genes/VHL.md). The main analysis used only these 12 genes, whatever the platform [PMID:36815791](../papers/36815791.md).
- **Tissue:** a single frozen block for 86% of cases, requiring at least 70% viable tumor cells (C1/C2) or at least 50% (C3). The rest used FFPE tissue (three 1-mm punches from one block). Mutation rates were similar for frozen and FFPE tissue [PMID:36815791](../papers/36815791.md).
- **Variant calling** [PMID:36815791](../papers/36815791.md):
  - Pipeline: GenPipes DNA-Seq high-coverage pipeline, with Trimmomatic trimming.
  - Alignment: [bwa](../methods/bwa.md)-mem to GRCh37, then [GATK](../methods/gatk.md)/Picard refinement. fgbio built UMI consensus reads for C3.
  - Calling: [VarScan2](../methods/varscan.md) for somatic and germline calls; [VarDict](../methods/vardict.md) re-called indels and complex variants.
  - Annotation: snpEff and Gemini.
  - Filters: high or moderate predicted impact, tumor VAF of at least 5%, and 1000 Genomes frequency below 0.01%. Calls were then checked manually in [IGV](../methods/integrative-genomics-viewer.md).
- **Clinical profile:** median age 61 (23–86); 61.5% male. 806 patients (85%) had stage I–III disease. Median follow-up was 5.7 years (IQR 3.8–7.3), with 160 recurrences (17%) and 192 cancer-specific deaths (20%) [PMID:36815791](../papers/36815791.md).
- **Endpoints and statistics** [PMID:36815791](../papers/36815791.md):
  - Endpoints: DFS, RCC-specific survival and non-RCC-related survival.
  - Main analyses: [Kaplan–Meier](../methods/kaplan-meier.md) curves and multivariable [Cox proportional hazards](../methods/cox-proportional-hazards.md) models, stratified by TNM stage and adjusted for age.
  - Competing risks: cumulative incidence (cmprsk) and cause-specific hazards models.
- **External replication:** 247 VHL-mutated ccRCCs from TCGA (ref. 12, Ricketts et al., Cell Rep 2018). The paper does not name a specific cBioPortal study ID for this data. TCGA data were also used for the TMB analyses [PMID:36815791](../papers/36815791.md).

## Key findings

- **Mutation frequencies (n=943):** [VHL](../genes/VHL.md) 76%, [PBRM1](../genes/PBRM1.md) 39%, [SETD2](../genes/SETD2.md) 18%, [BAP1](../genes/BAP1.md) 14%, [KDM5C](../genes/KDM5C.md) 8%.
  - The VHL rate is well above earlier reports; TCGA reported 52.3% ([PMID:23792563](../papers/23792563.md)).
  - The authors attribute the higher rate to deep coverage, sensitive indel detection and exclusion of non-clear-cell [RCC](../cancer_types/RCC.md) [PMID:36815791](../papers/36815791.md).
- **Single genes were not reproducibly prognostic** [PMID:36815791](../papers/36815791.md):
  - In stage-stratified, age-adjusted multivariable models, [BAP1](../genes/BAP1.md) mutation was associated with DFS in Discovery (q=0.02).
  - No gene stayed significant in both cohorts after multiple-testing correction.
  - In cause-specific hazards models, no gene was significantly associated with RCC-specific survival in either cohort.
- **Genomically defined groups** among 720 VHL-mutant tumors ([ccrcc-12-gene-classifier](../methods/ccrcc-12-gene-classifier.md)) [PMID:36815791](../papers/36815791.md):
  - VHL+0: n=245 (26%)
  - VHL+1: n=284 (30%)
  - VHL+2: n=148 (16%)
  - VHL+≥3: n=43 (5%)
  - VHL wild-type tumors (n=218; 23%) were excluded because they may have different biological drivers.
- **Groups differ clinicopathologically** (Kruskal–Wallis; P<0.001 for age, grade, stage and sarcomatoid/rhabdoid change; P=0.002 for tumor size) [PMID:36815791](../papers/36815791.md):
  - VHL+0 tumors were mostly stage I–II (175; 72%) and low grade (154; 63%), and none of 245 had sarcomatoid or rhabdoid change.
  - Half of VHL+2 and VHL+≥3 tumors (96; 50%) were stage III/IV or high grade, and 9.9% (19/191) had sarcomatoid or rhabdoid change.
  - VHL+1 tumors were mixed: 63% low stage, 31% high stage, and 4.2% (12/284) sarcomatoid or rhabdoid.
  - Median tumor size rose from 50 mm (VHL+0) to 65 mm (VHL+≥3), and median age from 60 to 64 years.
- **DFS falls stepwise with each extra mutated gene (5-year DFS)** [PMID:36815791](../papers/36815791.md):
  - Discovery: VHL+0 90.8% (95% CI 85–97%), VHL+1 80.1% (73–88%), VHL+2 68.2% (57–82%), VHL+≥3 50.7% (32–80%).
  - Validation: VHL+0 90.4%, VHL+1 84.7%, VHL+2 73.7%, VHL+≥3 61.5%.
- **Recurrence hazard ratios (stage-stratified, age-adjusted)** [PMID:36815791](../papers/36815791.md):
  - VHL+2: Discovery HR 4.3 (1.8–10.2), P=0.000862; Validation HR 2.5 (1.1–5.7), P=0.02883.
  - VHL+≥3: Discovery HR 6.7 (2.4–18.3), P=0.000212; Validation HR 4.6 (1.5–13.5), P=0.00615.
  - The result replicated in 247 VHL-mutated TCGA ccRCCs (Supplementary Fig. S1).
- **RCC-specific death (competing risks)** [PMID:36815791](../papers/36815791.md):
  - Risk of RCC-related death rose with the number of mutated genes.
  - For VHL+0 patients, the cumulative incidence of RCC death and of death from other causes were almost identical.
  - Cause-specific hazards: VHL+2 HR 3.4 (1.6–7.2), P=0.00190; VHL+≥3 HR 4.1 (1.6–10.5), P=0.00286. The same pattern appeared in Validation.
  - There was no such trend for non-RCC death.
- **BAP1/PBRM1 co-mutation (VHL-mutant patients, DFS)** [PMID:36815791](../papers/36815791.md):
  - PBRM1-mutant: HR 1.72 (1.09–2.72); the paper prints P=0.20.
  - BAP1-mutant: HR 2.59 (1.51–4.44); P=0.0006.
  - BAP1 and [PBRM1](../genes/PBRM1.md) co-mutated: HR 7.29 (3.03–17.54); P<0.0001, the worst outcome.
- **The classifier adds to known co-mutation markers** [PMID:36815791](../papers/36815791.md):
  - Compared with VHL+0, BAP1/PBRM1-co-mutant VHL+2 tumors had HR 12.08 (95% CI printed as 3.95–136.95), P<0.0001.
  - The other VHL+2 tumors still did significantly worse: HR 2.74 (1.49–5.01), P=0.001.
  - BAP1/PBRM1 co-occurrence explains only 7% (8/115) of VHL+2 cases, so the classifier stratifies more patients.
  - PBRM1/SETD2 co-mutation gave comparable results (Supplementary Table S9).
- **Adjuvant-eligible patients** (pT2 grade 3–4; pT3/pT4 any grade; any pT N+) [PMID:36815791](../papers/36815791.md):
  - 5-year DFS was 63.6% (57–71%) in the 196 eligible patients, versus 90.6% (88–94%) in the 397 ineligible patients.
  - Within the eligible group, 5-year DFS by classifier group:

    | Group | n (%) | 5-year DFS (95% CI) |
    | :-- | :-- | :-- |
    | VHL+0 | 56 (29%) | 79.3% (69–91%) |
    | VHL+1 | 77 (39%) | 69.4% (60–81%) |
    | VHL+2 | 46 (23%) | 45.6% (33–63%) |
    | VHL+≥3 | 17 (9%) | 35.3% (19–67%) |

  - VHL+2 and VHL+≥3 did significantly worse than VHL+0 (P=0.00055 and P<0.0001).
- **Adds to the [Leibovich score](../methods/leibovich-score.md)** (available for 181/196 eligible patients) [PMID:36815791](../papers/36815791.md):
  - High-risk Leibovich patients had worse DFS than intermediate-risk patients (P<0.001).
  - Within the intermediate-risk group, 5-year DFS was 89% (80–99%) for VHL+0, 66% (47–92%) for VHL+2 (P=0.02) and 25% (5–100%) for VHL+≥3 (P<0.0001; only 4 patients).
- **Stage I disease (exploratory)** [PMID:36815791](../papers/36815791.md):
  - Stage I VHL+0 patients (140/365; 38%): 5-year DFS 96% (93–99%).
  - Stage I VHL+≥3 patients (16/365; 4%): 78% (59–100%), P=0.01.
- **Independent of TMB** [PMID:36815791](../papers/36815791.md):
  - In the WGS cohort (C1), TMB differed only between VHL+0 and the other groups, not among VHL+1, +2 and +≥3.
  - In TCGA, the only difference was a marginally significant one between VHL+0 and VHL+2.
  - TMB alone was not associated with DFS.
  - With TMB as a covariate, VHL+2 and VHL+≥3 still had significantly worse DFS: C1 P=0.025 and P=0.043; TCGA P=0.002 and P=0.046.

## Genes & alterations

- [VHL](../genes/VHL.md): somatic mutations in 76% of tumors, the anchor of the classifier. Tumors with a VHL mutation alone (VHL+0) had the best outcomes: 5-year DFS about 90% overall and 96% in stage I. For VHL+0 patients, the risk of dying from RCC was about the same as the risk of dying from other causes. VHL wild-type tumors (23%) were not classified [PMID:36815791](../papers/36815791.md).
- [PBRM1](../genes/PBRM1.md): mutated in 39%. Within VHL-mutant tumors, PBRM1 mutation was linked to worse DFS (HR 1.72). Co-mutation with BAP1 (HR 7.29) or with [SETD2](../genes/SETD2.md) marked the worst-outcome subsets. However, most VHL+2 tumors with worse outcomes did not carry either co-mutation pair [PMID:36815791](../papers/36815791.md).
- [SETD2](../genes/SETD2.md): mutated in 18%. It was not independently prognostic here. By contrast, a pooled 1,049-patient analysis cited by the authors (Manley et al., 2017) found SETD2 marginally significant for recurrence-free survival. PBRM1/SETD2 co-mutation behaved like BAP1/PBRM1 co-mutation [PMID:36815791](../papers/36815791.md).
- [BAP1](../genes/BAP1.md): mutated in 14%. It was the only gene significant for DFS in the Discovery multivariable model (q=0.02), but this did not replicate in Validation. Among VHL-mutant tumors, BAP1 mutation gave HR 2.59 for DFS [PMID:36815791](../papers/36815791.md).
- [KDM5C](../genes/KDM5C.md): mutated in 8%. It is part of the classifier gene set and was chosen for its known role in ccRCC [PMID:36815791](../papers/36815791.md).
- [TP53](../genes/TP53.md): included for its previously reported role or clinical associations in ccRCC. Its individual frequency is not given in the main text [PMID:36815791](../papers/36815791.md).
- [ATM](../genes/ATM.md), [ATP9B](../genes/ATP9B.md), [COL11A1](../genes/COL11A1.md), [DMD](../genes/DMD.md), [PTK7](../genes/PTK7.md), [TRRAP](../genes/TRRAP.md): lower-frequency genes added because of preliminary links to outcome or clinical features in C1/C2. The authors say their individual significance "could not be robustly established" even in this large cohort. Each one counts toward the "+n" tally [PMID:36815791](../papers/36815791.md).

## Clinical implications

- **Choosing patients for adjuvant therapy** [PMID:36815791](../papers/36815791.md):
  - Current risk estimates for resected RCC rely on pathology alone. The authors cite [pembrolizumab](../drugs/pembrolizumab.md) approval based on KEYNOTE-564, where 68% of placebo patients were still recurrence-free at 2 years. They take this as evidence that current risk models over-treat.
  - Among clinically eligible patients, VHL+0 (29%; 5-year DFS 79.3%) marks a group that could be spared adjuvant therapy.
  - VHL+2 and VHL+≥3 (32%; 5-year DFS 45.6% and 35.3%) mark a group to prioritize.
- **Harms and cost of adjuvant ICIs** [PMID:36815791](../papers/36815791.md):
  - Adjuvant ICIs are expensive and can cause severe immune-mediated adverse events.
  - Three other adjuvant ICI trials were negative (refs. 7–9): IMmotion010 with [atezolizumab](../drugs/atezolizumab.md), CheckMate 914 with [nivolumab](../drugs/nivolumab.md) plus [ipilimumab](../drugs/ipilimumab.md), and PROSPER with perioperative [nivolumab](../drugs/nivolumab.md).
  - Both points strengthen the case for molecular risk refinement.
- **Adds prognostic information beyond clinical tools:** the classifier sub-stratified patients within Leibovich intermediate-risk disease and within stage I. It was independent of stage, grade, age and TMB [PMID:36815791](../papers/36815791.md).
- **Practical to use:** the groups come from a small gene panel sequenced from a single tumor region, so the authors describe the test as "readily applied to the clinic" [PMID:36815791](../papers/36815791.md).
- **Trial design:** the authors say the findings affect the design, cost and success of future adjuvant RCC trials. They do not show that genomic high-risk patients benefit more from adjuvant ICIs [PMID:36815791](../papers/36815791.md).

## Limitations & open questions

- **Untested for benefit:** it is unknown whether adjuvant ICIs help VHL+2 or VHL+≥3 patients; the authors say this needs study. The cohort (1998–2014) was recruited before adjuvant ICIs, so this is prognostic evidence only, not predictive [PMID:36815791](../papers/36815791.md).
- **Not all tumors covered:** VHL wild-type tumors (23%) are excluded, so about a quarter of patients cannot be classified [PMID:36815791](../papers/36815791.md).
  - The classifier uses mutations only. TCGA reported epigenetic VHL silencing in about 7% of ccRCC, mutually exclusive with VHL mutation ([PMID:23792563](../papers/23792563.md)).
  - Some "VHL [WT](../cancer_types/WT.md)" tumors here may therefore still have VHL inactivated.
- **Gene panel is not final:** apart from [COL11A1](../genes/COL11A1.md), no PI3K/AKT/mTOR pathway genes were included. Copy-number alterations were not analyzed. Lower-frequency genes could not be individually validated [PMID:36815791](../papers/36815791.md).
- **Single-region sequencing:** the effect of spatial intratumor heterogeneity is poorly understood. Such heterogeneity is well characterized in ccRCC, e.g. [PMID:24487277](../papers/24487277.md). Multi-region sequencing would find more mutations, but the clinical weight of small subclonal drivers is unknown [PMID:36815791](../papers/36815791.md).
- **Mixed assays across cohorts:** the cohorts used different assays (WGS, WES, a 42-gene panel, a 12-gene UMI panel), different tumor-content thresholds (70% vs 50%) and different depths (296X vs 1475X). The authors report similar mutation rates across cohorts [PMID:36815791](../papers/36815791.md).
- **Small subgroups:** VHL+≥3 has n=43 overall, 17 among adjuvant-eligible patients, 16 among stage I patients and 4 in the Leibovich intermediate-risk group. The authors call the stage I finding exploratory [PMID:36815791](../papers/36815791.md).
- **Validation cohort choice:** TCGA's VHL mutation rate (52.3%) is lower than this cohort's. The authors say an ideal validation cohort would be larger and have a similarly high VHL detection rate [PMID:36815791](../papers/36815791.md).
- **Internal numeric inconsistencies (recorded, not resolved):**
  - VHL-mutant count: 720 in Results versus 718 (76%) in Discussion.
  - Share of cases classified: 76% in Discussion versus "almost 80%" in the Translational Relevance section.
  - PBRM1 DFS result: HR 1.72 (95% CI 1.09–2.72) is printed with P=0.20, even though the CI excludes 1.
  - VHL+2 BAP1/PBRM1 result: the 95% CI is printed as 3.95–136.95 around HR 12.08.
- **Link to other work:** the groups roughly match the TRACERx Renal evolutionary subtypes ("VHL monodriver", "multiple clonal drivers", "VHL WT"; Turajlic et al., Cell 2018; not in this corpus). This study adds outcome data from a much larger single-region cohort. Prospective evaluation is still needed [PMID:36815791](../papers/36815791.md).
