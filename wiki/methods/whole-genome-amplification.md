# Whole-genome amplification (WGA)

## Overview

Whole-genome amplification is a library-preparation technique that non-specifically amplifies limited-input or single-cell genomic DNA to yield enough material for downstream sequencing.

## Used by

- Ten single-cell-derived colony samples underwent WGA prior to matched tumor/constitutional [whole-exome sequencing](../methods/whole-exome-seq.md), with 18 technical replicates run to assess WGA-associated sequencing artifacts [PMID:24030381](../papers/24030381.md)

## Notes

- Used here as an input-amplification step ahead of WES on low-DNA single-cell colonies, not as a standalone variant-calling method.
