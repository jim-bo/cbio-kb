# sotigalimab

## Overview

An agonist monoclonal antibody targeting CD40, intended to activate antigen-presenting
cells and prime anti-tumor T cell responses; tested in combination with chemotherapy
and/or PD-1 blockade in metastatic pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma.

## Evidence in the corpus

- In the PRINCE trial (NCT03214250) of first-line metastatic pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma, sotigalimab (anti-CD40 agonist, RP2D 0.3 mg/kg) plus gemcitabine/nab-paclitaxel did not meet its primary 1-year OS endpoint (48.1% vs. 35% historical control, P=0.062, n=36), nor did the triple combination with [nivolumab](../drugs/nivolumab.md) (41.3%, P=0.233, n=35), while nivolumab plus chemotherapy did meet the endpoint (57.7%, P=0.006, n=34). [PMID:35662283](../papers/35662283.md)
- Cytokine release syndrome occurred more often on sotigalimab-containing arms than on nivolumab/chemo alone: 9/37 (24%) with sotiga/chemo and 12/35 (34%) with sotiga/nivo/chemo, versus 0% with nivo/chemo (no grade 4/5 events). [PMID:35662283](../papers/35662283.md)
- Higher pre-treatment circulating CD1c+CD141+ cross-presenting dendritic cells and pre-treatment PD-1+Tbet+ and Tbet+Eomes+ non-naive [CD4](../genes/CD4.md) T cells were associated with longer OS specifically on the sotiga/chemo arm. [PMID:35662283](../papers/35662283.md)
