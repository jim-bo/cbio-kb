# CTNND1

## Overview

CTNND1 encodes p120-catenin (delta-1-catenin), a member of the Armadillo repeat protein family that stabilizes cadherin complexes at adherens junctions and regulates cell–cell adhesion and motility. Germline variants have been proposed as a predisposition gene for early-onset gastric cancer in familial non-hereditary gastric cancer (FNHGC) contexts.

## Alterations observed in the corpus

- Newly proposed germline predisposition gene for early-onset gastric cancer (Herrera-Pariente et al. 2024); disrupts cell–cell interactions, potentiates motility and metastasis; candidate explanation for a subset of FNHGC [PMID:41097736](../papers/41097736.md)
- CTNND1 (p120-catenin) mutated in ~4% of colorectal tumors as a high-confidence significantly mutated gene. [PMID:35487942](../papers/35487942.md)
- A novel CTNND1-DENND6A gene fusion removing the armadillo-repeat exons was identified by WGS in a CDH1-wild-type invasive lobular carcinoma case and proposed as the driver of the lobular phenotype. [PMID:38347189](../papers/38347189.md)

## Cancer types (linked)
