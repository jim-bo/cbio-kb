# Renal Cell Carcinoma (St. James, Clin Cancer Res 2023)

## Overview

A 943-patient clear-cell renal cell carcinoma cohort assembled from three sequencing sub-cohorts (C1/C2/C3) of patients who had nephrectomy between March 1998 and February 2014 at 17 centers across the UK, Czech Republic, Romania, Russia and Serbia, used to test survival associations for 12 candidate driver genes. cBioPortal study record: 943 samples, hg19 (GRCh37). [PMID:36815791](../papers/36815791.md)

## Composition

- Cancer type: [CCRCC](../cancer_types/CCRCC.md). Median age 61 (23–86); 61.5% male; 85% stage I–III disease; median follow-up 5.7 years. [PMID:36815791](../papers/36815791.md)
- C1 (n=93): tumor-germline pairs, previously reported in the CAGEKID descriptive study. C2 (n=376): 24 WES + 352 targeted 42-gene panel. C3 (n=474): targeted 12-gene RCC panel (validation set). Discovery = C1+C2 (n=469); validation = C3 (n=474). [PMID:36815791](../papers/36815791.md)
- Analysis restricted to 12 genes across all platforms: [ATM](../genes/ATM.md), [ATP9B](../genes/ATP9B.md), [BAP1](../genes/BAP1.md), [COL11A1](../genes/COL11A1.md), [DMD](../genes/DMD.md), [KDM5C](../genes/KDM5C.md), [PBRM1](../genes/PBRM1.md), [PTK7](../genes/PTK7.md), [SETD2](../genes/SETD2.md), [TP53](../genes/TP53.md), [TRRAP](../genes/TRRAP.md), [VHL](../genes/VHL.md). [PMID:36815791](../papers/36815791.md)
