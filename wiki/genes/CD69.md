# CD69

## Overview

CD69 is an early lymphocyte activation marker expressed on T cells and NK cells. In the corpus it is used as part of an innate/NK-cell transcript panel that increases with PD-1 blockade.

## Alterations observed in the corpus

- CD69 was among a set of innate and NK-cell function genes (with KLRB1, KLRD1, KLRG1, NKG7) that increased significantly in tumor on nivolumab treatment; the authors hypothesize NK-cell engagement and suggest combining with NK-directed agents such as [lirilumab](../drugs/lirilumab.md) [PMID:27169994](../papers/27169994.md).
