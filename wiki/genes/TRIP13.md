# TRIP13

## Overview

TRIP13 (Thyroid Hormone Receptor Interactor 13) is an AAA-ATPase involved in the spindle assembly checkpoint and DNA repair, and is overexpressed in several cancers. In the cbio-kb corpus it appears among genes differentially expressed specifically in TERT-altered pheochromocytoma/paraganglioma.

## Alterations observed in the corpus

- TRIP13 was among the few genes differentially expressed specifically in TERT-altered pheochromocytoma/paraganglioma tumors [PMID:40097403](../papers/40097403.md).

## Cancer types (linked)

- [PHC](../cancer_types/PHC.md) / [PGNG](../cancer_types/PGNG.md) — TRIP13 (with [IRX3](../genes/IRX3.md)) was specifically differentially expressed in TERT-altered tumors within a multi-omic SDHB-deficient [PCPG](../cancer_types/PCPG.md) cohort [PMID:40097403](../papers/40097403.md).

## Co-occurrence and mutual exclusivity

- Co-varies with [IRX3](../genes/IRX3.md) specifically in the context of [TERT](../genes/TERT.md) alteration [PMID:40097403](../papers/40097403.md).

## Therapeutic relevance

- Not yet characterized in the corpus.

## Open questions

- Whether TRIP13 upregulation is mechanistically linked to [TERT](../genes/TERT.md) alteration (e.g., telomere maintenance) or is a downstream bystander effect is unresolved in the source text [PMID:40097403](../papers/40097403.md).
