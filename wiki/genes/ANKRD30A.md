# ANKRD30A

## Overview

ANKRD30A (ankyrin repeat domain 30A, also known as NY-BR-1) is a breast-differentiation antigen with highly restricted normal expression in breast and testis tissue, making it a long-studied candidate breast-cancer-associated antigen for cancer immunotherapy. Its tumor expression has been explored as a correlate of immune recognition and treatment response in breast cancer, including triple-negative breast cancer treated with checkpoint inhibitors.

## Alterations observed in the corpus

- Higher pretreatment tumor expression, along with SYCE1 and TUBB2B, in responders (CR+PR) versus non-responders (SD+PD) to pembrolizumab-based therapy in a phase II trial of metastatic triple-negative breast cancer [PMID:35121644](../papers/35121644.md)

## Cancer types (linked)

- [BRCA](../cancer_types/BRCA.md) (triple-negative breast cancer): higher pretreatment expression associated with response to low-dose cyclophosphamide + pembrolizumab [PMID:35121644](../papers/35121644.md).

## Co-occurrence and mutual exclusivity

- Co-elevated with SYCE1 and TUBB2B in pretreatment tumors of responders [PMID:35121644](../papers/35121644.md).

## Therapeutic relevance

- Candidate predictive biomarker of response to pembrolizumab + low-dose cyclophosphamide priming in metastatic triple-negative breast cancer; hypothesis-generating in a single 40-patient trial [PMID:35121644](../papers/35121644.md).

## Open questions

- Whether ANKRD30A expression is a specific predictor of response or a proxy for a broader immune-active tumor state; not yet validated in an independent cohort [PMID:35121644](../papers/35121644.md).
