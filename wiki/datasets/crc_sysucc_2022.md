# Colorectal Cancer- ChangKang Project (SYSUCC, Nat Commun 2022)

## Overview

A prospective, single-center Chinese colorectal cancer (CRC) cohort of 1,015 patients resected at SYSUCC as part of the ChangKang Project (launched 2017), tumor and matched nonneoplastic tissue sequenced with an extended whole-exome panel (HapOnco WESplus). cBioPortal study record: 1,015 samples, hg19. [PMID:35487942](../papers/35487942.md)

## Composition

- 1,015 patients (age 18–80 at resection); median age at diagnosis 59; 404 female (39.8%), 611 male. [PMID:35487942](../papers/35487942.md)
- Cancer types: [COADREAD](../cancer_types/COADREAD.md) comprising [COAD](../cancer_types/COAD.md) 729 (71.8%) and [READ](../cancer_types/READ.md) 286 (28.2%). [PMID:35487942](../papers/35487942.md)
- AJCC stage I 91 (9.0%), II 384 (37.8%), III 287 (28.3%), IV 252 (24.8%); median follow-up 68.8 months. Versus TCGA, this cohort had more metastatic disease, more rectal cancer, more males, more left-sided primaries, and younger age of onset (all P<0.001). [PMID:35487942](../papers/35487942.md)

## Assays / panels (linked)

- [Whole-exome sequencing](../methods/whole-exome-seq.md) with HapOnco WESplus (HaploX), an extended capture also covering noncoding hotspots, fusions, three viral genomes (HBV/HPV/EBV) and the mitochondrial genome; Illumina NovaSeq 6000, aligned to hg19; median depth 219x tumor / 223x normal. [PMID:35487942](../papers/35487942.md)
