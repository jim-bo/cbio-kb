# Methylation-Specific PCR

## Overview

Methylation-specific PCR (MSP) uses bisulfite-converted DNA and primer pairs designed to selectively amplify methylated versus unmethylated alleles at a target locus, providing a targeted, low-cost assay for promoter methylation status.

## Used by

- Used to assess [CDH1](../genes/CDH1.md) promoter methylation status in invasive lobular breast carcinomas lacking CDH1 genetic alterations [PMID:38347189](../papers/38347189.md).
