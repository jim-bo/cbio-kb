# PROX1

## Overview

PROX1 is a homeobox transcription factor identified as a central regulator of fetal-like reprogramming and lineage plasticity during colorectal cancer metastasis.

## Alterations observed in the corpus

- Gene-expression finding (not somatic mutation): PROX1 was the transcription factor most tightly co-induced with the fetal progenitor state and most strongly induced by [irinotecan](../drugs/irinotecan.md) in colorectal cancer organoids; it was expressed in poorly differentiated CDX2-low cells at the primary-tumor invasion front and acts as a context-dependent repressor of non-canonical (squamous/neuroendocrine) differentiation [PMID:39478232](../papers/39478232.md).

## Cancer types (linked)

- [COADREAD](../cancer_types/COADREAD.md): PROX1 supports ISC-like organoid-initiation capacity in more-canonical colorectal cancer cell states; both this function and its differentiation-repressor activity are progressively lost as tumors become more non-canonical/metastatic. PROX1 knockdown alone does not drive metastatic outgrowth of otherwise-canonical organoids [PMID:39478232](../papers/39478232.md).
