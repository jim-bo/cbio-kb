# CXCL2

## Overview

CXCL2 is a myeloid-associated chemokine included in composite immune gene signatures used to stratify tumors by inflammatory phenotype and predict response to anti-angiogenic and checkpoint-blockade therapy.

## Alterations observed in the corpus

- CXCL2 is part of a myeloid inflammation gene signature (with [IL6](../genes/IL6.md), [CXCL1](../genes/CXCL1.md), [CXCL3](../genes/CXCL3.md), [CXCL8](../genes/CXCL8.md), [PTGS2](../genes/PTGS2.md)); MyeloidHigh renal cell carcinoma tumors had worse PFS on [atezolizumab](../drugs/atezolizumab.md) monotherapy, proposed as innate resistance overcome by [bevacizumab](../drugs/bevacizumab.md).
