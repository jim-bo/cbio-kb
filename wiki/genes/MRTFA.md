# MRTFA

## Overview

MRTFA (myocardin-related transcription factor A, formerly MKL1) is a transcriptional coactivator of serum response factor. As an [RBM15](../genes/RBM15.md)::MRTFA fusion partner it defines a distinct molecular category of pediatric acute myeloid leukemia.

## Alterations observed in the corpus

- RBM15::MRTFA fusion defines a distinct pediatric [AML](../cancer_types/AML.md) molecular category (10 of 887 cases) [PMID:38212634](../papers/38212634.md).

## Cancer types (linked)

- **[AML](../cancer_types/AML.md)** — RBM15::MRTFA fusion-positive cases form a small, molecularly distinct pediatric AML subgroup [PMID:38212634](../papers/38212634.md).
