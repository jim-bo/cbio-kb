# zanidatamab

## Overview

A biparatopic bispecific antibody that binds two distinct, non-overlapping HER2
epitopes.

## Evidence in the corpus

- In a metastatic gallbladder cancer case with HER2 IHC 3+ and ERBB2 amplification (19.3-fold) that had progressed on FOLFOX and gemcitabine/cisplatin, zanidatamab produced a partial response (100% tumor shrinkage by RECIST 1.1, CA19-9 fell from 11,016 to 19) lasting 24 months. [PMID:42360806](../papers/42360806.md)
- In a second metastatic gallbladder cancer case (HER2 IHC 3+, ERBB2 amplification 17.3-fold, ERBB2 R678Q), zanidatamab gave a partial response (84% RECIST reduction) lasting 14 months; at progression, HER2 PET remained avid and biopsy confirmed retained ERBB2 amplification (10-fold), after which the patient was switched to trastuzumab deruxtecan. [PMID:42360806](../papers/42360806.md)
