Clinical activity and molecular correlates of response to atezolizumab alone or in combination with bevacizumab versus sunitinib in renal cell carcinoma

TL;DR

IMmotion150 was a randomized, open-label phase 2 trial in 305 patients with treatment-naive metastatic renal cell carcinoma (mRCC; clear cell and/or sarcomatoid histology). Patients were randomized 1:1:1 to atezolizumab (anti-PD-L1) plus bevacizumab (anti-VEGF), atezolizumab alone, or sunitinib. The trial was designed to estimate effect sizes, not to test for significance. In the intent-to-treat population, neither atezolizumab arm improved progression-free survival (PFS) over sunitinib (HR 1.00 and 1.19). In patients with PD-L1 on ≥1% of tumor-infiltrating immune cells (IC), atezolizumab + bevacizumab showed a favorable trend (median PFS 14.7 vs. 7.8 months; HR 0.64, 95% CI 0.38–1.08). The exploratory biomarker analyses used RNA-seq of 263 tumors and whole-exome sequencing of 201 tumors. Tumor mutation burden (TMB) and neoantigen burden did not predict outcome. Three gene-expression signatures separated the arms instead: Angio (angiogenesis), Teff (T-effector/IFN-γ) and myeloid inflammation. Sunitinib worked best in AngioHigh and PBRM1-mutant tumors, and atezolizumab + bevacizumab beat sunitinib in TeffHigh tumors. In TeffHighMyeloidHigh tumors, atezolizumab + bevacizumab outperformed atezolizumab alone, suggesting that adding bevacizumab may overcome myeloid-inflammation-associated resistance. PMID:29867230

Cohort & data

  • Trial: IMmotion150, a prospectively designed, multicenter (96 institutions), randomized, open-label phase 2 trial. Enrollment ran from 8 January 2014 to 16 March 2015. Clinical cutoff was 17 October 2016, with a median survival follow-up of 20.7 months. PMID:29867230
  • Population: Adults (≥18 years, Karnofsky performance score ≥70) with unresectable advanced or metastatic renal cell carcinoma with a clear cell and/or sarcomatoid component and no prior systemic therapy. Predominant clear cell histology (CCRCC) was present in 92–96% of patients per arm, and a sarcomatoid component in 14–15%. PMID:29867230
  • Arms (ITT N = 305): Atezolizumab 1,200 mg IV + bevacizumab 15 mg/kg every 3 weeks (n = 101); atezolizumab 1,200 mg IV every 3 weeks (n = 103); sunitinib 50 mg/day orally, 4 weeks on / 2 weeks off (n = 101). The safety population was 304 because one sunitinib patient withdrew before dosing. Patients progressing on atezolizumab or sunitinib could cross over to the combination in some regions (not in Europe). PMID:29867230
  • Stratification: Memorial Sloan Kettering Cancer Center (MSKCC) risk, prior nephrectomy, and PD-L1 IC status (≥5% vs. <5%) by the SP142 IHC assay (Ventana), scored centrally. PD-L1 positivity was later redefined as ≥1% IC, and PFS in PD-L1+ patients became a co-primary endpoint. PD-L1+ rates were 59% (sunitinib), 52% (atezolizumab) and 50% (atezolizumab + bevacizumab). PMID:29867230
  • Baseline: Median age 61–62 years; 73–78% male; prior nephrectomy 86–87%. MSKCC risk was favorable in 21–30%, intermediate in 61–69% and poor in 8–10% of patients per arm. PMID:29867230
  • Transcriptomics: Whole-transcriptome RNA-seq (Illumina TruSeq RNA Access) was run on 263 pretreatment tumors. Reads were aligned to NCBI Build 38 with GSNAP, and gene counts were edgeR-normalized and voom-transformed. PMID:29867230
  • Exomes: Tumor/PBMC whole-exome sequencing (Agilent SureSelect v5, 51 Mb; HiSeq 2500) was generated for 208 patients, and 201 tumors were evaluable for mutation analyses. Somatic calls were the union of LoFreq and Strelka (indel classification used Strelka calls only), with GATK indel realignment and VEP annotation. Single-gene mutant status required a VAF of ≥5%. PMID:29867230
  • Neoantigens: Expressed mutations were confirmed in RNA-seq. HLA typing used Polysolver on PBMC exomes, and 8–11-mer neoepitope binding was predicted with NetMHCcons. PMID:29867230
  • IHC: Beyond PD-L1 (SP142), immunohistochemistry was used for CD31 (vascular density) and CD8 T-cell infiltration. PMID:29867230
  • Statistics: PFS was estimated by Kaplan–Meier and compared with a stratified Cox proportional hazards model. Biomarker associations used Cox regression, and P values were descriptive and not adjusted for multiplicity. PMID:29867230
  • Data availability: Anonymized genetic data from the 163 consenting patients are deposited at EGA (EGAS00001002928). PMID:29867230
  • cBioPortal: rcc_iatlas_immotion150_2018 is an iAtlas-harmonized reprocessing of the IMmotion150 sequencing data. cBioPortal study metadata lists 263 samples on reference genome hg38.

Key findings

Primary efficacy (independent review facility-assessed)

  • ITT PFS: Median PFS was 11.7 months (95% CI 8.4–17.3) with atezolizumab + bevacizumab and 8.4 months (7.0–14.0) with sunitinib, HR 1.00 (0.69–1.45). With atezolizumab monotherapy it was 6.1 months (5.4–13.6), HR 1.19 (0.82–1.71) vs. sunitinib. PFS events were 67/101, 61/103 and 59/101. PMID:29867230
  • PD-L1+ (≥1% IC) PFS: Median PFS was 14.7 months (8.2–25.1) with atezolizumab + bevacizumab vs. 7.8 months (3.8–10.8) with sunitinib, HR 0.64 (0.38–1.08). With atezolizumab monotherapy it was 5.5 months (3.0–13.9), HR 1.03 (0.63–1.67). Arm sizes were n = 50, 54 and 60. PMID:29867230
  • ORR, ITT:
    • Atezolizumab + bevacizumab: 32% (7% CR, 25% PR).
    • Atezolizumab: 25% (11% CR, 14% PR).
    • Sunitinib: 29% (5% CR, 24% PR).
    PMID:29867230
  • ORR, PD-L1+:
    • Atezolizumab + bevacizumab: 46% (12% CR, 34% PR).
    • Atezolizumab: 28% (15% CR, 13% PR).
    • Sunitinib: 27% (7% CR, 20% PR).
    PMID:29867230
  • PFS benefit trended upward with higher PD-L1 IC expression in both atezolizumab arms vs. sunitinib at the ≥5% cutoff (unstratified). Investigator- and independent-review-assessed PFS agreed in 77% of patients. PMID:29867230

Safety

  • Treatment-related grade 3/4 AEs:
    • Sunitinib: 57%.
    • Atezolizumab + bevacizumab: 40%.
    • Atezolizumab: 17%.
    PMID:29867230
  • Treatment-related AEs leading to discontinuation: 9% with atezolizumab + bevacizumab (most often proteinuria, 5%), 3% with atezolizumab, and 9% with sunitinib (most often increased creatinine and palmar-plantar erythrodysesthesia, 2% each). PMID:29867230
  • Treatment-related deaths: 2 with sunitinib, 1 with atezolizumab + bevacizumab and 0 with atezolizumab. PMID:29867230

Gene-expression signatures (263 tumors; high/low split at the median)

  • A heatmap of angiogenesis, immune and myeloid-inflammation genes showed distinct biological subgroups. AngioHigh tumors had higher CD31 IHC vascular density (P = 4.19×10⁻²¹). The Teff score was associated with PD-L1 IC IHC (P = 3.26×10⁻²⁰) and CD8 IHC (P = 1.26×10⁻²⁸). PMID:29867230
  • Angio within the sunitinib arm: AngioHigh tumors had better ORR (46% vs. 9%) and PFS (HR 0.31, 0.18–0.55) than AngioLow tumors. PMID:29867230
  • Angio across arms: In AngioLow tumors, atezolizumab + bevacizumab improved PFS vs. sunitinib (HR 0.59, 0.35–0.98). In AngioHigh tumors, no arm differed from sunitinib. PMID:29867230
  • Teff within the atezolizumab + bevacizumab arm: TeffHigh tumors had better ORR (49% vs. 16%) and PFS (HR 0.50, 0.30–0.86). PMID:29867230
  • Teff across arms: In TeffHigh tumors, atezolizumab + bevacizumab improved PFS vs. sunitinib (HR 0.55, 0.32–0.95). PMID:29867230
  • Myeloid inflammation within arms: MyeloidHigh tumors had shorter PFS with atezolizumab (HR 2.98, 1.68–5.29) and, less strongly, with atezolizumab + bevacizumab (HR 1.71, 1.01–2.88), but not with sunitinib. PMID:29867230
  • Myeloid inflammation across arms: In MyeloidHigh tumors, atezolizumab did worse than sunitinib (HR 2.03, 1.21–3.40). Atezolizumab + bevacizumab did not differ from sunitinib in this subgroup. PMID:29867230
  • TeffHigh tumors split by myeloid inflammation:
    • With atezolizumab monotherapy, TeffHighMyeloidHigh tumors did worse than TeffHighMyeloidLow tumors (HR 3.82, 1.70–8.60).
    • In TeffHighMyeloidHigh tumors, atezolizumab + bevacizumab improved PFS vs. atezolizumab alone (HR 0.25, 0.10–0.60).
    • In TeffHighMyeloidLow tumors, the two atezolizumab arms did not differ.
    PMID:29867230
  • Taken alone, none of the three dichotomized signatures separated atezolizumab + bevacizumab from atezolizumab monotherapy. PMID:29867230

Mutational load (201 WES tumors)

  • Medians: Expressed TMB 30 (range 1–309), tumor neoantigen burden 8 (0–79), indel load 3 (0–113), frameshift mutation burden 2 (0–55). PMID:29867230
  • None of these measures was associated with the Teff signature or with clinical benefit in any arm. TMB by response group gave t-test P = 0.06 (sunitinib), 0.14 (atezolizumab + bevacizumab) and 0.93 (atezolizumab). PMID:29867230
  • The authors report that this larger randomized RCC cohort did not confirm the proposed link between indel/frameshift-derived neoantigens and immunogenicity. PMID:29867230

Driver mutations

  • VHL was mutated in 62% of tumors and PBRM1 in 44%. Both mutant groups had higher mean Angio signature scores. VHL status was not associated with PFS in any arm. PMID:29867230
  • PBRM1 mutation was associated with better PFS on sunitinib (HR 0.38, 0.20–0.73). PMID:29867230
  • Within PBRM1-mutant tumors, atezolizumab did worse than sunitinib (HR 2.49, 1.26–4.91), and atezolizumab + bevacizumab did better than atezolizumab (HR 0.42, 0.22–0.82). PMID:29867230

Genes & alterations

  • VHL: somatic mutation in 62% of 201 tumors. Mutants had higher mean Angio signature expression, and mutation status was not associated with PFS in any arm. PMID:29867230
  • PBRM1: mutated in 44% of tumors. Mutants had higher mean Angio signature expression and better PFS on sunitinib (HR 0.38). In the mutant subgroup, atezolizumab monotherapy did worse than sunitinib (HR 2.49), and the authors state their data do not support PBRM1 mutation as a predictor of checkpoint-inhibitor response. PMID:29867230
  • CD274 (PD-L1): IC protein expression by the SP142 assay defined the co-primary PD-L1+ (≥1% IC) population. CD274 is also one of the five Teff signature genes, and the Teff score correlated strongly with PD-L1 IC IHC. PMID:29867230
  • Angio signature: VEGFA, KDR, ESM1, PECAM1, ANGPTL4 and CD34. AngioHigh predicted sunitinib benefit and tracked CD31 IHC vascular density (CD31 is encoded by PECAM1). PMID:29867230
  • Teff signature: CD8A, EOMES, PRF1, IFNG and CD274. TeffHigh predicted benefit from atezolizumab + bevacizumab within that arm and vs. sunitinib, and tracked CD8 IHC. PMID:29867230
  • Myeloid inflammation signature: IL6, CXCL1, CXCL2, CXCL3, CXCL8 and PTGS2. MyeloidHigh was associated with worse PFS on atezolizumab monotherapy, and this was proposed as innate resistance that bevacizumab may overcome. PMID:29867230

Clinical implications

  • In PD-L1+ (≥1% IC, 54% of enrolled patients) treatment-naive mRCC, atezolizumab + bevacizumab showed encouraging PFS vs. sunitinib. The authors note this was supported by the phase 3 IMmotion151 trial. PMID:29867230
  • The authors propose a diagnostic or enrichment strategy based on PD-L1 IC IHC and/or an immune (Teff) gene-expression signature for first-line mRCC. They note that PD-L1 on tumor cells (24% of patients) also enriched for benefit in a phase 3 trial of ipilimumab + nivolumab vs. sunitinib. PMID:29867230
  • Biology that may match patients to treatment:
    • Highly angiogenic (AngioHigh) and PBRM1-mutant tumors may benefit most from sunitinib.
    • Tumors with pre-existing immunity (TeffHigh / PD-L1 IC+) favor atezolizumab + bevacizumab.
    • Immune-inflamed tumors with low myeloid inflammation (TeffHighMyeloidLow) respond to atezolizumab alone.
    PMID:29867230
  • Myeloid inflammation (IL-6, CXCL8-family chemokines, prostaglandins/PTGS2) is proposed as a mechanism of innate resistance to PD-L1 blockade that VEGF blockade may reverse. The authors point to preclinical and human data showing that VEGF/VEGFR blockade reduces myeloid-derived suppressor cells. PMID:29867230
  • TMB and neoantigen burden should not be assumed to predict checkpoint-inhibitor benefit in RCC, unlike in NSCLC and metastatic urothelial carcinoma. PMID:29867230
  • The single-agent activity and complete responses with atezolizumab motivated the adjuvant IMmotion010 trial. Atezolizumab + bevacizumab showed activity across MSKCC risk groups, including favorable risk. An ad hoc IMDC analysis (78% concordance with MSKCC) corroborated PFS activity across risk groups. PMID:29867230

Limitations & open questions

  • The study was a hypothesis-generating phase 2 trial built to estimate effect sizes, not to establish statistical significance. Endpoints were changed mid-trial: the PD-L1 cutoff was revised from ≥5% to ≥1% IC and a co-primary endpoint was added, which likely caused the imbalance in PD-L1+ patients across arms. The authors therefore describe the analysis as exploratory. PMID:29867230
  • The trial was open-label. Overall survival and crossover data were immature and not reported. PMID:29867230
  • Biomarker analyses covered subsets (RNA-seq 263/305; WES 201). Signatures were split at the cohort median with no prespecified threshold, and P values were not adjusted for multiple testing. The authors say the findings require validation in larger randomized trials (IMmotion151). PMID:29867230
  • An analysis of a stromal-high subpopulation that appeared resistant to immunotherapy was ad hoc. PMID:29867230
  • The biological basis of immunogenicity in RCC is unresolved. The authors suggest clonal indels and endogenous retrovirus expression as future directions. PMID:29867230
  • Cross-paper conflict on PBRM1: This trial found PBRM1 mutation associated with sunitinib benefit, not checkpoint-inhibitor benefit. The authors state this does not support a retrospective report from a heterogeneous population linking PBRM1 mutation to checkpoint-inhibitor response. Their reference list includes Miao et al., PMID:29301960, whose anti-PD-(L)1 ccRCC cohorts showed PBRM1 loss-of-function enriched in patients with clinical benefit. The authors attribute the discrepancy to “differences in the patient population and study design”; IMmotion150 was a first-line randomized trial. PMID:29867230
  • Later reuse: IMmotion150 (n = 163) was later included in a 14-cohort ccRCC meta-analysis, where the IMmotion150 Angio signature served as a comparator for TKI-response prediction. PMID:40834854
