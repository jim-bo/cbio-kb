# PGD

## Overview

PGD (Phosphogluconate Dehydrogenase) encodes an enzyme in the pentose phosphate pathway. In cancer genomics, PGD has been identified as a GWAS susceptibility locus associated with hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC) risk, particularly in the context of viral hepatitis (HBV/HCV).
