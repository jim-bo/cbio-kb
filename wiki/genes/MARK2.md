# MARK2

## Overview

MARK2 is a serine/threonine kinase identified in this corpus through
phosphoproteomic profiling of neoadjuvant-treated triple-negative breast
cancer as a marker enriched in chemoresistant tumors.

## Alterations observed in the corpus

- Target phosphosites enriched in non-pathologic-complete-response (non-pCR), chemoresistant tumors in a proteogenomic study of neoadjuvant-treated triple-negative breast cancer [PMID:36001024](../papers/36001024.md).

## Cancer types (linked)

- [BRCA](../cancer_types/BRCA.md) — phosphosite activity enriched in non-pCR triple-negative breast cancer tumors [PMID:36001024](../papers/36001024.md).

## Co-occurrence and mutual exclusivity

- None reported.

## Therapeutic relevance

- None reported.

## Open questions

- Whether MARK2 phosphosite activity is causally linked to chemoresistance or reflects a broader non-pCR phosphoproteomic state was not established [PMID:36001024](../papers/36001024.md).
