# Single-nuclear ATAC sequencing (snATAC-seq)

## Overview

snATAC-seq profiles chromatin accessibility at single-nucleus resolution, using the transposase Tn5 to tag open chromatin in isolated nuclei from fresh-frozen tissue. It is used alongside snRNA-seq to link regulatory element accessibility to cell-state-specific gene expression programs, and is particularly suited to archival or frozen samples where intact whole cells are unavailable.

## Used by

- Profiled 7 of 94 SDHB-mutant pheochromocytoma/paraganglioma tumors by 10x snATAC-seq (alongside snRNA-seq, bulk WGS, RNA-seq and EPIC methylation) to characterize chromatin accessibility in this cohort [PMID:40097403](../papers/40097403.md).
