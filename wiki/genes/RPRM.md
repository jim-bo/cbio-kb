# RPRM

## Overview

RPRM (Reprimo) is a G2/M cell-cycle checkpoint gene. In the corpus it is reported as transcriptionally repressed in ATRX-altered pheochromocytoma/paraganglioma (PCPG).

## Alterations observed in the corpus

- G2/M checkpoint gene (with DRG2) repressed in ATRX-altered pheochromocytoma/paraganglioma, coinciding with overexpression of RIPK4 in the same tumors. [PMID:40097403](../papers/40097403.md)

## Cancer types (linked)

- No cancer-type page (OncoTree code) established yet for the PCPG cohort in this corpus.

## Co-occurrence and mutual exclusivity

- Co-repressed with DRG2 specifically in ATRX-altered PCPG tumors. [PMID:40097403](../papers/40097403.md)

## Therapeutic relevance

- No therapeutic data reported in the current corpus.

## Open questions

- Whether RPRM/DRG2 repression is a direct consequence of ATRX-driven chromatin changes (ALT phenotype) or an independent downstream effect is unresolved. [PMID:40097403](../papers/40097403.md)
