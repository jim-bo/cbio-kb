# RMDN1

## Overview

RMDN1 (regulator of microtubule dynamics 1) is located at 8q21.3, a region recurrently amplified in triple-negative breast cancer that co-locates with the DNA-repair/replication genes [LIG1](../genes/LIG1.md), [POLD1](../genes/POLD1.md) and [XRCC1](../genes/XRCC1.md).

## Alterations observed in the corpus

- One of four genes (with [CPNE3](../genes/CPNE3.md), [DECR1](../genes/DECR1.md), [OTUD6B](../genes/OTUD6B.md)) in the amplified 8q21.3 region with higher mRNA and protein in non-pathologic-complete-response (non-pCR) triple-negative breast tumors after neoadjuvant chemotherapy. [PMID:36001024](../papers/36001024.md)

## Cancer types (linked)

- No cancer-type-specific prevalence data reported in the current corpus.

## Co-occurrence and mutual exclusivity

- Co-located in the amplified 8q21.3 region with CPNE3, DECR1, OTUD6B and [RIPK2](../genes/RIPK2.md) (elevated at the protein level only). [PMID:36001024](../papers/36001024.md)

## Therapeutic relevance

- No therapeutic data reported in the current corpus.

## Open questions

- Whether 8q21.3 amplification genes including RMDN1 contribute mechanistically to [carboplatin](../drugs/carboplatin.md) resistance, versus co-varying passively with the proposed LIG1/POLD1/XRCC1 lagging-strand-synthesis deficiency, is unresolved. [PMID:36001024](../papers/36001024.md)
