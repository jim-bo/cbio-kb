# LRPAP1

## Overview

LRPAP1 (LRP-associated protein 1) appears in this corpus as the 5' partner
of a clinically actionable kinase fusion in pediatric solid tumor
patient-derived xenografts.

## Alterations observed in the corpus

- LRPAP1–PDGFRA fusion (kinase domain retained, high PDGFRA expression, RT-PCR validated) identified as a clinically actionable kinase fusion in glioblastoma and germ cell tumor patient-derived xenograft samples [PMID:37990009](../papers/37990009.md).

## Cancer types (linked)

- [GB](../cancer_types/GB.md) — LRPAP1–PDGFRA fusion identified as clinically actionable [PMID:37990009](../papers/37990009.md).
- [GCT](../cancer_types/GCT.md) — LRPAP1–PDGFRA fusion identified as clinically actionable [PMID:37990009](../papers/37990009.md).

## Co-occurrence and mutual exclusivity

- Fuses with PDGFRA, with the PDGFRA kinase domain retained and PDGFRA highly expressed [PMID:37990009](../papers/37990009.md).

## Therapeutic relevance

- The retained PDGFRA kinase domain and high PDGFRA expression in LRPAP1–PDGFRA fusion-positive tumors are noted as clinically actionable [PMID:37990009](../papers/37990009.md).

## Open questions

- Whether LRPAP1–PDGFRA-fusion tumors respond to PDGFRA-targeted therapy was not tested in this cohort [PMID:37990009](../papers/37990009.md).
