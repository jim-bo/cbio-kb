# IRX3

## Overview

IRX3 (Iroquois homeobox 3) encodes a homeodomain transcription factor. In the cbio-kb corpus it appears in a multi-omic pheochromocytoma/paraganglioma ([PCPG](../cancer_types/PCPG.md)) study as one of a small number of genes whose expression is specifically altered in TERT-altered tumors; its functional role in PCPG biology has not otherwise been characterized in the cited work.

## Alterations observed in the corpus

- IRX3 (together with [TRIP13](../genes/TRIP13.md)) was among the few genes differentially expressed specifically in TERT-altered pheochromocytoma/paraganglioma tumors [PMID:40097403](../papers/40097403.md).

## Cancer types (linked)

- Pheochromocytoma/paraganglioma (PHC/PGNG): differential IRX3 expression tracked [TERT](../genes/TERT.md) alteration status in a cohort of SDHB-mutant PCPG tumors [PMID:40097403](../papers/40097403.md).

## Co-occurrence and mutual exclusivity

- No co-mutation or mutual-exclusivity relationship is reported for IRX3 in the corpus; the observed association is with TERT alteration status at the expression level, not genomic co-alteration [PMID:40097403](../papers/40097403.md).

## Therapeutic relevance

- None reported in the corpus.

## Open questions

- Whether IRX3 differential expression is a driver or a downstream consequence of TERT alteration in PCPG is unresolved [PMID:40097403](../papers/40097403.md).
