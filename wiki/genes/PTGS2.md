# PTGS2

## Overview

PTGS2 encodes prostaglandin-endoperoxide synthase 2 (COX-2), the inducible cyclooxygenase that produces prostaglandins mediating inflammation. In gastric carcinogenesis, PTGS2 is activated by H. pylori infection through epigenetic remodeling: the histone demethylase [KDM4B](../genes/KDM4B.md) (JMJD2B) is upregulated and drives H4 acetylation at the [CDKN1A](../genes/CDKN1A.md) (p21) promoter, contributing to PTGS2 activation and sustaining the pro-inflammatory microenvironment that promotes gastric cancer development.

## Alterations observed in the corpus

- Activated as part of the KDM4B/PTGS2/CDKN1A axis driven by H. pylori infection; H. pylori upregulates [KDM4B](../genes/KDM4B.md) histone demethylase and drives H4 acetylation at the [CDKN1A](../genes/CDKN1A.md) promoter, activating PTGS2 (COX-2) and contributing to gastric carcinogenesis [PMID:41097736](../papers/41097736.md)
- COX-2; induced downstream of [S1PR2](../genes/S1PR2.md) (taurocholic-acid–activated) via ERK/AKT/NF-kB signaling in cholangiocarcinoma; contributes to suppression of CD8+ T-cell activity and regulatory T cell recruitment [PMID:41018102](../papers/41018102.md)
- PTGS2 (COX-2) synthesizes PGE2, which promotes [HCC](../cancer_types/HCC.md) and immunosuppression; NSAIDs ([aspirin](../drugs/aspirin.md), [celecoxib](../drugs/celecoxib.md)) targeting COX-2/PTGS2 sensitize HCC to [sorafenib](../drugs/sorafenib.md) and IFN-alpha in preclinical models [PMID:41118283](../papers/41118283.md)
- PTGS2 is part of a myeloid inflammation signature (IL6, CXCL1-3, CXCL8, PTGS2) in renal cell carcinoma; MyeloidHigh tumors had worse PFS on atezolizumab monotherapy [PMID:29867230](../papers/29867230.md)

## Cancer types (linked)

- Gastric cancer ([STAD](../cancer_types/STAD.md)): PTGS2 is a mediator of H. pylori-driven gastric carcinogenesis through the [KDM4B](../genes/KDM4B.md) epigenetic axis; relevant to familial and sporadic gastric cancer pathogenesis [PMID:41097736](../papers/41097736.md)

## Co-occurrence and mutual exclusivity

- Functions downstream of [KDM4B](../genes/KDM4B.md) in the KDM4B/PTGS2/CDKN1A carcinogenic axis activated by H. pylori [PMID:41097736](../papers/41097736.md)

## Therapeutic relevance

- COX-2 inhibition has been explored as a chemopreventive strategy in gastric cancer, though no targeted therapy data for PTGS2 in familial gastric cancer is reported in this corpus [PMID:41097736](../papers/41097736.md)

## Open questions

- The extent to which PTGS2 upregulation via the [KDM4B](../genes/KDM4B.md) axis contributes to familial vs. sporadic gastric cancer and whether COX-2 inhibition modifies cancer risk in H. pylori-eradicated patients remain unresolved [PMID:41097736](../papers/41097736.md)
