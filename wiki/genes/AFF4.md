# AFF4

## Overview

AFF4 (AF4/FMR2 family member 4) encodes a core scaffold subunit of the super elongation complex (SEC), which recruits P-TEFb to promote transcriptional pause-release and processive RNA Pol II elongation. AFF4 is best known as a recurrent fusion partner of [KMT2A](../genes/KMT2A.md) (MLL) in KMT2A-rearranged acute leukemias, where the fusion hijacks SEC-mediated elongation to drive a leukemogenic transcriptional program; AFF4 rearrangements involving other partner genes are rare and less well characterized in cancer genomics.

## Alterations observed in the corpus

- 5' partner in an AFF4 exon 1–TERT exon 3 fusion arising from chr5 chromothripsis in a metastatic SDHB-mutant pheochromocytoma/paraganglioma, producing the cohort's highest TERT expression [PMID:40097403](../papers/40097403.md)

## Cancer types (linked)

- [PCPG](../cancer_types/PHC.md) (pheochromocytoma/paraganglioma): a single case (of 79 SDHB-mutant tumors) carried the AFF4–[TERT](../genes/TERT.md) fusion, associated with the cohort's highest TERT expression [PMID:40097403](../papers/40097403.md).

## Co-occurrence and mutual exclusivity

- Co-occurred with germline SDHB mutation and chr5 chromothripsis in the single reported case [PMID:40097403](../papers/40097403.md).

## Therapeutic relevance

- Not yet reported in the corpus.

## Open questions

- Whether AFF4–TERT fusions represent a recurrent, targetable mechanism of telomerase reactivation in SDHB-mutant PCPG beyond the single reported case [PMID:40097403](../papers/40097403.md).
