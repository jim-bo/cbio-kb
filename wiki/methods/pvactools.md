# pVACtools

## Overview

pVACtools (including the pVACseq module) is a neoantigen-prediction pipeline that combines somatic variant calls with HLA typing and peptide-MHC binding predictors (e.g. NetMHCpan) to identify candidate tumor neoantigens.

## Used by

- Used (pVACseq, following OptiType HLA typing, with NetMHCpan, PickPocket, SMM and SMMPMBEC; peptides kept at best mutant-allele IC50 ≤500 nM) for neoantigen prediction in a pediatric solid-tumor patient-derived xenograft genomic study [PMID:37990009](../papers/37990009.md).
