# PD-L1 IHC SP142

## Overview

The VENTANA SP142 immunohistochemistry assay scores PD-L1 staining on tumor-infiltrating immune cells (IC), with IC0/IC1/IC2-3 cutoffs (<1%, 1-<5%, ≥5% of immune-cell area). It is the companion-diagnostic assay used with atezolizumab trials.

## Used by

- Scored PD-L1 on tumor-infiltrating immune cells prospectively (central read, HistoGeneX; cutoffs IC0 <1%, IC1 1-<5%, IC2/3 ≥5%: 39/48/32 patients) as an exploratory biomarker in a single-arm phase 2 atezolizumab first-line urothelial carcinoma trial [PMID:27939400](../papers/27939400.md).
- Used the SP142 IHC assay (Ventana) to score PD-L1 on tumor-infiltrating immune cells (≥5%, later redefined ≥1%, positivity threshold; 59%/52%/50% positive by arm) for risk stratification in a phase 2 atezolizumab ± bevacizumab vs. sunitinib renal cell carcinoma trial [PMID:29867230](../papers/29867230.md).
- Scored PD-L1 on tumor-infiltrating immune cells prospectively (central read, HistoGeneX; cutoffs IC0 <1%, IC1 1-<5%, IC2/3 ≥5%: 39/48/32 patients in cohort 1) in the final-analysis report of the phase 2 IMvigor210 atezolizumab urothelial carcinoma trial [PMID:39642637](../papers/39642637.md).

## Notes

- Not a cBioPortal gene panel or registered molecular-profile type; corpus-grown slug. Distinct from the 22C3 (pembrolizumab) and 28-8 (nivolumab) PD-L1 companion-diagnostic assays.
