# RPL5

## Overview

RPL5 encodes a component of the large ribosomal subunit. In the corpus it is reported as an acquired mutation in a metastatic pheochromocytoma/paraganglioma ([PCPG](../cancer_types/PCPG.md)) case after chemotherapy.

## Alterations observed in the corpus

- Mutation acquired after CVD (cyclophosphamide/vincristine/dacarbazine) chemotherapy in a post-treatment PCPG metastasis (case E169-M2), alongside a [POLE](../genes/POLE.md) mutation. [PMID:40097403](../papers/40097403.md)

## Cancer types (linked)

- No cancer-type page (OncoTree code) established yet for the PCPG cohort in this corpus.

## Co-occurrence and mutual exclusivity

- Co-occurred with an acquired POLE mutation in the same post-CVD metastasis (case E169-M2); the POLE-deficiency mutational signature SBS14 was absent in this tumor. [PMID:40097403](../papers/40097403.md)

## Therapeutic relevance

- No therapeutic data reported in the current corpus.

## Open questions

- Whether the acquired RPL5 mutation is a treatment-induced driver of resistance/progression or a passenger in this single post-CVD metastasis is unresolved. [PMID:40097403](../papers/40097403.md)
