# EGFL7

## Overview

EGFL7 (EGF-like domain multiple 7) is a secreted angiogenesis-associated gene. In the corpus it appears as a non-canonical structural rearrangement partner of EWSR1 identified in Ewing sarcoma chromoplexy analysis, rather than as a recurrently mutated cancer gene.

## Alterations observed in the corpus

- EGFL7 is among the cancer-related genes found as non-canonical EWSR1 fusion partners in chromoplectic structural variants in Ewing sarcoma; the rearrangement was not expressed by RNA sequencing [PMID:38335254](../papers/38335254.md).

## Cancer types (linked)

- [ES](../cancer_types/ES.md) (Ewing sarcoma): non-canonical EWSR1::EGFL7 structural rearrangement identified as part of a chromoplexy event [PMID:38335254](../papers/38335254.md).

## Co-occurrence and mutual exclusivity

- Identified alongside other non-canonical EWSR1 chromoplexy partners including ATR, ETS1, EP300, EZR, FOSB, NAB2 and ZNF331 [PMID:38335254](../papers/38335254.md).
