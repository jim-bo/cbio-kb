# lirilumab

## Overview

Lirilumab is an anti-KIR (killer-cell immunoglobulin-like receptor) monoclonal antibody that blocks inhibitory KIR2DL1/2/3 and KIR3DL1 signaling on NK cells, intended to enhance NK-cell-mediated anti-tumor activity. It has been investigated as a combination partner for anti-PD-1 therapy.

## Evidence in the corpus

- Authors propose combining nivolumab with the NK-cell-directed agent lirilumab in metastatic renal cell carcinoma, based on nivolumab-induced increases in NK-function gene transcripts (KLRB1, KLRD1, KLRG1, CD69, NKG7) in on-treatment tumor biopsies. [PMID:27169994](../papers/27169994.md)
