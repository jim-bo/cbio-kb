# vorasidenib

## Overview

A brain-penetrant, dual inhibitor of mutant [IDH1](../genes/IDH1.md) and [IDH2](../genes/IDH2.md) enzymes.

## Evidence in the corpus

- The authors propose extending IDH1/2 inhibition, including vorasidenib, as an additional treatment option for high-grade solitary fibrous tumors carrying the IDH1 p.R132S hotspot mutation. [PMID:37980418](../papers/37980418.md)
