# Liver Hepatocellular Carcinoma (RIKEN, Nat Genet 2012)

## Overview

cBioPortal study of whole-genome sequencing of 27 hepatocellular carcinoma ([HCC](../cancer_types/HCC.md)) tumor/normal pairs from RIKEN (Fujimoto et al., *Nat Genet* 2012, PMID 22634756). That source publication is not in this corpus; the page records how corpus papers use the cohort.

## Composition

- Cancer type: [HCC](../cancer_types/HCC.md)
- 27 HCC tumor/normal pairs, whole-genome sequenced (per the cBioPortal study description).

## Assays / panels (linked)

- [Whole-genome sequencing](../methods/whole-genome-seq.md)

## Papers using this cohort

- [PMID:31130341](../papers/31130341.md) — pan-Asia combined HCC–ICC integrative genomic study; used RIKEN-LIHC as a reference HCC cohort.

## Notable findings derived from this cohort

- Served as a reference HCC cohort (RIKEN-LIHC) in the pan-Asia cHCC-ICC integrative genomic study; contributed to the pan-liver-cancer transcriptomic clustering (n=367) identifying P1–P4 clusters and to comparative mutational-landscape benchmarks for TP53 and TERT promoter frequencies [PMID:31130341](../papers/31130341.md)
