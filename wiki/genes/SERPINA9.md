# SERPINA9

## Overview

SERPINA9 is a germinal-center B-cell (GCB) associated gene. In the corpus it is reported as differentially expressed between Burkitt lymphoma subgroups.

## Alterations observed in the corpus

- GCB-associated gene downregulated in intermediate-cluster Burkitt lymphoma (IC-BL) versus the DGG-BL subgroup. [PMID:36201743](../papers/36201743.md)
