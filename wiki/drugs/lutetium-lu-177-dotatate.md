# lutetium-lu-177-dotatate

## Overview

Lutetium Lu-177 dotatate (177Lu-DOTA-octreotate; Lutathera) is a radiolabeled somatostatin analog that delivers targeted beta radiation to SSTR2-expressing neuroendocrine tumor cells, including pheochromocytoma/paraganglioma ([PCPG](../cancer_types/PCPG.md)).

## Evidence in the corpus

- In a cfDNA case study, plasma was drawn from a high-volume SDHB-associated PCPG patient after three cycles of [temozolomide](../drugs/temozolomide.md) plus lutetium Lu-177 dotatate (177Lu-DOTA-octreotate). [PMID:40097403](../papers/40097403.md)
- Authors propose testing DNA-damage-response inhibitors (targeting [ATM](../genes/ATM.md), [WEE1](../genes/WEE1.md), [ATR](../genes/ATR.md), PARP) as radiosensitizers in combination with lutetium Lu-177 dotatate or 131I-MIBG in ATRX-altered PCPG. [PMID:40097403](../papers/40097403.md)
