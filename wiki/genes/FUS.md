# FUS

## Overview

FUS (FUS RNA Binding Protein) encodes a TET-family RNA-binding protein. In Ewing sarcoma, FUS is an alternative TET-family N-terminal fusion partner in rare cases where [EWSR1](../genes/EWSR1.md) is not the fusion driver.

## Alterations observed in the corpus

- Listed as an alternative TET-family N-terminal fusion partner in rare Ewing sarcoma cases; mechanistically related to EWSR1 as both are TET-family RNA-binding proteins [PMID:25223734](../papers/25223734.md).
- FUS fusions (e.g., FUS-ERG, FUS-FEV) were identified as alternative oncogenic drivers in a subset of Ewing sarcoma cases [PMID:41102727](../papers/41102727.md)
- Partner in FET-ETS family fusions (e.g. FUS::[ERG](../genes/ERG.md)) defining a rare [AML](../cancer_types/AML.md) expression subgroup (16 of 887 cases) [PMID:38212634](../papers/38212634.md).

## Cancer types (linked)

- **[ES](../cancer_types/ES.md) (Ewing sarcoma)**: rare alternative TET-family N-terminal fusion partner (alongside [TAF15](../genes/TAF15.md)) in Ewing sarcoma cases lacking EWSR1 fusions [PMID:25223734](../papers/25223734.md).
