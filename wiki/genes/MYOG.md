# MYOG

## Overview

MYOG (myogenin) is a late-stage myogenic transcription factor and terminal differentiation marker used diagnostically and to characterize tumor cell states in MYOD1-mutant rhabdomyosarcoma.

## Alterations observed in the corpus

- Late-stage myogenic effector (myogenin) with increased protein activity marking the differentiated cell state in [MYOD1](../genes/MYOD1.md) L122R-mutant spindle cell/sclerosing rhabdomyosarcoma; diffusely positive MYOD1 with rare-to-negative myogenin was the immunohistochemical signature across six tumors [PMID:41758938](../papers/41758938.md).

## Cancer types (linked)

- **[SCSRMS](../cancer_types/SCSRMS.md)** — myogenin protein activity distinguishes the differentiated tumor cell state from the MYOD1-high progenitor state in MYOD1 L122R-mutant spindle cell/sclerosing rhabdomyosarcoma [PMID:41758938](../papers/41758938.md).
