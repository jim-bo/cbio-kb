# copanlisib

## Overview

Copanlisib is a small-molecule pan-class I PI3K inhibitor with predominant activity against the PI3Kalpha ([PIK3CA](../genes/PIK3CA.md)) and PI3Kdelta ([PIK3CD](../genes/PIK3CD.md)) isoforms.

## Evidence in the corpus

- In a rhabdomyosarcoma patient-derived tumor organoid (PDXO) drug screen (42 single-agent/combination regimens), organoids showed relative sensitivity to copanlisib despite broad chemotherapy resistance; at the drug-class level, PI3K/AKT/mTOR inhibitors fell at or below the predefined 25th-percentile sensitivity threshold. [PMID:41758938](../papers/41758938.md)

## Resistance mechanisms

- No resistance mechanisms to copanlisib are reported in the current corpus.
