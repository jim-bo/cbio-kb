# KIF16B

## Overview

KIF16B encodes a kinesin motor protein involved in endosomal trafficking. It was identified as a novel highly-confident significantly mutated gene (HC-SMG) in a large colorectal cancer whole-exome cohort.

## Alterations observed in the corpus

- KIF16B was a novel highly confident significantly mutated gene (HC-SMG) in colorectal cancer, mutated in 3% of the cohort [PMID:35487942](../papers/35487942.md).

## Cancer types (linked)

- Colorectal cancer (COADREAD): one of eight novel HC-SMGs (with LYST, DAPK1, CR2, NPIPB15, SYTL2, ZNF91, KIAA0586) that together were mutated in 14.9% of the cohort and 19.55% of TCGA colorectal cases [PMID:35487942](../papers/35487942.md).

## Co-occurrence and mutual exclusivity

- Not reported in the corpus.

## Therapeutic relevance

- None reported in the corpus.

## Open questions

- Functional validation of KIF16B as a colorectal cancer driver gene is not yet established in the corpus [PMID:35487942](../papers/35487942.md).
