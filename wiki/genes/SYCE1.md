# SYCE1

## Overview

SYCE1 (synaptonemal complex central element protein 1) is a cancer-testis-associated gene observed as a differentially expressed transcript in a metastatic triple-negative breast cancer (TNBC) immunotherapy trial cohort.

## Alterations observed in the corpus

- SYCE1 (along with ANKRD30A and TUBB2B) showed higher pretreatment tumor expression in responders (CR+PR) to a cyclophosphamide-priming plus pembrolizumab regimen in metastatic triple-negative breast cancer [PMID:35121644](../papers/35121644.md).

## Cancer types (linked)

- [BRCA](../cancer_types/BRCA.md): elevated pretreatment SYCE1 expression associated with response in a metastatic TNBC pembrolizumab trial [PMID:35121644](../papers/35121644.md).

## Co-occurrence and mutual exclusivity

- No co-occurrence or mutual-exclusivity relationships reported for SYCE1 in the corpus to date.

## Therapeutic relevance

- Higher pretreatment SYCE1 expression was associated with response to cyclophosphamide-priming plus pembrolizumab in metastatic TNBC, though the extracted text does not establish a causal or targetable role [PMID:35121644](../papers/35121644.md).

## Open questions

- Whether elevated SYCE1 expression is a driver of, or merely correlated with, immunotherapy response in TNBC is not addressed in the extracted text [PMID:35121644](../papers/35121644.md).
