# EMP1

## Overview

EMP1 (epithelial membrane protein 1) is a tetraspan membrane protein gene. In the corpus it is reported as a transcriptomic/cell-state marker rather than a mutated driver: it co-marks an injury-repair, tumor-regenerative gene-expression module at the colorectal cancer invasion front that has been linked to metastasis-initiating cells.

## Alterations observed in the corpus

- EMP1 (with [L1CAM](../genes/L1CAM.md)) is a co-expressed marker of an LGR5-low injury-repair/tumor-regenerative gene-expression module at the colorectal cancer invasion front, associated with metastasis-initiating cells [PMID:39478232](../papers/39478232.md).

## Cancer types (linked)

- [COADREAD](../cancer_types/COADREAD.md) (colorectal cancer): EMP1/L1CAM injury-repair module expressed at the primary-tumor invasion front and in an intermediate cell state between canonical intestinal stem cell and non-canonical/fetal metastatic states [PMID:39478232](../papers/39478232.md).

## Co-occurrence and mutual exclusivity

- Co-expressed with [L1CAM](../genes/L1CAM.md); the module is also linked to [TACSTD2](../genes/TACSTD2.md) (TROP2), which is upregulated at the protein level in metastases relative to primary tumors [PMID:39478232](../papers/39478232.md).
