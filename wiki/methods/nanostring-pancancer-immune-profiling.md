# NanoString nCounter PanCancer Immune Profiling Panel

## Overview

A 770-gene, hybridization-based digital mRNA counting panel (NanoString nCounter platform) spanning immune cell type markers, checkpoint genes, cytokines/chemokines and antigen-presentation machinery. It directly quantifies transcript counts without amplification, which makes it well suited to FFPE tissue and small-input immune-profiling studies.

## Used by

- Direct mRNA quantification with the 770-gene panel (n=28 tumors) was used to score an 18-gene interferon-gamma-related signature, which was higher in neoadjuvant than adjuvant-only PD-1 blockade recurrent glioblastoma tumors (P=0.03) [PMID:30742122](../papers/30742122.md).

## Notes

- 770-gene panel covering immune cell types, checkpoints, cytokine/chemokine signaling and antigen presentation.
- Hybridization-based digital counting; no amplification step, which limits input-material bias relative to RNA-seq.
