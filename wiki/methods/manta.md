# Manta

## Overview

Manta is a structural-variant and indel caller for whole-genome and whole-exome sequencing data, designed to detect germline and somatic rearrangements — deletions, duplications, insertions, inversions, and translocations — directly from paired-end sequencing reads.

## Used by

- Used to call structural variants, including MYC-IG rearrangements, as part of genetic subgroup classification in adult and pediatric Burkitt lymphoma [PMID:36201743](../papers/36201743.md).
- Used to call structural variants from whole-exome and whole-genome sequencing of patient-derived xenografts in a study of childhood solid cancer genomic evolution [PMID:37990009](../papers/37990009.md).
- Used to call structural variants in whole-genome sequencing of CDH1-wildtype invasive lobular breast carcinomas [PMID:38347189](../papers/38347189.md).

## Notes

- Corpus-grown slug; not in the cBioPortal gene-panel or molecular-profile ontology.
- Commonly paired with other SV/CNA callers (e.g., GRIDSS, Battenberg, SvABA) for cross-validation of calls.
