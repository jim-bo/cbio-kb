# PTPRC

## Overview

PTPRC (CD45) is a pan-leukocyte surface marker used by IHC and multiplex immunofluorescence to quantify immune infiltration in tumor tissue.

## Alterations observed in the corpus

- PTPRC (CD45) and GFAP were used as leukocyte and glial markers, respectively, in a multiplex immunofluorescence panel assessing tumor-infiltrating immune cells in recurrent glioblastoma treated with neoadjuvant pembrolizumab [PMID:30742122](../papers/30742122.md).

## Cancer types (linked)

- [GB](../cancer_types/GB.md): PTPRC/CD45 staining was part of the multiplex IF panel used to characterize immune infiltration in glioblastoma tumors treated with neoadjuvant versus adjuvant-only PD-1 blockade [PMID:30742122](../papers/30742122.md).
