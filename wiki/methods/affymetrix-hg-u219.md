# Affymetrix HG-U219 Array

## Overview

The Affymetrix Human Genome U219 (HG-U219) array is a whole-transcriptome gene-expression microarray run on the GeneTitan multichannel platform. It is used to measure relative mRNA abundance from labeled RNA, including in pharmacodynamic and biomarker profiling of paired pre-/on-treatment clinical samples.

## Used by

- Tumor and whole-blood RNA from a phase Ib [nivolumab](../drugs/nivolumab.md) trial in metastatic clear-cell renal cell carcinoma (91 treated patients) were profiled on the Affymetrix HG-U219 array (GeneTitan platform; WT-Pico Ovation labeling; RMA-summarized over 18,562 loci using BrainArray v.10) to track baseline-to-C2D8 pharmacodynamic expression changes; data were deposited to ArrayExpress as E-MTAB-3218 and E-MTAB-3219. [PMID:27169994](../papers/27169994.md)

## Notes

- Applied here to both tumor biopsy and whole-blood samples across multiple on-treatment timepoints in an immune-checkpoint-inhibitor trial.
- Probe intensities were summarized with RMA using a BrainArray custom CDF (v10, 18,562 loci) rather than the manufacturer's default probe-set annotation.
