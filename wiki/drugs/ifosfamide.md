# ifosfamide

## Overview

Ifosfamide is an alkylating agent that cross-links DNA after activation by hepatic cytochrome P450 enzymes. It is used in salvage regimens for germ cell tumors ([GCT](../cancer_types/GCT.md)) including TIP ([paclitaxel](../drugs/paclitaxel.md) + ifosfamide + [cisplatin](../drugs/cisplatin.md)) and VIP (vinblastine/etoposide + ifosfamide + [cisplatin](../drugs/cisplatin.md)), typically after failure of first-line BEP chemotherapy. Hemorrhagic cystitis is a key toxicity, prevented by mesna co-administration.

## Evidence in the corpus

- Component of TIP/VIP salvage regimens received by 20% of 180 advanced [GCT](../cancer_types/GCT.md) patients in cisplatin-resistance biomarker study; [TP53](../genes/TP53.md) and [MDM2](../genes/MDM2.md) alterations predicted shorter progression-free survival (HR 1.83; 95% CI 1.12-2.98; P=.016) independent of IGCCCG risk model [PMID:27646943](../papers/27646943.md)
- Component of ISG/SSG IV protocol ([vincristine](../drugs/vincristine.md), [doxorubicin](../drugs/doxorubicin.md), ifosfamide, [etoposide](../drugs/etoposide.md)) in [EWSR1](../genes/EWSR1.md)::[BEND2](../genes/BEND2.md) bladder sarcoma; nephrotoxicity with ifosfamide-containing regimen led to switch to [docetaxel](../drugs/docetaxel.md) + [gemcitabine](../drugs/gemcitabine.md) [PMID:41102727](../papers/41102727.md).
- Part of both the VIP (cisplatin + etoposide + ifosfamide) and TICE (high-dose [carboplatin](../drugs/carboplatin.md) + etoposide + ifosfamide + [paclitaxel](../drugs/paclitaxel.md) + autologous SCT) regimens used in 15 patients with primary mediastinal [NSGCT](../cancer_types/NSGCT.md) ([NSGCT](../cancer_types/NSGCT.md)) and concomitant hematologic malignancy (median OS 6.3 months); poor response was attributed in part to shared [TP53](../genes/TP53.md) loss in the ancestral GCT/hematologic-malignancy clone [PMID:32897884](../papers/32897884.md).
- Listed as a component of the CODOX/IVAC±R first-line regimen in a 297-sample mature B-cell neoplasm genomics cohort; treatment was reported as cohort context only, not analyzed for outcome [PMID:36201743](../papers/36201743.md).
