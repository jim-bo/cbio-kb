# pd0325901

## Overview

PD0325901 is a small-molecule MEK1/2 (MAP2K1/MAP2K2) inhibitor investigated as a targeted therapy for RAS/MEK-pathway-altered tumors.

## Evidence in the corpus

- One NRAS p.Q61K solitary fibrous tumor case, part of a 3-case (2.1%) RAS/MEK-pathway alteration group, was matched to the MEK inhibitors fcn-159 and pd0325901 as candidate targeted therapy. [PMID:37980418](../papers/37980418.md)
