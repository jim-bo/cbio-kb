# Sarcomatoid Carcinoma of the Lung (SARCL)

## Overview

Sarcomatoid Carcinoma of the Lung (SARCL) is a rare, aggressive histologic subtype of Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer (parent [LUNG](../cancer_types/LUNG.md)), characterized by mixed epithelial and sarcoma-like or sarcomatous differentiation. It is grouped with pleomorphic carcinoma of the lung ([LUPC](../cancer_types/LUPC.md)) in pan-cancer [NSCLC](../cancer_types/NSCLC.md) histology breakdowns.

## Cohorts in the corpus

- No dedicated SARCL cohort in the corpus yet; SARCL/LUPC appears as a histologic subgroup within a 66,285-tumor MSK-IMPACT pan-cancer MET-amplification screen [PMID:36044468](../papers/36044468.md).

## Recurrent alterations

- [MET](../genes/MET.md) amplification recurred in pleomorphic and sarcomatoid NSCLC (SARCL/LUPC) at 8.5% in a 66,285-tumor MSK-IMPACT pan-cancer screen, one of the highest rates among the cancer types and histologies screened, well above the 1.9% rate seen in NSCLC overall. [PMID:36044468](../papers/36044468.md)

## Subtypes

- Grouped with pleomorphic carcinoma of the lung (LUPC) as a distinct NSCLC histology in pan-cancer breakdowns; distinguished from [LUAD](../cancer_types/LUAD.md), [LUSC](../cancer_types/LUSC.md) and [LUNE](../cancer_types/LUNE.md). [PMID:36044468](../papers/36044468.md)

## Therapeutic landscape

- No SARCL-specific treatment outcomes are reported in the corpus yet; MET inhibitors ([crizotinib](../drugs/crizotinib.md), [cabozantinib](../drugs/cabozantinib.md), [capmatinib](../drugs/capmatinib.md), [tepotinib](../drugs/tepotinib.md)) were studied in MET-amplified NSCLC broadly, not broken out by SARCL histology. [PMID:36044468](../papers/36044468.md)
