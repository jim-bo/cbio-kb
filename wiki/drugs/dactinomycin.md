# dactinomycin

## Overview

Dactinomycin (actinomycin D) is a DNA-intercalating cytotoxic antibiotic that blocks RNA polymerase-mediated transcription; it is a standard component of VAC (vincristine/dactinomycin/cyclophosphamide) chemotherapy for rhabdomyosarcoma.

## Evidence in the corpus

- Dactinomycin was part of standard VAC (vincristine/dactinomycin/cyclophosphamide) neoadjuvant chemotherapy received by all rhabdomyosarcoma patients in the cohort before delayed resection. [PMID:41758938](../papers/41758938.md)

## Resistance mechanisms

- No resistance mechanisms to dactinomycin are reported in the current corpus.
