# sapanisertib

## Overview

Sapanisertib (MLN0128/TAK-228) is an orally bioavailable ATP-competitive mTORC1/2 inhibitor investigated in PI3K/AKT/mTOR-pathway-driven solid tumors, including sarcomas.

## Evidence in the corpus

- In a 3D patient-derived tumor organoid (PDXO) mini-ring drug screen of MYOD1-mutant spindle cell/sclerosing rhabdomyosarcoma (42 single-agent/combination regimens), sapanisertib (mTORC1/2 inhibitor) showed relative sensitivity against a background of broad chemotherapy resistance; PI3K/AKT/mTOR inhibitors as a drug class fell at or below the predefined 25th-percentile sensitivity threshold. [PMID:41758938](../papers/41758938.md)
