# CARTPT

## Overview

CART prepropeptide (CARTPT), the precursor of the cocaine- and amphetamine-regulated transcript neuropeptide, is used in the corpus as a cell-of-origin expression marker distinguishing chromaffin from non-chromaffin tumor cells.

## Alterations observed in the corpus

- CARTPT is one of a small set of cell-of-origin markers (with TPH1 and HOX/HOTAIR) used to characterize pheochromocytoma/paraganglioma (PCPG) tumors: TPH1 is high in non-chromaffin tumors, while CARTPT and HOX/HOTAIR are low in them [PMID:40097403](../papers/40097403.md).

## Cancer types (linked)

- Pheochromocytoma/paraganglioma (PCPG): CARTPT expression used as a cell-of-origin marker distinguishing chromaffin from non-chromaffin tumor subtypes [PMID:40097403](../papers/40097403.md).
