# CDH23

## Overview

CDH23 encodes a large cadherin-superfamily protein best known for its role in stereocilia tip-links in the inner ear (mutated in Usher syndrome type 1D). In cancer genomics cohorts it recurs among frequently altered genes, in part reflecting its large coding size.

## Alterations observed in the corpus

- CDH23 was among the most frequently altered genes (13%) in solitary fibrous tumor across four cohorts, alongside [ZFHX3](../genes/ZFHX3.md) (25%), [KMT2C](../genes/KMT2C.md) (21%), [TERT](../genes/TERT.md) (19%), [SLX4](../genes/SLX4.md) (17%), [FAT1](../genes/FAT1.md) (16%), [KMT2D](../genes/KMT2D.md) (16%), [ARID1B](../genes/ARID1B.md) (14%) and [NOTCH1](../genes/NOTCH1.md) (9%) [PMID:37980418](../papers/37980418.md).

## Cancer types (linked)

- [SFT](../cancer_types/SFT.md) (solitary fibrous tumor) — CDH23 alteration frequency ~13% across the four SYSUCC-led cohorts; not reported as part of the paper's prognostic/PFS risk model [PMID:37980418](../papers/37980418.md).

## Co-occurrence and mutual exclusivity

- Not separately analyzed for co-occurrence with other genes in the corpus [PMID:37980418](../papers/37980418.md).

## Therapeutic relevance

- No CDH23-targeted therapy is evaluated; the paper does not propose CDH23 as an actionable target [PMID:37980418](../papers/37980418.md).

## Open questions

- Given CDH23's large coding size, whether its 13% alteration frequency in solitary fibrous tumor reflects a driver role versus passenger mutations is not addressed [PMID:37980418](../papers/37980418.md).
