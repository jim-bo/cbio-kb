# NPIPB15

## Overview

NPIPB15 was identified as a novel highly-confident significantly mutated gene (HC-SMG) candidate in a large Chinese colorectal cancer exome cohort, one of eight such novel genes not previously implicated in cancer at the time of the study.

## Alterations observed in the corpus

- Mutated in 3% of the cohort, one of eight novel HC-SMGs (with LYST, DAPK1, CR2, KIF16B, SYTL2, ZNF91, KIAA0586) collectively mutated in 14.9% of the discovery cohort and 19.55% of TCGA [PMID:35487942](../papers/35487942.md).
