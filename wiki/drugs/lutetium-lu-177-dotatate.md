# lutetium-lu-177-dotatate

## Overview

Lutetium Lu-177 dotatate (177Lu-DOTA-octreotate; Lutathera) is a radiolabeled somatostatin analog that delivers targeted beta radiation to SSTR2-expressing neuroendocrine tumor cells, including pheochromocytoma/paraganglioma (PCPG).

## Evidence in the corpus

- In a cfDNA case study, plasma was drawn from a high-volume SDHB-associated PCPG patient after three cycles of temozolomide plus lutetium Lu-177 dotatate (177Lu-DOTA-octreotate). [PMID:40097403](../papers/40097403.md)
- Authors propose testing DNA-damage-response inhibitors (targeting ATM, WEE1, ATR, PARP) as radiosensitizers in combination with lutetium Lu-177 dotatate or 131I-MIBG in ATRX-altered PCPG. [PMID:40097403](../papers/40097403.md)
