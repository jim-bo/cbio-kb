# Propensity Score Matching

## Overview

A statistical technique that matches cases and controls on estimated propensity scores derived from covariates, used to reduce confounding in observational comparisons.

## Used by

- Used (MatchIt nearest-neighbor method, matched on sex, age at diagnosis, stage, distance from anal verge, tumor size and circumferential tumor location) to build a matched comparator cohort in a genomic study of secondary rectal cancer after prostate-cancer radiotherapy [PMID:40100215](../papers/40100215.md).
