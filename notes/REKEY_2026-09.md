# Re-key of 35 mislabeled paper pages (2026-09-10)

## What was wrong

35 of the 377 wiki papers held the full text of a *different* paper than the PMID
they were filed under. Each was a 2014–2025 paper that **cites** the original
cBioPortal study publication, e.g. `papers/28445112.md` (TRACERx, NEJM 2017)
contained "Genomic landscape of endometrial polyps" (Genome Med 2025).

Cause: the PMID→PMCID elink fallback in `src/cbio_kb/ingest/pmid2pmcid.py`
returned `pubmed_pmc_refs` links (PMC records *citing* the PMID) when the PMID
itself had no PMC record. The fix (`2ac63992`, 2026-04-15 13:36) pinned
`linkname=pubmed_pmc`, but `data/pmid_to_pmcid.csv` had been generated at 13:30
and was never regenerated, so the PDFs, raw extracts, and compiled pages all
carried the wrong papers.

Detection: every wiki paper title was compared against PubMed's title for its
PMID (esummary). Exactly the 35 elink-derived mappings failed (title similarity
≤ 0.64); all other 342 matched (≥ 0.68, typically ≥ 0.95).

## What changed

Driven by `notes/rekey_2026-09-10.csv`, applied by `scripts/rekey_papers.py`:

- Each page moved to the citing paper's true PMID (resolved PMCID→PMID via the
  NCBI ID converter; all 35 titles match PubMed exactly). Citations across the
  vault, the eval corpus lists, 3 eval gold sets (LS06, LS10, S17), the
  `_observed.md` ontology notes, and the index News section were rewritten.
- `study_id` cleared on all 35: none of them is the publication behind a
  cBioPortal study.
- Dataset links inherited from the wrong PMID were checked against each paper's
  full text. Kept where the paper really used the cohort (4): `lung_smc_2016`
  (ATLAS classifier validation), `luad_oncosg_2020` (never-smoker LUAD external
  validation), `coadread_tcga_pan_can_atlas_2018` (FBXO7 CRC via cBioPortal),
  `pog570_bcgsc_2020` (OncoMark benchmarking). Dropped elsewhere (9), where the
  study only appears in the bibliography.
- 8 dataset pages that existed only because of a dropped link were deleted
  (their content described the citing paper's cohort, e.g. `nbl_amc_2012` said
  "3 cell lines, EV proteomics"). `datasets/lihc_riken.md` was rewritten from
  cBioPortal metadata; `cancer_types/BCC.md` lost a false EGA-accession claim.
- One claims sidecar claim (`study: mds_tokyo_2011`) was dropped.

## What was deliberately not changed

- `data/seed/cbioportal_study_pmids.csv` and `schema/ontology/studies.json`:
  cBioPortal's own study→PMID facts were always correct.
- `eval/results/`, `eval/verify/`, `eval/corpus_pmids_v1_56.txt`: historical
  records of runs made against the mislabeled pages.
- The original publications for those 35 PMIDs are not in PMC, so they are
  simply absent from the corpus (the ID converter reports no PMCID).

## Follow-ups

- Re-emit claim tests: `uv run python scripts/emit_claim_tests.py`.
- The rag/hybrid FAISS index was re-keyed in place (`meta.jsonl`); rebuild the
  BM25 sidecar with `uv run cbio-kb index build-bm25`.
- Eval numbers produced before this date include these 35 pages under the old
  PMIDs; LS10 (test split) gold changed.
