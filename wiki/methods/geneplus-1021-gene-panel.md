# GenePlus 1021-Gene Panel

## Overview

A commercial hybrid-capture targeted next-generation sequencing panel (Geneplus-Beijing, GenePlus 2000 platform, ~1.1 Mb footprint, 2x100bp paired-end) covering approximately 1,021 cancer-associated genes. Not a cBioPortal-registered gene panel; treated as a corpus-observed method.

## Used by

- Used for targeted NGS (aligned to hg38 with BWA; SNVs/indels called with MuTect and annotated with ANNOVAR; CNVs with CONTRA) of the 131-patient SYSUCC discovery cohort of solitary fibrous tumors [PMID:37980418](../papers/37980418.md).

## Notes

- Applied only to the SYSUCC discovery cohort (n=131) in the citing paper; validation cohorts used other confirmatory assays (Sanger sequencing, IHC).
