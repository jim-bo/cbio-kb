# GNAI2

## Overview

GNAI2 encodes an inhibitory Gαi2 heterotrimeric G-protein subunit implicated in G-protein-coupled receptor signaling. It is recurrently mutated in germinal-center-derived B-cell lymphomas.

## Alterations observed in the corpus

- Defining gene of the DGG-BL molecular subgroup of Burkitt lymphoma; more often mutated in EBV-negative BL than EBV-positive BL [PMID:36201743](../papers/36201743.md).
